Missing at the Top: Women in Indian Management

Why in news: An ILO report released in early November 2026 found that women hold under 12% of manager jobs in India, ranking it 32nd of 34 Asia-Pacific economies.

More women now study in college than men, but few reach senior roles. The 'leaky pipeline' starts at marriage and childbirth, when many women leave work. Women also earn about 28% less per hour than men. Firms with more women leaders often perform better, so this is an economic loss, not only a social one.

  • ILO: 'Women in Business and Management: Scaling Success'
  • Women in management in India: under 12%
  • India: 32nd of 34 Asia-Pacific economies
  • Gender pay gap: about 28% per hour

Way forward: workplace creches and paternity leave, flexible and hybrid work, transparent promotion criteria, mentoring networks, and gender-pay audits in large firms.

Rajasthan angle: Rajeevika SHGs have built a large base of women entrepreneurs; the next step is helping them scale into firms and FPOs led by women, with credit, branding and market access.

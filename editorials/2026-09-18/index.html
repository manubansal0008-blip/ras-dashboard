Rank 131: What the Gender Gap Index Tells India

Why in news: The WEF Global Gender Gap Report 2026 ranked India 131st of 145 countries with a score of 0.645; Iceland topped for the 17th year.

India does well on educational attainment, where gaps are nearly closed, but scores low on economic participation and political empowerment. Women's labour force participation, though rising in PLFS data, is still far below men's, and women hold a small share of senior and legislative posts.

  • India: 131/145, score 0.645
  • Global gap closed: 69.2%; parity in ~120 years
  • Four pillars: economy, education, health, politics
  • Women's Reservation Act, 2023: 33% in Lok Sabha & Assemblies (after delimitation)

Way forward: public creches and care services, safe transport, equal-pay enforcement, skilling in new sectors, and early implementation of the 33% reservation.

Rajasthan angle: Rajeevika SHGs, 'Lakhpati Didi' efforts and schemes like Kalibai Bhil scooty and Udaan sanitary pads support women; the State must also tackle child marriage and low female literacy in desert districts.

'One Nation, One Election' vs the federal compact

At a New Delhi conclave, a group of eminent citizens — retired bureaucrats, judges, lawyers and civil-society leaders — rejected the 'One Nation, One Election' (ONOE) proposal, warning that synchronising Lok Sabha and Assembly polls would fracture India's federal compact and curtail the ability of State Assemblies to run their own affairs.

Why the tank still matters in a drone-dominated battlefield

As drones and precision-guided weapons reshape war, critics ask whether the battle tank is obsolete. Military experts argue the opposite: because holding ground still needs a physical presence, tanks remain indispensable — and India is inducting a new indigenous light tank, the Zorawar, built for the high Himalayas.

Science & Technology / EconomyRAS Mains GS-III · Science & technology; space economy

India's space sector goes private, with ISRO's help

Skyroot Aerospace has announced that its first orbital rocket, Vikram-1, will lift off in a July 12–August 4 window — India's first private orbital launch. Its co-founder Pawan Kumar Chandana calls the ISRO–private relationship 'similar to NASA and SpaceX in the US', signalling a decisive private turn in a sector opened up by recent reforms.

International Relations / EconomyRAS Mains GS-II · India & the world; economic diplomacy

India–Israel investment treaty and the new BIT model

The India–Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement (BIA), signed on 8 September last year, came into force on Saturday. It promises a 'secure and predictable' climate for two-way investment and, unusually, covers portfolio investments — a deviation from India's cautious 2015 model treaty.

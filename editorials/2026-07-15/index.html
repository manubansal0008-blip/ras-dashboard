The Anaemia Fight and a Missing Injection

For about five months, Rajasthan's government hospitals have not stocked Ferric Carboxymaltose (FCM), the iron injection used to treat severe anaemia in pregnant women. With the drug costing Rs 2,500-6,000 privately, the shortage falls hardest on the poor — and a maternal death has already been reported from Bikaner.

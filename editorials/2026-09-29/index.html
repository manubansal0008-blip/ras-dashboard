Drug-Free India by 2029: From Seizures to Society

Why in news: At the 3rd ANTF Conference (22–23 September 2026), the Union Home Minister set the target of a drug-free India by 31 December 2029 under a Vision Document on Narcotics Control (2026–2029).

Drugs fund terror and organised crime and destroy young lives. Heroin and synthetic drugs enter through the western border, sometimes dropped by drones, and through sea routes. Anti-Narcotics Task Forces in every State now coordinate with the Narcotics Control Bureau.

  • 3rd ANTF Conference: 22–23 Sept 2026, New Delhi
  • Target: drug-free India by 31 Dec 2029
  • Theme: 'United Resolve, Shared Responsibility'
  • Law: NDPS Act, 1985; MANAS helpline 1933

Way forward: balance supply control with demand reduction — school and college awareness, registered de-addiction centres under the Mental Healthcare Act, community policing, and financial investigation of drug networks.

Rajasthan angle: border districts like Sri Ganganagar and Barmer face drone drops of heroin, and doda post (poppy husk) addiction is a long-standing problem; Rajasthan has begun registering de-addiction centres under the State Mental Health Authority.

Economy / Energy SecurityRAS Mains · Energy security; crude-oil imports & diversification; strategic autonomy; sanctions

The oil conundrum: India's crude imports need a strategy, not just a discount

Official data show India's oil receipts from Russia have climbed back above 40% of its imports — the highest in two years — even as the discount that first made Russian crude attractive has narrowed and the premium has risen. With West Asia in turmoil and secondary-sanction risks looming, India's growing concentration on a single supplier looks less like a considered strategy and more like drift. For a country that imports over 85% of its crude, how it buys oil is as important as how much.

Justice on vacation: five crore Indians wait while the courts take a break

As the Supreme Court and major High Courts resume after a six-week summer break — running only a few vacation Benches while more than five crore cases wait — the annual debate over court vacations has returned. Shops, hospitals and police stations never shut for weeks; the most powerful court in the world's most populous democracy does. With a third of High Court seats vacant and pendency that would take generations to clear, the question is not whether judges deserve rest, but whether justice can afford to close.

Self-inflicted solitude: the making of Israel's retreat into isolation

Israel was founded so that a persecuted people could escape isolation and live like any other nation. Yet through a relentless exercise of military power — in Gaza, Lebanon, Syria and Iran, and through relentless settlement expansion in the West Bank — Israel is steadily recreating the very isolation it once sought to escape. Its isolation, analysts argue, is no longer imposed by hostile neighbours; it is increasingly self-inflicted, and even its strongest ally is signalling strain.

BRICS at New Delhi: Bridge-Builder in a Divided World

Why in news: India hosted the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026 — its fourth chairship — and leaders adopted the New Delhi Declaration.

BRICS has grown from five members to an expanded group including Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, the UAE and Indonesia. Its theme under India — 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability' — spells the acronym itself. India used the chair to push practical cooperation (health, digital public goods, climate finance) rather than an anti-West agenda.

  • Dates: 12–13 September 2026, Bharat Mandapam
  • India's chairships: 2012, 2016, 2021, 2026
  • Outcome: New Delhi Declaration, over 50 outcomes
  • Health: mental-health hubs coordinated by NIMHANS, Bengaluru

Way forward: keep BRICS issue-based and non-aligned, strengthen the New Development Bank, push local-currency trade without a common currency, and link outcomes with India's G20 legacy on digital public infrastructure.

Rajasthan angle: BRICS cooperation on renewable energy and drylands can bring investment and know-how to Rajasthan's solar parks and arid-zone farming.

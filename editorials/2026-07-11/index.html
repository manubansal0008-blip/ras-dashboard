Polity / Elections & RightsRAS Mains · Electoral rolls & the Election Commission; right to vote (Article 326); citizenship vs entitlements

You vote, therefore you are: the hidden consequences of deletion from the electoral roll

A distinguished former newspaper editor recently found his passport renewal stalled — because his name had been deleted from the electoral roll during a Special Intensive Revision (SIR), and a clerk treated 'not on the voters' list' as a reason to doubt his very existence. The episode crystallises a growing worry: that deletion from the electoral roll, meant only to decide who may vote, is quietly beginning to influence who may receive welfare, banking and travel documents. The Election Commission has assured the Supreme Court that SIR carries no consequence beyond the right to vote — but on the ground, that wall is starting to erode.

Governance / Digital Rights & Child SafetyRAS Mains · Digital governance; child rights & online safety; data protection (DPDP Act); regulation vs freedom

Screens and children: should India follow Australia and lock minors out of social media?

At the Australia-India summit, the Prime Minister praised Australia's world-first law barring under-16s from social media and said India was 'learning a lot'. At home, the IT Ministry is in talks on age-based safeguards, some states have floated their own limits, and the Allahabad High Court is hearing a petition to restrict minors' access to online gaming platforms. The question is no longer whether to protect children online, but how — and whether a blunt legal ban is the right instrument for a country of India's scale and digital diversity.

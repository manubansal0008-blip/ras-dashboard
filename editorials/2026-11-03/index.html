EKA-1 and the Case for Sovereign AI

Why in news: On 3 November 2026 the IndiaAI Mission released indigenous AI models, including EKA-1 (26.3 billion parameters, 22 Scheduled languages plus English) and compact offline models Dhīr 2B and 5B.

Most large AI models are trained abroad on English data and run on foreign clouds. That raises concerns over data security, cultural bias and cost. Indian models trained on Indian languages can serve farmers, courts and citizens in their own language, and small offline models can work where internet is weak.

  • Released 3 Nov 2026 under the IndiaAI Mission
  • EKA-1 (Soket Labs): 26.3 bn parameters, 22 languages + English
  • Dhīr 2B / 5B (ZenteiQ): compact, run offline
  • IndiaAI Mission approved 2024 with an outlay of about ₹10,372 crore

Way forward: shared public GPU capacity, open language datasets (Bhashini), clear rules on AI safety and data protection under the DPDP Act, and government use cases that create early demand.

Rajasthan angle: the State's AI-ML Policy 2026 and its 'AI-based competency assessment' in schools can use Indian-language models to reach students and farmers in Rajasthani-speaking regions.

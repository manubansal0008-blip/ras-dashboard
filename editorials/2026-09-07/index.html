AISHE's Good News and the Quality Question

Why in news: The Education Ministry released AISHE 2022-23 and 2023-24: enrolment in higher education reached 4.50 crore and universities rose to 1,289.

Enrolment grew 31.5% since 2014-15 and women now outnumber men (GPI 1.08). But NEP 2020's target of 50% Gross Enrolment Ratio by 2035 needs faster growth, and expansion has been led by private and low-quality colleges in many States.

  • Enrolment: 3.42 crore (2014-15) → 4.50 crore (2023-24)
  • Universities: 760 → 1,289
  • Gender Parity Index: 1.08 (above 1 for 7 years)
  • NEP 2020 target: GER 50% by 2035

Way forward: fill faculty posts, fund State universities through PM-USHA, link degrees to apprenticeships, and use accreditation to close weak colleges.

Rajasthan angle: Rajasthan University entered the QS Asia 2026 ranking for the first time; the State must now improve its own GER and faculty strength, especially in tribal and desert districts.

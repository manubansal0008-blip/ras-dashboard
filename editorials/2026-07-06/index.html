A second home for the Asiatic lion

India's Asiatic lion is a celebrated conservation success — the population has climbed to about 891 — yet the entire species survives in a single landscape, the Gir region of Gujarat. A decade after the Supreme Court ordered a second population, translocation to Kuno remains stalled, leaving the lion one catastrophe away from disaster.

Environment / Blue EconomyRAS Mains GS-III · Marine resources; sustainability; livelihoods

The real crisis in India's marine fisheries

The Government of India's ocean-fisheries prognosis (11 February 2026) claims India has largely 'avoided overfishing' and that most stocks are healthy. But the FAO offers a far more cautious view, and coastal communities report decades of decline. The real crisis, critics argue, is the degradation of inshore grounds and the unchecked spread of mechanised trawling — not aggregate catch.

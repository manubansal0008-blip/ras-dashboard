India–New Zealand 'Roadmap to 2030': Issue-Based Partnership over Alliance

The first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to New Zealand in four decades has ended with the relationship being raised to a Strategic Partnership and the adoption of a 'Roadmap to 2030'. Notably, the Roadmap is non-binding: it carries no legal obligation and no financial commitment, and works purely as a framework for implementation.

Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill: One Regulator, Many Doubts

The Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025 — which would replace the UGC, AICTE and NCTE with a single apex regulator — is before a Joint Parliamentary Committee. Several States, universities and premier institutions including the IITs, IIMs and IISERs have objected, warning of over-centralisation and an erosion of institutional autonomy.

Biogas and India's Energy Security Architecture

Renewed conflict in West Asia has put India's fuel lifeline at risk: roughly 90% of the country's LPG imports pass through the Strait of Hormuz, and about 85% of its crude oil demand is met by imports. That vulnerability has pushed compressed biogas (CBG) from a rural-sanitation idea to the centre of the energy-security conversation.

Draft Regulations for Use of AI in Courts, 2026: Efficiency Without Abdication

The Supreme Court's Artificial Intelligence Committee has drafted the 'Regulations for Use of Artificial Intelligence in Courts, 2026' — the first attempt to lay down a single ethical and structural framework for how AI may be used across India's judiciary. Its organising idea is 'Human Adjudicative Primacy': the machine may assist, but it may never decide.

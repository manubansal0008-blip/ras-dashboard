RAS Current Affairs — 22 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 22 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

International Relations

1. Consider the following statements regarding the ASEAN-related summits held in Manila:
(a) India announced free tourist visas for all ASEAN citizens.
(b) India and ASEAN concluded the revision of the ASEAN-India Trade in Goods Agreement.
(c) ASEAN accounts for about 11% of India's global trade.
(d) India walked out of the RCEP negotiations in 2019.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: there was no progress on the long-pending AITIGA revision.

Environment

2. With reference to the climate conferences, which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. COP31 is being held at Antalya, Türkiye.
  2. At COP29 in Baku, developed nations agreed to lead in mobilising at least $300 billion a year by 2035.
  3. The latest COP31 draft cover text includes a firm date for phasing out fossil fuels.
  4. COP30 was held at Belém, Brazil.

Answer: C. The latest COP31 draft cover text includes a firm date for phasing out fossil fuels. — C is wrong: the latest draft removed references to fossil-fuel phase-out and net-zero dates.

International — Disarmament

3. Consider the following statements about the recent UN talks on 'killer robots':
(a) The talks were held under the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) in Geneva.
(b) Around 100 countries backed legally binding rules.
(c) The US, Russia and Israel opposed quick legally binding rules.
(d) States adopted a legally binding treaty banning such weapons.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: states only pledged to develop rules within two years; whether they will be binding is left open.

Current Affairs — Projects

4. Match List-I with List-II and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Siang Upper Multipurpose Project
(B) Nitish Setu
(C) Bharatpur–Beawar Expressway
(D) Edamalakkudy
List-II
(1) 11,200 MW, Arunachal Pradesh
(2) Ganga bridge, Patna–Vaishali
(3) 277 km through five Rajasthan districts
(4) Kerala's first tribal panchayat
Code:

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
  2. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  3. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

Answer: D. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4 — Siang Upper — 11,200 MW, Arunachal; Nitish Setu — 9.76-km Ganga bridge (Kacchi Dargah–Bidupur); Bharatpur–Beawar — 277 km, ₹8,656 crore; Edamalakkudy (Idukki) — Kerala's first tribal panchayat, now shut due to fund crunch.

Geography — Cyclones

5. Which one of the following pairs (cyclone — landfall) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Arnab — Yanam
  2. Akvan — Paradip
  3. Murasu — Ongole
  4. Helen (2013) — Andhra coast

Answer: B. Akvan — Paradip — Cyclone Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur (Odisha) on 20 November, not Paradip.

Health

6. Consider the following statements about the national HPV vaccination campaign:
(a) It was launched in February 2026 for 14-year-old girls.
(b) A single-dose vaccine is given free of cost.
(c) Uttar Pradesh leads in the number of girls vaccinated.
(d) Rajasthan has achieved 100% coverage of its target population.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Rajasthan's coverage is 85.23%. The six states with 100% are Gujarat, UP, Andhra Pradesh, Mizoram, MP and Bihar.

Economy

7. According to the EdelGive Hurun India Philanthropy List 2026, which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. The Shiv Nadar family was the top donor with ₹2,593 crore.
  2. Education received the largest share of donations.
  3. 219 Indians donated ₹11,554 crore in FY 2025-26.
  4. The Mukesh Ambani family was the top donor.

Answer: D. The Mukesh Ambani family was the top donor. — D is wrong: the Mukesh Ambani family was fourth (₹620 crore); the Shiv Nadar family gave 22.4% of all donations.

Polity & International

8. Consider the following statements:
(a) Ladakh became a Union Territory on 31 October 2019.
(b) Ladakh's first panchayat poll after UT status recorded about 70% turnout.
(c) The headquarters of the International Maritime Organization is in London.
(d) The IMO vote on the net-zero framework for shipping is expected on 4 December.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only b and d
  4. Only a and c

Answer: A. a, b, c and d — All statements are correct. Ladakh's panchayat turnout was 70.03% and municipal turnout 52.4%.

Rajasthan — Current Affairs

9. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Rashtriya Loktantrik Party was founded by Hanuman Beniwal.
(b) RLP won 51 zila parishad seats and 497 panchayat samiti seats in the 2026 polls.
(c) The BRICS Women Leadership Summit is being held at Banasthali in Tonk district.
(d) The BRICS Women Leadership Summit was inaugurated by the Vice-President.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the summit was inaugurated by Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla. About 120 women leaders are attending; it ends on 23 November.

Environment — Biodiversity

10. Which one of the following statements is NOT correct?

  1. The Siang Upper Multipurpose Project is located in Assam.
  2. The Andaman cobra is endemic to the Andaman Islands.
  3. The Andaman cobra was first described by Frank Wall in 1913.
  4. The Andaman cobra can spit venom as a defence.

Answer: A. The Siang Upper Multipurpose Project is located in Assam. — A is wrong: the 11,200-MW Siang Upper project is in Arunachal Pradesh; the Siang becomes the Brahmaputra in Assam.

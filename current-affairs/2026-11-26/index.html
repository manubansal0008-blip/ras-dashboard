RAS Current Affairs — 26 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 26 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Polity

1. Consider the following statements:
(I) The Constitution of India was adopted on 26 November 1949.
(II) Constitution Day has been observed on 26 November since 2015.
(III) Under Article 394, all provisions of the Constitution came into force on 26 November 1949.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — only some provisions (citizenship, elections, provisional Parliament etc.) came into force on 26 November 1949; the rest on 26 January 1950.

Economy

2. With reference to the Cabinet decisions of 25 November 2026, consider the following statements:
(I) A greenfield airport has been approved at Doloo near Silchar in Assam.
(II) A second container terminal at Kamarajar Port will be built fully with government funds.
(III) A scheme for the Construction and Infrastructure Equipment manufacturing ecosystem has been approved.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: C. I and III only — II is false — the Kamarajar terminal will be built under the PPP mode.

International Relations

3. List-I (Grouping / Exercise) | List-II (Associated countries)
A. Colombo Security Conclave | 1. India and Nepal
B. Surya Kiran | 2. India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Bangladesh, Seychelles
C. Thomas Cup 2028 host | 3. India
Select the correct match:

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-2, B-3, C-1
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A. A-2, B-1, C-3 — CSC has six members; Surya Kiran is the India–Nepal army exercise; India will host the 2028 Thomas & Uber Cup in New Delhi.

Economy

4. The rupee's recent recovery to about 92 per US dollar has been attributed mainly to:
(I) RBI curbs on speculative bets against the rupee
(II) Record FPI inflows into Indian equities
(III) A shift by India to a fixed exchange-rate regime
Select the correct answer:

  1. I only
  2. II and III only
  3. I, II and III
  4. I and II only

Answer: D. I and II only — III is wrong — India continues with a managed float; the recovery came from RBI measures and FPI inflows.

Science & Health

5. Consider the following statements about snakebite antivenom in India:
(I) The standard polyvalent antivenom targets the 'Big Four' snakes.
(II) Russell's viper is one of the 'Big Four'.
(III) The standard antivenom is fully effective against all pit vipers of the North-East.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — the standard antivenom works poorly on North-East pit vipers, which is why a region-specific antivenom has been developed.

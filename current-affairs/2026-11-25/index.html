RAS Current Affairs — 25 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 25 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Polity

1. Consider the following statements about the regulation of road transport in India:
(I) The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 is a central law.
(II) 'Mechanically propelled vehicles' is a subject in the Concurrent List.
(III) Bike taxis are expressly defined in the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — the term 'bike taxi' is not defined in the Act; the courts held that motorcycles fall within 'transport vehicles'.

Economy

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

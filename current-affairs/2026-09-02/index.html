Economy — National Accounts

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Real GDP grew 7.8% in April–June 2026 according to the NSO.
(b) The revised National Accounts series uses 2022-23 as its base year.
(c) The base year of the previous National Accounts series was 2004-05.
(d) The National Statistics Office works under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: the previous base year was 2011-12.

Geography — Rivers

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Sone is a right-bank tributary of the Ganga.
(b) The Sone rises in the Amarkantak region.
(c) The Sone joins the Ganga near Prayagraj.
(d) The Bansagar Dam is built on the Sone river.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: the Sone joins the Ganga near Patna. Bihar and Jharkhand settled their Sone water dispute in 2026.

Health — Child Health

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Exclusive breastfeeding for the first six months reduces infant illness.
Reason (R): Breast milk provides antibodies that protect infants from infections.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. Rajasthan launched the Samagra Shishu Bal Swasthya Karyakram for child health.

Geography — River Origins

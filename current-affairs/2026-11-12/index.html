RAS Current Affairs — 12 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 12 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Agriculture & Energy

1. With reference to the PM-KUSUM scheme, consider the following statements:
(a) Component A of PM-KUSUM supports decentralised grid-connected solar power plants of up to 2 MW.
(b) Component B of PM-KUSUM is for solarisation of existing grid-connected agricultural pumps.
(c) Component C of PM-KUSUM includes feeder-level solarisation.
(d) Rajasthan ranks first in the country in the implementation of PM-KUSUM.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Component B is for standalone solar pumps; solarising grid-connected pumps falls under Component C. Rajasthan has installed over 1,779 MW (A) and 3,758 MW (C).

Rajasthan — Wildlife

2. With reference to the Great Indian Bustard (GIB), consider the following statements:
(a) The Great Indian Bustard is the State Bird of Rajasthan.
(b) The Great Indian Bustard is listed as Critically Endangered on the IUCN Red List.
(c) Captive-bred Great Indian Bustards were released into the wild at Sam in the Desert National Park, Jaisalmer.
(d) The Great Indian Bustard is listed in Schedule II of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the GIB is in Schedule I. Its wild population is about 150. Breeding centres are at Ramdevra and Sam, run with the Wildlife Institute of India.

Rajasthan — Protected Areas

3. Match List-I (Protected area) with List-II (District) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Desert National Park
(B) Sorsan grasslands
(C) Tal Chhapar Sanctuary
(D) Keoladeo National Park
List-II
(1) Baran
(2) Jaisalmer and Barmer
(3) Bharatpur
(4) Churu
Code:

  1. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  2. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  3. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: B. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3 — Desert NP: Jaisalmer and Barmer, the main GIB habitat. Sorsan: Baran, also a bustard habitat. Tal Chhapar: Churu, known for blackbuck. Keoladeo: Bharatpur.

Economy — Schemes

4. With reference to the Stand-Up India scheme, extended to 31 March 2031, consider the following statements:
(a) The Stand-Up India scheme was launched on 5 April 2016.
(b) Stand-Up India provides bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore for greenfield enterprises.
(c) Under Stand-Up India, each bank branch is expected to give at least one loan to an SC/ST borrower and one to a woman borrower.
(d) The Stand-Up India scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the scheme is run by the Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance) through scheduled commercial banks. The new credit guarantee corpus is ₹4,111 crore.

Economy — Employment

5. Match List-I (PLFS indicator) with List-II (Meaning) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR)
(B) Worker Population Ratio (WPR)
(C) Unemployment Rate (UR)
(D) Current Weekly Status (CWS)
List-II
(1) Percentage of employed persons in the population
(2) Percentage of persons in the labour force in the population
(3) Activity status based on a reference period of the last 7 days
(4) Percentage of unemployed persons among the labour force
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  2. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  3. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  4. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Answer: D. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3 — PLFS July–September 2026 (CWS): UR 5.1%, LFPR 55.5%, WPR 52.7%. Rural UR was 4.3% and urban 6.8%.

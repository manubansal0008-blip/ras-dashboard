RAS Current Affairs — 18 August 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 18 August 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Road Safety

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Jaipur Range Police launched 'Operation Safer Roads'.
(b) 93 black spots were identified in the Jaipur range.
(c) The black spots are marked on Google Maps.
(d) A black spot is a road stretch with no street lighting.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: a black spot is a stretch with repeated serious accidents.

Sports — Awards

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Arjuna Award is given by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.
(b) The Dronacharya Award is given to sports journalists.
(c) The Dronacharya Award is given to coaches.
(d) The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna is India's highest sports honour.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and c
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: it is for coaches.

Rajasthan — Roads

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Fixing black spots can reduce road deaths.
Reason (R): Black spots are stretches where accidents cluster.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A.

RAS Current Affairs — 20 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 20 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

International

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The U.S.–China–Russia trilateral summit was held in Shenzhen.
(b) The three countries agreed to launch a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks.
(c) India is a member of the International Criminal Court.
(d) The ICC began functioning in 2002 under the Rome Statute.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: India is not a member of the ICC.

International Law

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The ICJ found Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya.
(b) The case against Myanmar was filed by Bangladesh.
(c) The ICJ has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. a, b and c
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only b and c

Answer: A. Only a and c — Statement b is wrong: the case was filed by The Gambia in 2019.

Economy — Social Security

3. Consider the following statements:
(a) EPFO has approved a systematic withdrawal plan for retirees.
(b) The minimum balance requirement has been cut from 25% to 10%.
(c) The waiting period for final settlement has been raised to 24 months.
(d) EPFO works under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: the waiting period was cut from 12 months to two months.

Health — TB

5. Consider the following statements:
(a) India accounts for nearly a quarter of the global TB burden.
(b) India's TB death rate fell from 28 to 18 per lakh between 2015 and 2025.
(c) India's TB elimination target was 2030, the same as the SDG target.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b and c
  2. a, b and c
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only a and c

Answer: C. Only a and b — Statement c is wrong: India's target was 2025, five years ahead of the SDG 2030 target.

Rajasthan — Energy

6. Consider the following statements:
(a) RAPP Unit-8 at Rawatbhata has a capacity of 700 MW.
(b) It is a boiling water reactor.
(c) AERB granted permission for its first approach to criticality.
(d) Rawatbhata is located on the Chambal river.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, c and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a and c

Answer: B. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: it is a pressurised heavy water reactor (PHWR).

Rajasthan — Places

7. Which one of the following pairs (place — district) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Raniji ki Baori — Kota
  2. Rawatbhata — Chittorgarh
  3. Pushkar — Ajmer
  4. Banasthali Vidyapith — Tonk

Answer: A. Raniji ki Baori — Kota — Raniji ki Baori is in Bundi, not Kota.

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

8. Consider the following statements:
(a) Results of 37 zila parishads were due on 20 November.

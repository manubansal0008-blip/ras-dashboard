RAS Current Affairs — 23 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 23 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

International Relations

1. Consider the following statements regarding India–Nepal relations:
(a) A weekly train between Ayodhya and Janakpurdham, covering 604 km, has been proposed.
(b) Janakpurdham, in Nepal, is regarded as the birthplace of Sita.
(c) India considers Lipulekh, Limpiyadhura and Kalapani a part of Sikkim.
(d) Balendra Shah became Prime Minister of Nepal in March 2026.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: India treats Lipulekh, Limpiyadhura and Kalapani as part of Uttarakhand.

Environment

2. With reference to COP31, which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. It was held at Antalya, Türkiye.
  2. It failed to reach consensus on the main political agreement for the first time in over 15 years.
  3. The EU and over 80 countries refused a draft that did not mention fossil-fuel phase-out.
  4. It was presided over by Australia's climate minister.

Answer: D. It was presided over by Australia's climate minister. — D is wrong: COP31 was presided over by Türkiye's Murat Kurum.

Economy — Food Security

3. Consider the following statements about the evaluation study of PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY):
(a) It found an inclusion error of about 13.2%.
(b) West Bengal had the highest inclusion error.
(c) Rajasthan had one of the lowest inclusion errors.
(d) PMGKAY provides free foodgrains under the National Food Security Act, 2013.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only b and d
  4. Only a and c

Answer: A. a, b, c and d — All statements are correct. West Bengal 39.5%; Rajasthan 1.8%, MP 0.5% (lowest). The study covered 51,303 households.

Economy — Social Security

5. With reference to EPFO 3.0, which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. It is being rolled out under the Code on Wages, 2019.
  2. Aggregator contributions for gig workers are capped at 5% of the amount payable to them.
  3. EPFO has nearly 8 crore active members.
  4. A facility to withdraw PF through UPI on the BHIM app is planned.

Answer: A. It is being rolled out under the Code on Wages, 2019. — A is wrong: gig-worker PF coverage is in line with the Code on Social Security, 2020, not the Code on Wages.

Rajasthan — Polity

6. Consider the following statements about Rajasthan's electoral roll:
(a) The gender ratio in the SSR-2027 draft roll is 913.
(b) The final SIR roll of 21 February 2026 had a gender ratio of 911.
(c) Rajasthan's electoral gender ratio is better than that of the southern states.
(d) The roll has over 5.26 crore voters.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. Only b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: Rajasthan is better than UP and Bihar but behind southern and north-eastern states.

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

7. Consider the following statements regarding panchayat samiti results in Rajasthan (2026 vs 2020-21):
(a) Congress won 229 panchayat samitis on its own.
(b) BJP's own panchayat samitis fell from 139 to 51.
(c) In 2020-21, the gap between Congress and BJP was 41 samitis.
(d) BJP increased its number of panchayat samitis compared to 2020-21.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: BJP's own samitis fell by 88; with supported candidates it reached only 79 against Congress's 301.

Geography & Culture

8. Which one of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Bundi Festival — 27–29 November
  2. Bagheera AT1 trial — Pokhran
  3. Mount Abu — Sirohi district
  4. Janakpurdham — Uttar Pradesh

Answer: D. Janakpurdham — Uttar Pradesh — Janakpurdham is in Nepal; the proposed train links it with Ayodhya (UP).

Economy — Trade

9. Consider the following statements about the India–EU free trade agreement:
(a) Negotiations were concluded on 27 January 2026.
(b) It is likely to be signed on 16 December.
(c) The European Union has 28 member states.
(d) India–EU FTA talks were first launched in 2007.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: the EU has 27 members since the UK left in 2020.

Sports

  1. The 2026 World Chess Championship is being held in London.

  4. India declared their first innings at 720/9 in Wellington.

Answer: A. The 2026 World Chess Championship is being held in London. — A is wrong: the Gukesh–Sindarov World Championship is in Geneva (24 November–12 December).

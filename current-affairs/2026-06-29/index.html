Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated Current Affairs — 29 June 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Economy

1. The India–United Kingdom free trade agreement, in the news, is formally known by which name? Difficulty: Easy

  1. Economic Cooperation and Partnership Agreement (ECPA)
  2. Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA)
  3. Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP)
  4. Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA)

Answer: B. Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) — The India–UK FTA is formally the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA), coming into force on 15 July 2026.

Environment

4. A 2026 study on elephant mortality in Kerala (2019-20 to 2024-25) identified which factor as the leading unnatural cause of elephant deaths in the State? Difficulty: Easy

  1. Poaching for ivory
  2. Train collisions
  3. Electrocution
  4. Poisoning of waterholes

Answer: C. Electrocution — The study led by then Chief Wildlife Warden Pramod G. Krishnan identified electrocution as the leading unnatural cause; 744 wild elephants died between April 2019 and March 2025.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Rajasthan

6. In June 2026, Rajasthan signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on sharing of Yamuna river water with which state, in the presence of the Union Home Minister? Difficulty: Easy

  1. Punjab
  2. Haryana
  3. Madhya Pradesh
  4. Gujarat

Answer: B. Haryana — The MoU is between Rajasthan and Haryana. Under it Rajasthan receives its share of water from the Hathnikund Barrage per the 1994 agreement, with a 265-km underground pipeline planned to carry water to the Shekhawati region (Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Churu).

Source: ANI News, June 2026

Rajasthan

7. The Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project (ERCP) has been renamed as which of the following? Difficulty: Easy

  1. Vande Ganga Link Project
  2. Amrit Sarovar Link Project
  3. Ram Jal Setu Link Project
  4. Chambal Aqueduct Project

Answer: C. Ram Jal Setu Link Project — The ERCP has been renamed the Ram Jal Setu Link Project. It aims to provide drinking water to nearly 30 million people across 17 districts of eastern Rajasthan and irrigate about 4 lakh hectares; a 2,280-metre aqueduct over the Chambal between Kota and Bundi is under construction.

Source: ANI News, June 2026

Rajasthan

8. With reference to measures announced by the Rajasthan CM on International MSME Day (27 June 2026), consider the following statements: I. Rajasthan was described as the fourth-largest MSME base in the country, with more than 33 lakh enterprises. II. Financial support for 25 new startups was announced through the Rajasthan Venture Capital Fund. III. The capital subsidy period for MSMEs under RIPS was reduced from 10 years to seven years. How many of the above statements are correct? Difficulty: Medium

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. None
  4. All three

Answer: D. All three — All three are correct. On International MSME Day the CM said Rajasthan is the fourth-largest MSME base with over 33 lakh enterprises, announced support for 25 startups via the Rajasthan Venture Capital Fund, and reduced the RIPS capital subsidy period from 10 to seven years; the Rajasthan Industrial Development Policy and RAMP Portal were also launched.

Source: IANS, June 2026

RAS Current Affairs — 24 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 24 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Polity

1. Consider the following statements about Constitution Day:
(I) It marks the adoption of the Constitution of India on 26 November 1949.
(II) It has been observed as Constitution Day since 1950.
(III) MY Bharat is holding a Yuva Samvad on this day in over 600 districts.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. I and III only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: B. I and III only — II is false — 26 November has been observed as Constitution Day since 2015.

Social Sector

2. With reference to the Nayi Chetna campaign, consider the following statements:
(I) It is run under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–National Rural Livelihoods Mission.
(II) It is a campaign against gender-based violence led by women's self-help groups.
(III) It is launched every year on International Women's Day, 8 March.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — it is launched on 25 November, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women.

Geography

3. Consider the following pairs:
(I) Strait of Hormuz — Iran and Oman
(II) Bhadla Solar Park — Jaisalmer district
(III) COP31 — Antalya, Türkiye
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: C. I and III only — II is wrong — Bhadla Solar Park is in Phalodi district (earlier part of Jodhpur district).

Infrastructure

4. With reference to Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF) tolling, consider the following statements:
(I) It allows vehicles to pass toll points without stopping, using number-plate recognition cameras and FASTag.
(II) The Daulatpura toll plaza on the Delhi–Jaipur section of NH-48 uses this system.
(III) India's first MLFF plaza was set up on the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — India's first MLFF plaza was Chorayasi on NH-48 in Gujarat (May 2026).

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — Gukesh won the title in Singapore in 2024.

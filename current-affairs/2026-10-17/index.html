RAS Current Affairs — 17 October 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 17 October 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Housing

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Rajasthan Housing Board was constituted in 1970.
(b) The Rajasthan Housing Board is headquartered in Jaipur.
(c) The Rajasthan Housing Board was constituted in 1990.
(d) The Rajasthan Housing Board allots flats through a lottery system.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only a, b and d

Answer: D. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: RHB was constituted in 1970. RHB opened registration for new housing schemes.

Geography — Islands

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Great Nicobar Project includes a transshipment port at Galathea Bay.
(b) Great Nicobar is the northernmost island of the Andaman and Nicobar group.
(c) Indira Point on Great Nicobar Island is the southernmost point of India.
(d) The Great Nicobar Biosphere Reserve is part of UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere network.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, c and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Great Nicobar is the southernmost island of the group.

Society — PVTGs

List-I
(A) Sahariya
(B) Jarawa
(C) Toda
(D) Baiga
List-II
(1) Tamil Nadu
(2) Madhya Pradesh
(3) Andaman and Nicobar Islands
(4) Rajasthan
Code:

  1. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  4. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2

Answer: B. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2 — The Sahariya are the only PVTG of Rajasthan. Great Nicobar is home to the Shompen, another PVTG.

Rajasthan — Tribes

5. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The Sahariya tribe is concentrated mainly in Baran district.
Reason (R): The Sahariya are classified as a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A — Both are true, but PVTG status does not explain where the tribe lives. The Sahariya live mainly in the Kishanganj and Shahabad tehsils of Baran.

RAS Current Affairs — 28 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 28 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Polity

1. Which Constitutional Amendment extended the freeze on inter-State allocation of Lok Sabha seats till the first census after 2026?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 84th Amendment
  3. 87th Amendment
  4. 106th Amendment

Answer: B. 84th Amendment — The 84th Amendment (2001) extended the freeze; the 42nd (1976) had first frozen it till 2001; the 87th (2003) allowed use of the 2001 Census for boundaries.

Infrastructure

2. Tunnel-14, which recently achieved its main breakthrough, is part of which rail project?

  1. Udhampur–Srinagar–Baramulla Rail Link
  2. Bilaspur–Manali–Leh line
  3. Rishikesh–Karnaprayag line
  4. Sivok–Rangpo line

Answer: C. Rishikesh–Karnaprayag line — The 6.59-km Tunnel-14 in Rudraprayag district is on the Rishikesh–Karnaprayag line in Uttarakhand.

Polity — Reservation

3. A reserved-category candidate who scores above the cut-off for the unreserved category on merit should, as per settled law:

  1. Be counted against the open (unreserved) category
  2. Be counted only against the reserved quota
  3. Be excluded from both lists
  4. Be counted in both lists

Answer: A. Be counted against the open (unreserved) category — Open category is open to all on merit (Indra Sawhney, 1992; Saurav Yadav, 2020). The Allahabad HC set aside the UPPSC PCS-2025 prelims result for breaking this rule.

International Relations

4. List-I (Item) | List-II (Associated with)
A. Samriddha Nepal, Samunnat Bharat | 1. ITU
B. Plenipotentiary Conference 2030 | 2. India–Nepal vision document
C. Vindhyagiri | 3. BIMSTEC
D. Under-23 athletics meet at Bhubaneswar | 4. Project 17A
Select the correct match:

  1. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
  3. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  4. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Answer: D. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3 — Samriddha Nepal, Samunnat Bharat is the basis of the India–Nepal vision document; PP-30 is the ITU conference India will host; Vindhyagiri is the last Project 17A frigate; India topped the BIMSTEC U-23 athletics meet.

Rajasthan

5. In the Panchayat Samiti Pradhan elections declared on 27 November 2026 in Rajasthan, how many Panchayat Samitis went to polls?

  1. 352
  2. 419
  3. 37
  4. 295

Answer: B. 419 — 419 Panchayat Samitis in 37 districts; Congress won 255 and BJP 105 Pradhan posts.

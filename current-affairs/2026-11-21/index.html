RAS Current Affairs — 21 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 21 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

International

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum was held in New Delhi.
(b) India had proposed this forum at the 2025 SCO Summit in Tianjin.
(c) India and Pakistan became full members of the SCO in 2017.
(d) The Indus Waters Treaty was signed in 1965 after the India–Pakistan war.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Indus Waters Treaty was signed in 1960 (Karachi), with World Bank mediation.

Economy — Industry

2. With reference to the new series of the Index of Core Industries (ICI), which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. Its base year is 2022-23.
  2. Iron ore has been added as the ninth core industry.
  3. It is compiled and released by the National Statistics Office (MoSPI).
  4. The core industries together carry about 32.88% weight in the IIP.

Answer: C. It is compiled and released by the National Statistics Office (MoSPI). — C is wrong: the ICI is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT (Ministry of Commerce & Industry). October 2026 growth was 10.9%.

Health

3. Consider the following statements about the National Health Accounts (NHA) Estimates 2023-24:
(a) Sikkim had the highest per capita government health expenditure among states.
(b) Bihar had the lowest per capita government health expenditure.
(c) Government health expenditure was about 1.43% of GDP.
(d) Government health expenditure was more than 60% of total health expenditure.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only a and c
  3. Only b and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: government spending was 43.5% of total health expenditure. Sikkim ₹16,026, Bihar ₹1,126; national ₹2,959 per person.

Rajasthan — Art & Culture

5. Which one of the following pairs (event — place) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Shekhawati Heritage Meet, 8 December — Mandawa
  2. Lok Manthan-2026 — Jawahar Kala Kendra, Jaipur
  3. Panchtirth snan — Brahma Sarovar, Pushkar
  4. Baba Shyam birth anniversary — Khatushyamji, Sikar

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

6. Consider the following statements regarding the 2026 Panchayati Raj election results in Rajasthan:
(a) Congress won a majority in 23 zila parishads.
(b) RLP won a clear majority in the Nagaur zila parishad.
(c) Zila pramukhs are to be elected on 25 November.
(d) Counting was held for all 41 zila parishads of the state.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b and c
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: counting was held for 37 of the 41 zila parishads. Pradhans will be elected on 27 November.

Society — Demography

7. Consider the following statements regarding Pew Research Center's 'Future of World Religions' projections:
(a) Hindus are projected to form about 75.8% of India's population in 2070.
(b) The Muslim share in India is projected to rise to about 19.6% by 2070.
(c) India will have the world's largest Muslim population by 2070.
(d) India alone holds about 94.5% of the world's Hindus today.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. Only b and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: India will have the world's second-largest Muslim population. Worldwide Hindu share slips from 14.9% to 13.6%.

International

8. Consider the following statements:
(a) Nepal PM Balendra Shah's visit on 22–23 November is his first to India since assuming office in March 2026.
(b) The UK and the EU have agreed to link their emissions trading schemes.
(c) The UK formally left the European Union on 31 January 2020.
(d) India and Nepal signed the Treaty of Peace and Friendship in 1950.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: D. a, b, c and d — All statements are correct. The UK–EU deals also scrap checks on plant and animal products and create a youth movement scheme.

International — Security

9. According to estimates cited after the US–China–Russia summit, which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. Russia has the world's largest nuclear arsenal.
  2. The US had about 3,708 nuclear warheads in 2023.
  3. China has more nuclear warheads than the United States.
  4. The Pentagon projects China will have over 1,000 operational warheads by 2030.

Answer: C. China has more nuclear warheads than the United States. — C is wrong: China had about 410 warheads in 2023, far fewer than the US (3,708). Russia has about 5,420 (March 2026).

Polity — Judiciary

10. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Uniform Civil Code is a Fundamental Right under Article 44.
(b) The Supreme Court held that a soldier injured by firing from his own troops is also entitled to injury pension.
(c) The Himachal Pradesh High Court held that 12 weeks' maternity leave is available even for the third child.
(d) Uttarakhand was the first state to implement a Uniform Civil Code.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: Article 44 is a Directive Principle of State Policy (Part IV), not a Fundamental Right. Uttarakhand implemented its UCC in January 2025.

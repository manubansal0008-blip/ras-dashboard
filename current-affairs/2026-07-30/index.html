RAS Current Affairs — 30 July 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 30 July 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — ADR

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Jaipur is hosting a Commonwealth conference on peace mediation and the rule of law.
(b) The conference is expected to adopt the 'Jaipur Peace Mediation Declaration'.
(c) India's Mediation Act was enacted in 2023.
(d) The Commonwealth has 25 member countries.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Commonwealth has 56 members; 52 were invited to this conference.

Education — Loans

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The PM Vidyalaxmi scheme covers students in 1,425 quality higher educational institutions.
(b) Loans up to ₹7.5 lakh get a 75% credit guarantee under PM Vidyalaxmi.
(c) PM Vidyalaxmi loans require collateral above ₹1 lakh.
(d) PM Vidyalaxmi gives 3% interest subvention for family income up to ₹8 lakh.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: PM Vidyalaxmi loans are collateral-free and guarantor-free.

International — Nuclear

3. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The US and Saudi Arabia signed a civil nuclear cooperation agreement.
Reason (R): Section 123 of the US Atomic Energy Act, 1954 governs such cooperation agreements.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A.

Panchayati Raj — Digital Platforms

4. Which one of the following pairs (platform — purpose) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. DAANVEER — Computer donation to Gram Panchayats
  2. e-GramSwaraj — Panchayat planning and accounting
  3. AuditOnline — Audit of panchayat accounts
  4. Meri Panchayat — Ministry of Defence app

Answer: D. Meri Panchayat — Ministry of Defence app — The Meri Panchayat App belongs to the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

International — Abraham Accords

5. Consider the following statements:
(a) Saudi Arabia signed the Abraham Accords in 2020.
(b) The Abraham Accords were signed in 2020.
(c) The UAE and Bahrain were the first to sign the Abraham Accords with Israel.
(d) Morocco later joined the Abraham Accords.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: Saudi Arabia has not joined; the US linked its nuclear deal to Saudi entry.

RAS Current Affairs — 1-10 June 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 1-10 June 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Cyber Security

1. Match List-I with List-II (cyber-security initiatives): List-I: (a) National cybercrime helpline (b) Complaint portal (c) Special police drive (d) MHA WhatsApp chatbot List-II: (I) cybercrime.gov.in (II) 1930 (III) TELL 14C (IV) Operation Cyber Shield

  1. (a)-II, (b)-I, (c)-IV, (d)-III
  2. (a)-I, (b)-II, (c)-III, (d)-IV
  3. (a)-II, (b)-IV, (c)-I, (d)-III
  4. (a)-III, (b)-I, (c)-IV, (d)-II

Answer: A. (a)-II, (b)-I, (c)-IV, (d)-III — Helpline 1930; portal cybercrime.gov.in; Operation Cyber Shield; chatbot TELL 14C.

Source: Sujas e-Bulletin / DIPR Rajasthan (May–June 2026)

Border Development

2. With reference to Vibrant Villages Programme-II in Rajasthan, consider: (I) Strategic villages of 5 border districts have been selected. (II) A total of 184 villages have been chosen. (III) Sri Ganganagar has the highest number of selected villages (68). (IV) Each selected village gets development works worth ₹3 crore per year. Which are correct?

  1. I, II, III and IV
  2. I and II only
  3. II, III and IV only
  4. I, III and IV only

Answer: A. I, II, III and IV — All four are correct.

Source: Sujas e-Bulletin / DIPR Rajasthan (May–June 2026)

  3. II and III only
  4. I and III only

Agriculture

5. Assertion (A): A farmer in Rajasthan now gets ₹9,000 per year under Kisan Samman Nidhi. Reason (R): The State adds ₹3,000 per year over and above the Centre's ₹6,000. Select the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A — ₹6,000 (Centre) + ₹3,000 (State) = ₹9,000; R explains A.

Source: Sujas e-Bulletin / DIPR Rajasthan (May–June 2026)

  1. (a)-III, (b)-I, (c)-II, (d)-IV
  2. (a)-I, (b)-III, (c)-II, (d)-IV
  3. (a)-III, (b)-II, (c)-I, (d)-IV

RAS Current Affairs — 22 October 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 22 October 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Forts

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Mehrangarh Fort was founded by Rao Jodha in 1459.
(b) Mehrangarh Fort is part of the UNESCO 'Hill Forts of Rajasthan' site.
(c) Mehrangarh Fort hosts the Rajasthan International Folk Festival (RIFF).
(d) Chittorgarh and Kumbhalgarh are among the Hill Forts of Rajasthan on the UNESCO list.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only a, c and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: the six UNESCO Hill Forts are Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore, Gagron, Amer and Jaisalmer. Mehrangarh is not one of them.

Rajasthan — Forts

2. Match List-I (Fort) with List-II (Builder) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Mehrangarh
(B) Kumbhalgarh
(C) Jaisalmer (Sonar Qila)
(D) Junagarh (Bikaner)
List-II
(1) Raja Rai Singh
(2) Rawal Jaisal
(3) Rao Jodha
(4) Rana Kumbha
Code:

  1. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  2. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  3. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  4. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1

Answer: C. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1 — Junagarh is one of the few major forts not built on a hilltop.

Polity — Sixth Schedule

3. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Sixth Schedule applies to Manipur.
(b) The Sixth Schedule provides for Autonomous District Councils in tribal areas.
(c) The Sixth Schedule applies to Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram.
(d) Leaders from Ladakh have demanded inclusion of Ladakh in the Sixth Schedule.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: Manipur's hill areas have district councils under a separate Act, not the Sixth Schedule.

International Organisations — Energy

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): India is not a full member of the International Energy Agency (IEA).
Reason (R): Full membership of the IEA is open only to OECD member countries.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. India is an IEA association country.

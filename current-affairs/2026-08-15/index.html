RAS Current Affairs — 15 August 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 15 August 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Startups

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) iStart Rajasthan is run by the Union Ministry of Commerce.
(b) Nirmala Sitharaman addressed the Rajasthan Youth Conclave in Jaipur.
(c) The Rajasthan Youth Conclave was held at the Rajasthan International Centre.
(d) iStart Rajasthan is the State's startup platform.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: D. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: iStart is run by the State's Department of IT and Communication.

Schemes — Water

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) Jal Jeevan Mission is a Central Sector Scheme fully funded by the Centre.
(b) Jal Jeevan Mission was launched on 15 August 2019.
(c) Jal Jeevan Mission is run by the Ministry of Jal Shakti.
(d) Jal Jeevan Mission targets 55 litres per person per day.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: B. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: it is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme (50:50 for most States, 90:10 for NE and Himalayan States).

Independence Day — Firsts

3. Match List-I (Event) with List-II (Person) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) First flag hoisting at the Red Fort, 1947
(B) First Governor-General of independent India
(C) First Indian Governor-General
(D) First President of India
List-II
(1) C. Rajagopalachari
(2) Jawaharlal Nehru
(3) Rajendra Prasad
(4) Lord Mountbatten
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  4. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2

Answer: C. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3 — The PM gave his 13th consecutive Independence Day address in 2026.

Rajasthan — Water

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Piped drinking water is especially important for rural Rajasthan.
Reason (R): Much of Rajasthan's groundwater is over-exploited or saline.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A.

Manufacturing — Schemes

5. Which one of the following pairs (Scheme — Focus) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. PM MITRA — Semiconductor fabs
  2. PLI — Production-linked incentives
  3. Make in India — 2014
  4. SPECS — Electronic components

Answer: A. PM MITRA — Semiconductor fabs — PM MITRA parks are mega textile parks; semiconductor fabs come under the India Semiconductor Mission.

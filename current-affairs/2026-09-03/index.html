RAS Current Affairs — 3 September 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 3 September 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Education — AISHE

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The All India Survey on Higher Education is released by the Ministry of Education.
(b) The Gender Parity Index in higher education was 1.08 in 2023-24.
(c) The All India Survey on Higher Education is conducted by NAAC.
(d) A Gender Parity Index above 1 means the female enrolment ratio is higher than the male ratio.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: AISHE is conducted by the Ministry of Education. Enrolment reached 4.50 crore in 2023-24.

Space — Artemis Accords

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) Türkiye became the 71st signatory of the Artemis Accords.
(b) The Artemis Accords were launched by the European Space Agency.
(c) The Artemis Accords are a set of non-binding principles for civil space exploration.
(d) India signed the Artemis Accords in June 2023.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only a, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: the Accords were launched by NASA and the US State Department in 2020.

Missions — Ministries

3. Match List-I (Mission) with List-II (Ministry/Department) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) National Quantum Mission
(B) India Semiconductor Mission
(C) Deep Ocean Mission
(D) National Green Hydrogen Mission
List-II
(1) Department of Science and Technology
(2) Ministry of Earth Sciences
(3) Ministry of Electronics and IT
(4) Ministry of New and Renewable Energy
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  4. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2

Answer: C. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4 — IISc Bengaluru hosts the Quantum Computing Thematic Hub of the National Quantum Mission.

Rajasthan — Tourism

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Rajasthan is one of India's leading destinations for foreign tourists.
Reason (R): Rajasthan has several UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Jantar Mantar and the Hill Forts.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. Rajasthan Police ordered a security audit of tourist sites.

Rajasthan — Forts

5. Which one of the following pairs (Fort — District) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Kumbhalgarh — Rajsamand
  2. Ranthambore — Sawai Madhopur
  3. Gagron — Kota
  4. Chittorgarh — Chittorgarh

Answer: C. Gagron — Kota — Gagron Fort is in Jhalawar district.

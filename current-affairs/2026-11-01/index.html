Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 1 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — History (Integration)

1. With reference to the integration of Rajasthan, consider the following statements:
(a) The formation of present-day Rajasthan was completed on 1 November 1956 with the merger of Ajmer-Merwara.
(b) Rajasthan Diwas is celebrated on 30 March to mark the formation of Greater Rajasthan in 1949.
(c) The Abu and Delwara tehsils were merged into Rajasthan on 1 November 1956.
(d) The Sunel Tappa of Rajasthan was transferred to Madhya Pradesh in 1956.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: in 1956 Sunel Tappa (Mandsaur) came to Rajasthan, while the Sironj sub-division (Kota) went to Madhya Pradesh. 1 November is linked to the seventh and final stage of integration.

Science & Technology — Space

3. Consider the following statements:
(a) NavIC is India's regional satellite navigation system developed by ISRO.
(b) NavIC covers India and a region extending about 1,500 km beyond its boundary.
(c) The NVS-03 navigation satellite was launched by GSLV-F18 from Sriharikota.
(d) NavIC provides global coverage like the American GPS.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: NavIC is a regional system, not global. NVS-03, a second-generation NavIC satellite, was launched on 30 October 2026.

Economy — Social Security

4. Consider the following statements:
(a) The EPFO was set up under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.
(b) The EPFO functions under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
(c) Under a new EPFO facility, Aadhaar-verified inoperative accounts with balances up to ₹1,000 will be settled without a claim.
(d) The Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance (EDLI) Scheme was introduced in 1995.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: EDLI was introduced in 1976. The Employees' Pension Scheme dates from 1995. EPFO marked its 74th Foundation Day on 1 November 2026.

Economy — Poverty

5. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The World Bank raised its international extreme poverty line from $2.15 to $3.00 per person per day.
Reason (R): The World Bank shifted its poverty measurement to 2021 purchasing power parities (PPPs).

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A: the update to 2021 PPPs raised the line to $3.00. At this line, India's extreme poverty was 2.6% in 2023.

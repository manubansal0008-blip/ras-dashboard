RAS Current Affairs — 7 October 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 7 October 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Heritage & Judiciary

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Jaipur's Walled City was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 2019.
(b) The principal seat of the Rajasthan High Court is at Jaipur.
(c) Article 226 gives High Courts the power to issue writs.
(d) Justices P.N. Bhagwati and V.R. Krishna Iyer are credited with developing Public Interest Litigation in India.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. Only a and c

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: the principal seat is at Jodhpur and the bench is at Jaipur. The High Court took suo motu note to protect Jaipur's heritage.

Rajasthan — Monuments

2. Match List-I (Monument) with List-II (Built by) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Hawa Mahal
(B) Jantar Mantar (Jaipur)
(C) Albert Hall
(D) Amber Fort
List-II
(1) Sawai Pratap Singh
(2) Sawai Ram Singh II
(3) Sawai Jai Singh II
(4) Raja Man Singh I
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  4. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Answer: C. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4 — Hawa Mahal (1799), Jantar Mantar (1734), Albert Hall (foundation stone 1876) and Amber Fort (begun under Man Singh I).

Economy — MSME

3. Consider the following statements:
(a) A ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund was announced in the Union Budget 2026.
(b) Under the revised criteria, a micro enterprise has investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore.
(c) The Udyam registration portal was launched in 2015.
(d) The Ministry of MSME was formed in 2007.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: the Udyam portal was launched in July 2020.

Rajasthan — Heritage

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Jaipur's Walled City is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Reason (R): Jaipur is a member of the UNESCO Creative Cities Network for Crafts and Folk Art.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A — Both are true, but R does not explain A: World Heritage status and the Creative Cities Network are separate UNESCO programmes.

Polity — Articles

5. Which one of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Article 32 — Writs by the Supreme Court
  2. Article 136 — Special Leave Petition
  3. Article 226 — Writs by High Courts
  4. Article 143 — Review of Supreme Court judgments

Answer: D. Article 143 — Review of Supreme Court judgments — Article 143 deals with the President's power to consult the Supreme Court (advisory jurisdiction). Review is under Article 137.

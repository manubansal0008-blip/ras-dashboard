RAS Current Affairs — 28 July 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 28 July 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Water

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Rajasthan launched its State Water Plan 2047 at the Rajasthan Water Conclave.
(b) The Ramjal Setu Link Project mainly draws water from the Narmada.
(c) The Ramjal Setu Link Project will supply drinking water to 17 districts.
(d) Under the Yamuna water agreement, water will reach Sikar, Jhunjhunu and Churu by pipeline.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Ramjal Setu (the modified PKC–ERCP) is based on the Parbati, Kalisindh and Chambal rivers.

Awards — Mathematics

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Fields Medal is awarded by the Royal Society of London.
(b) The Fields Medal is awarded every four years.
(c) The Fields Medal is given to mathematicians under 40.
(d) Maryam Mirzakhani was the first woman to win the Fields Medal.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: B. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: it is awarded by the International Mathematical Union.

(B) World Health Day
(C) World AIDS Day
(D) World Tuberculosis Day
List-II
(1) 1 December
(2) 7 April
(3) 24 March
(4) 28 July
Code:

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3

Answer: C. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3 — World Hepatitis Day 2026 had the theme 'Hepatitis: Let's Break It Down'.

Industry — Chemicals

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The BHAVYA Rasayan scheme will set up three dedicated chemical parks.
Reason (R): Under BHAVYA Rasayan, the Centre bears the entire cost of each park with no State share.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: C. A is true, but R is false — R is false: the Centre gives up to ₹1,000 crore per park and the State must give at least ₹500 crore.

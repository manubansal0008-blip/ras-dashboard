RAS Current Affairs — 4 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 4 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

1. With reference to the Panchayati Raj elections 2026 in Rajasthan, consider the following statements:
(a) EVMs are used for the election of Zila Parishad and Panchayat Samiti members.
(b) The number of electors in these elections is about 4.03 crore.
(c) The Pradhan is elected by the elected members of the Panchayat Samiti.
(d) The Sarpanch is elected indirectly by the elected Ward Panches.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: D. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: in Rajasthan the Sarpanch is directly elected by Gram Panchayat voters. Third-phase member polling was on 4 November, and all member votes are counted on 20 November.

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

2. Match List-I (Committee on Panchayati Raj) with List-II (Year) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Sadiq Ali Committee (Rajasthan)
(B) Girdhari Lal Vyas Committee (Rajasthan)
(C) Ashok Mehta Committee
(D) L.M. Singhvi Committee
List-II
(1) 1977
(2) 1964
(3) 1973
(4) 1986
Code:

  1. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Answer: D. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4 — Sadiq Ali (1964) and Girdhari Lal Vyas (1973) were Rajasthan committees. Ashok Mehta (1977) recommended a two-tier system. L.M. Singhvi (1986) recommended constitutional status for Panchayats.

Defence — Navy

List-I
(A) Project 15B
(B) Project 17A
(C) Project 15A
(D) Project 75
List-II
(1) Nilgiri-class frigates
(2) Visakhapatnam-class destroyers
(3) Kalvari-class submarines
(4) Kolkata-class destroyers
Code:

  1. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  2. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  3. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  4. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

International Organisations

4. With reference to the International Labour Organization (ILO), consider the following statements:
(a) The headquarters of the ILO is in Geneva.
(b) The ILO is the only tripartite agency of the United Nations, bringing together governments, employers and workers.
(c) India is a founding member of the ILO.
(d) The ILO was established under the UN Charter in 1945.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the ILO was created in 1919 under the Treaty of Versailles. Its 2026 report found women hold under 12% of manager jobs in India, which ranks 32nd of 34 Asia-Pacific economies.

Governance — Rural Employment

5. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The VB-G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026 provide for grievance bodies at the Gram Panchayat, Block and District levels.
Reason (R): The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 replaced the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A — Both are true, but R does not explain A. The rules require investigation within 7 working days and resolution within 15 days.

RAS Current Affairs — 26 September 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 26 September 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Economy — Pensions

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) NPS was introduced for new Central Government employees from 1 January 2014.
(b) The National Pension System is regulated by PFRDA.
(c) PFRDA is a statutory body under the PFRDA Act, 2013.
(d) 'NPS Swasthya' combines retirement saving with health cover.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: NPS applies to Central Government employees joining from 1 January 2004.

Transport — Aviation

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) UDAN was launched in 2016.
(b) UDAN stands for 'Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik'.
(c) UDAN is run by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.
(d) UDAN is a regional connectivity scheme of the Ministry of Civil Aviation.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only a, b and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: UDAN is run by the Ministry of Civil Aviation, which signed MoUs with 22 States/UTs for its next phase.

Economy — Reports

3. Match List-I (Report) with List-II (Publisher) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Asian Development Outlook
(B) World Economic Outlook
(C) Global Economic Prospects
(D) Trade and Development Report
List-II
(1) IMF
(2) UNCTAD
(3) World Bank
(4) ADB
Code:

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  3. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2
  4. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2

Answer: D. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2 — The ADB raised India's FY27 growth forecast to 7%.

Defence — Exercises

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The IAF hosts multinational air exercises such as Tarang Shakti.
Reason (R): Joint operations need common procedures among partner air forces.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. 'Tarang Shakti 2026' was held at Jodhpur.

Rajasthan — Airports

  2. Sanganer — Jaipur
  3. Kishangarh — Ajmer
  4. Nal — Kota

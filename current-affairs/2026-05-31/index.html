RAS Current Affairs — 21-31 May 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 21-31 May 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Infrastructure

1. With reference to the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (WDFC), consider: (I) About 39% of its length passes through Rajasthan. (II) It runs between JNPT and Dadri. (III) Its service of carrying loaded trucks on flat wagons is called the 'Roll-on-Roll' scheme. Which is/are correct?

  1. I and II only
  2. I, II and III
  3. II and III only
  4. I only

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — the verified facts are 39% and JNPT–Dadri; the truck-on-wagon service is the 'Rail-on-Truck' service.

Source: Sujas e-Bulletin / DIPR Rajasthan (May–June 2026)

Irrigation Projects

2. Match List-I (Project) with List-II (District/Region): List-I: (a) Upper High Level Canal (b) Bandh Baretha (c) Sei Dam (d) Parwan Project List-II: (I) Bharatpur (II) Vagad (Banswara) (III) Udaipur (IV) Jhalawar–Baran–Kota

  1. (a)-II, (b)-I, (c)-III, (d)-IV
  2. (a)-I, (b)-II, (c)-III, (d)-IV
  3. (a)-II, (b)-III, (c)-I, (d)-IV
  4. (a)-III, (b)-I, (c)-II, (d)-IV

Answer: A. (a)-II, (b)-I, (c)-III, (d)-IV — Upper High Level Canal–Vagad/Banswara (SCADA); Bandh Baretha–Bharatpur; Sei Dam–Udaipur; Parwan–Jhalawar/Baran/Kota.

Source: Sujas e-Bulletin / DIPR Rajasthan (May–June 2026)

Schemes

3. The 'Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission (Rural)' [VB-GREL], replacing MGNREGA, has which features? (I) It will be implemented from 1 July 2026. (II) It guarantees a minimum of 125 days of employment per year. (III) Wages will be paid weekly via DBT within 7 days. Which are correct?

  1. I, II and III
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I and III only

Answer: A. I, II and III — All three are correct.

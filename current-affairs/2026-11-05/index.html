RAS Current Affairs — 5 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 5 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Minerals

1. Match List-I (Mineral) with List-II (Associated deposit/location) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Lithium (inferred resources reported in 2023)
(B) Tungsten
(C) Copper
(D) Lead-Zinc
List-II
(1) Degana (Rajasthan)
(2) Zawar (Rajasthan)
(3) Reasi (Jammu & Kashmir)
(4) Khetri (Rajasthan)
Code:

  1. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Answer: C. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2 — Lithium: Reasi (J&K). Tungsten: Degana. Copper: Khetri. Lead-zinc: Zawar. The new ₹2,814 crore scheme gives 30% capital subsidy for two lithium and two nickel processing plants.

Rajasthan — Water Resources

2. Match List-I (Inter-state water project of Rajasthan) with List-II (Partner state/river) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Ramjal Setu Link Project
(B) Yamuna Water Agreement
(C) Kishau Dam Project
(D) Narmada Canal Project
List-II
(1) Haryana
(2) Madhya Pradesh
(3) Gujarat
(4) Tons river (Uttarakhand–Himachal Pradesh)
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  2. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  3. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: C. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3 — Ramjal Setu Link (formerly PKC-ERCP): with Madhya Pradesh. Yamuna water: Haryana. Kishau: Tons river on the Uttarakhand–HP border. Narmada canal: water from Gujarat (Sardar Sarovar). The CM's 5 November report counted these four as inter-state water agreements.

Rajasthan — Irrigation

3. With reference to the Narmada Canal in Rajasthan, consider the following statements:
(a) The Narmada Canal supplies water to parts of Jalore and Barmer districts.
(b) Micro-irrigation (sprinkler/drip) has been made compulsory in the Narmada Canal command area of Rajasthan.
(c) The Narmada Canal enters Rajasthan near Seelu in the Sanchore region.
(d) Rajasthan is a riparian state of the Narmada river basin.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Rajasthan is not a basin state of the Narmada. It received a share of water (0.5 MAF) under the Narmada Water Disputes Tribunal award.

Economy — Energy Storage

4. With reference to the ₹14,500 crore VGF scheme for grid-scale energy storage approved by the Union Cabinet, consider the following statements:
(a) The VGF scheme targets 92 GWh of storage, including 60 GWh of pumped storage and 30 GWh of battery storage.
(b) Battery storage projects under the VGF scheme must meet at least 40% domestic content.
(c) Viability Gap Funding supports projects that are economically justified but not financially viable on their own.
(d) A pumped storage plant pumps water uphill during peak demand and generates electricity during off-peak hours.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is reversed: water is pumped up in off-peak hours, using surplus or solar power, and released to generate during peak demand.

Polity — Judiciary

5. With reference to Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs), consider the following statements:
(a) The Centrally Sponsored Scheme for FTSCs began on 2 October 2019.
(b) The Central share of the FTSC scheme is funded from the Nirbhaya Fund.
(c) The FTSCs include exclusive courts for cases under the POCSO Act.
(d) The POCSO Act was enacted in 2019.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the POCSO Act was enacted in 2012 (amended in 2019). The scheme now runs to March 2031, and courts will rise from 779 to 850.

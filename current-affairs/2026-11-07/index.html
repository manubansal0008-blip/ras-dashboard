RAS Current Affairs — 7 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 7 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Water Resources

1. With reference to the Ramjal Setu Link Project, consider the following statements:
(a) The Ramjal Setu Link Project is the revised form of the PKC-ERCP (Parbati–Kalisindh–Chambal–Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project).
(b) The Chambal Aqueduct of the Ramjal Setu Link Project links Pipalda Samel (Kota) with Gohata (Bundi).
(c) The tripartite agreement among Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and the Centre for this project was exchanged in Jaipur on 17 December 2024.
(d) The Ramjal Setu Link Project mainly benefits the districts of western Rajasthan.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the project serves eastern Rajasthan. Phase-1 will provide drinking water to about 3.25 crore people, and the project cost is about ₹90,000 crore.

Rajasthan — Rivers

2. Match List-I (River) with List-II (Place of origin) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Chambal
(B) Banas
(C) Kalisindh
(D) Parbati (Parvati)
List-II
(1) Sehore region (Madhya Pradesh)
(2) Bagli (Dewas, Madhya Pradesh)
(3) Khamnor hills near Kumbhalgarh (Rajsamand)
(4) Janapav hills near Mhow (Madhya Pradesh)
Code:

  1. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  2. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  3. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Answer: A. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1 — Chambal rises in the Janapav hills, Banas in the Khamnor hills, Kalisindh near Bagli (Dewas) and the Parbati in the Sehore region. Parbati, Kalisindh and Chambal are the 'PKC' of PKC-ERCP.

Polity — Fundamental Rights

3. With reference to Article 12 of the Constitution, consider the following statements:
(a) Article 12 defines the term 'State' for the purposes of Part III of the Constitution.
(b) The Supreme Court has held that the National Book Trust is 'State' under Article 12.
(c) The tests for 'other authorities' under Article 12 were laid down in Ajay Hasia v. Khalid Mujib (1981).
(d) Being 'State' under Article 12 makes the National Book Trust a government department.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: D. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Court said NBT is bound by fundamental-rights obligations but does not become a government department. The 6 November 2026 ruling upheld a 2012 Delhi High Court decision.

List-I

  2. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  3. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: A. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1 — Ajay Hasia (1981): agency test. Kesavananda Bharati (1973): basic structure. Maneka Gandhi (1978): Article 21. Vivek Narayan Sharma (2023): demonetisation upheld 4:1.

Science & Technology — Electronics

5. Consider the following statements:
(a) The India Semiconductor Mission functions under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
(b) ECMS stands for the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme.
(c) India's first commercial semiconductor fab is being set up at Dholera, Gujarat.
(d) Micron's semiconductor assembly and test (ATMP) unit is located at Noida.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Micron's ATMP unit is at Sanand, Gujarat. The Semicon 2.0 portal was launched, and 12 new ECMS projects (₹7,503 crore) were approved.

RAS Current Affairs — 8 October 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 8 October 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Art & Culture

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The 20th International Art Festival 'Creyans' was held at Tonk.
(b) The princely state of Tonk was founded by Nawab Amir Khan.
(c) Sunehri Kothi is located in Tonk.
(d) Tonk is situated on the banks of the Chambal river.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Tonk lies on the Banas. The State Women Senior Kala Ratna Awards were presented at the festival.

Economy — Monetary Policy

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The RBI's MPC raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.5% in October 2026.
(b) Sanjay Malhotra is the Governor of the RBI.
(c) An increase in the repo rate increases liquidity in the economy.
(d) The Cash Reserve Ratio is the share of deposits that banks must keep with the RBI.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: a repo hike makes borrowing costlier and reduces liquidity. It was the first hike in about four years.

Economy — Monetary Tools

3. Match List-I (Instrument) with List-II (Meaning) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Repo rate
(B) Reverse repo rate
(C) Statutory Liquidity Ratio
(D) Cash Reserve Ratio
List-II
(1) Rate at which the RBI lends to banks against securities
(2) Liquid assets that banks keep with themselves
(3) Rate at which the RBI absorbs funds from banks
(4) Cash balance that banks keep with the RBI
Code:

  1. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

Answer: C. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4 — Repo and reverse repo are policy rates. SLR is held by banks themselves, while CRR is held with the RBI.

Polity — Union Territories

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): In Jammu and Kashmir, the Lieutenant Governor controls police and the All-India Services.
Reason (R): The J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 vests these subjects in the Lieutenant Governor of the Union Territory.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. The L-G's administrative reshuffle caused friction with the elected government.

Rajasthan — Rivers

5. Which one of the following pairs (City — River) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Tonk — Banas
  2. Jodhpur — Chambal
  3. Pali — Bandi
  4. Kota — Chambal

Answer: B. Jodhpur — Chambal — Jodhpur is not on the Chambal; it lies in the Luni basin, where the Jojari flows.

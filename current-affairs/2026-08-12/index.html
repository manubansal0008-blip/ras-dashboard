RAS Current Affairs — 12 August 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 12 August 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Governance

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Rabies is caused by a bacterium.
(b) The Rajasthan High Court gave the State one week to act on stray-dog directions.
(c) The directions include anti-rabies vaccination.
(d) The directions include registration of dangerous pets.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: C. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: rabies is caused by a virus.

Polity — National Symbols

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) Vande Mataram was composed by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay.
(b) Vande Mataram appears in the novel 'Anandamath'.
(c) The Constituent Assembly adopted Vande Mataram as the national song on 24 January 1950.
(d) Vande Mataram was written by Rabindranath Tagore.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: B. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Tagore wrote the national anthem, Jana Gana Mana. Vande Mataram now has the same legal protection as the anthem.

(D) National Bird

(1) Tiger

  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3

Polity — Fundamental Duties

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Insulting the national anthem is a punishable offence in India.
Reason (R): Article 51A(a) asks citizens to respect the national flag and the national anthem.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A — Both are true, but the penalty comes from the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971; Fundamental Duties are not enforceable.

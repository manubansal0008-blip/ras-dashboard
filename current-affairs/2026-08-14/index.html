Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 14 August 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Agriculture

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Centre approved ₹340.59 crore for Rajasthan under PM-RKVY and Krishonnati Yojana.
(b) Rajasthan's Annual Action Plan for 2026–27 under these schemes is ₹849.37 crore.
(c) Rajasthan is India's largest producer of bajra.
(d) Rajasthan is India's largest producer of sugarcane.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Uttar Pradesh leads in sugarcane.

History — Partition

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) Partition Horrors Remembrance Day is observed on 14 August.
(b) Partition took place under the Government of India Act, 1935.
(c) Partition Horrors Remembrance Day was declared in 2021.
(d) The Radcliffe Line divided Punjab and Bengal.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, c and d
  2. Only a and c
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Partition took place under the Indian Independence Act, 1947.

Space — Private Sector

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Private companies can now build and operate Earth observation satellites in India.
Reason (R): The Indian Space Policy, 2023 opened the space sector to private players.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A

  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. IN-SPACe signed a PPP for a 12-satellite constellation.

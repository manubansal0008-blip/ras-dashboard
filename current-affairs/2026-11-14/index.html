RAS Current Affairs — 14 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 14 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Economy — Monetary Policy

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The inflation target under the RBI Act is set by the Central Government in consultation with the RBI.
(b) Average CPI inflation outside the 2–6% band for three consecutive quarters is treated as a failure to meet the target.
(c) The Monetary Policy Committee has six members, three of whom are nominated by the Central Government.
(d) In case of a tie in the MPC, the RBI Governor has a second or casting vote.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only a and b
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: D. a, b, c and d — All four are correct (Sections 45ZA, 45ZB and 45ZN of the RBI Act). In this context, October 2026 CPI inflation was 6.09%, above the 6% upper limit.

List-II

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

3. With reference to Panchayati Raj in Rajasthan, consider the following statements:
(a) Panchayati Raj in Rajasthan was inaugurated at Nagaur on 2 October 1959.
(b) The State Election Commission conducts Panchayat elections under Article 243K of the Constitution.
(c) Women have 50% reservation in Panchayati Raj Institutions in Rajasthan.
(d) The Zila Pramukh is directly elected by the voters of the district.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Zila Pramukh is elected indirectly by the elected Zila Parishad members. The 2026 elections for this post are on 25 November, after counting on 20 November.

Rajasthan — Land Revenue

4. In the context of crop-loss assessment for drought relief in Rajasthan, which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Girdawari — field-wise inspection of crops by the patwari
  2. Namantaran — assessment of land revenue
  3. Jamabandi — record of rights
  4. Khasra — record by field (survey) number

Answer: B. Namantaran — assessment of land revenue — Namantaran (mutation) records a change in ownership; it is not revenue assessment. Kharif losses for the ₹9,321 crore package (87 tehsils, 15 districts) are being assessed through girdawari.

Rajasthan — Rural Livelihoods

5. Consider the following statements:
(a) Rajeevika is the state agency that implements DAY-NRLM in Rajasthan.
(b) A 'Lakhpati Didi' is an SHG member whose annual household income is at least ₹1 lakh.
(c) The Saras Aajeevika Mela is held to market products made by rural SHG women.
(d) The national target under the Lakhpati Didi initiative is 1 crore women.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and c
  3. Only a and b
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: B. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the national target is 3 crore. Rajasthan's target is 25 lakh, and about 20 lakh have reached it. The National Saras Fair will be held in Jaipur in December 2026.

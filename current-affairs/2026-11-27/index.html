RAS Current Affairs — 27 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 27 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Polity

1. Which of the following Articles of the Constitution deal with the composition of the Lok Sabha and readjustment after each census, respectively?

  1. Article 79 and Article 80
  2. Article 80 and Article 81
  3. Article 81 and Article 82
  4. Article 82 and Article 83

Answer: C. Article 81 and Article 82 — Article 81 covers the composition of the House of the People; Article 82 provides for readjustment after each census.

Geography

2. Consider the following statements about the Siang river:
(I) It is known as the Yarlung Tsangpo in Tibet.
(II) It becomes the Brahmaputra after being joined by the Dibang and Lohit.
(III) Arunachal Pradesh is covered by the Sixth Schedule.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: A. I and II only — III is false — Arunachal Pradesh is not under the Sixth Schedule.

Economy

3. As per the final estimates for 2025-26, India's foodgrain production was about:

  1. 378.5 million tonnes
  2. 332.3 million tonnes
  3. 353.9 million tonnes
  4. 405.0 million tonnes

Answer: A. 378.5 million tonnes — Foodgrain output was a record 378.53 million tonnes, 208 lakh tonnes above 2024-25.

Defence & IR

4. List-I (Exercise / Mechanism) | List-II (Partner)
A. Joint Defence Cooperation Committee (14th, Abu Dhabi) | 1. Nepal
B. Surya Kiran | 2. Colombo Security Conclave members
C. Desert Cyclone | 3. UAE (army exercise)
D. Five pillars incl. maritime security | 4. UAE
Select the correct match:

  1. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
  3. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  4. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2

Answer: D. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2 — JDCC is with the UAE; Surya Kiran with Nepal; Desert Cyclone is the India–UAE army exercise; the five pillars belong to the Colombo Security Conclave.

Health

5. Consider the following statements about vitamin B12:
(I) It is essential for the formation of red blood cells.
(II) Plant foods are its main natural source.
(III) An ICMR survey found nearly two-thirds of Indian adolescents deficient in it.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: C. I and III only — II is false — B12 comes mainly from animal foods such as milk, eggs and meat.

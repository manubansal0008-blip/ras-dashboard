RAS Current Affairs — 5 September 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 5 September 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Defence — Air Force

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) Air Vice Marshal Shakti Sharma is the first woman from a non-medical branch to reach two-star rank.
(b) Air Vice Marshal is equivalent to Lieutenant General in the Army.
(c) Air Vice Marshal is a two-star rank in the Indian Air Force.
(d) Shakti Sharma was commissioned into the IAF Education Branch in 1994.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Air Vice Marshal equals Major General.

Defence — Ranks

2. Match List-I (IAF rank) with List-II (Army equivalent) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Air Vice Marshal
(B) Air Marshal
(C) Air Commodore
(D) Group Captain
List-II
(1) Lieutenant General
(2) Brigadier
(3) Colonel
(4) Major General
Code:

  1. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  2. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  3. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  4. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Answer: B. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3 — Equivalent Army ranks for IAF officers.

Environment — Wetlands

3. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Ramsar Convention was signed in 1971.
(b) Rajasthan has no Ramsar site.
(c) Ramsar, the city after which the Convention is named, is in Iran.
(d) World Wetlands Day is observed on 2 February.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a, c and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a and c

Answer: B. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Rajasthan has Keoladeo, Sambhar, Khichan and Menar as Ramsar sites. India and Germany signed a declaration on wetland conservation.

Rajasthan — Health

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease is rising in India.
Reason (R): Obesity and a sedentary lifestyle are major risk factors for fatty liver.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. Rajasthan ranked first in fatty-liver screening under 'Mission Liver Smile'.

Rajasthan — Ramsar Sites

5. Which one of the following pairs (Ramsar site — District) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Menar — Udaipur
  2. Khichan — Phalodi
  3. Sambhar — Jaisalmer
  4. Keoladeo — Bharatpur

Answer: C. Sambhar — Jaisalmer — Sambhar Lake lies on the borders of Jaipur, Ajmer and Nagaur (now Didwana-Kuchaman) districts.

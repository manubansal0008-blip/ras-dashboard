RAS Current Affairs — 11 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 11 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Power Sector

1. With reference to the power sector in Rajasthan, consider the following statements:
(a) Rajasthan has three power distribution companies: Jaipur, Ajmer and Jodhpur DISCOMs.
(b) The transmission and distribution losses of the Rajasthan DISCOMs fell to 12.84% in 2025-26.
(c) The Rajasthan State Electricity Board was unbundled into separate companies in 2000.
(d) Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Prasaran Nigam (RVPN) is a power distribution company.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: D. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: RVPN handles transmission. T&D losses fell from 19.43% (2019-20) to 12.84%, and Jodhpur DISCOM's losses from about 38% to 18.64%.

Governance — Urban Finance

3. With reference to municipal bonds and urban local bodies, consider the following statements:
(a) Patna Municipal Corporation is the first civic body of Bihar to list municipal bonds on the NSE.
(b) The issue of municipal bonds in India is regulated by the RBI.
(c) Urban local bodies derive their constitutional status from the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.
(d) The Twelfth Schedule of the Constitution lists 18 subjects for municipalities.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only a and c
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: municipal debt securities are regulated by SEBI. Patna raised ₹199.99 crore for 10 years at a 9.20% coupon (rating AA).

Polity — Schedules

4. Match List-I (Schedule of the Constitution) with List-II (Subject) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Seventh Schedule
(B) Tenth Schedule
(C) Eleventh Schedule
(D) Twelfth Schedule
List-II
(1) Powers of Panchayats (29 subjects)
(2) Union, State and Concurrent Lists
(3) Powers of municipalities (18 subjects)
(4) Anti-defection provisions
Code:

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Answer: B. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3 — Seventh: legislative lists. Tenth: anti-defection (52nd Amendment, 1985). Eleventh: Panchayats (73rd). Twelfth: municipalities (74th).

Economy — MSME

5. With reference to the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP), consider the following statements:
(a) PMEGP was launched in 2015.
(b) PMEGP is implemented at the national level by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC).
(c) PMEGP is a scheme of the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.
(d) PMEGP was formed by merging the PMRY and REGP schemes.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: PMEGP was launched in August 2008. ₹1,000 crore in margin money subsidy was released for 11,333 new micro enterprises.

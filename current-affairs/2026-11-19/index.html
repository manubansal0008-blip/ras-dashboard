Internal Security

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The NCB's drive against illicit cannabis and opium covered Rajasthan.
(b) The Vision Document on Narcotics Control (2026-2029) is issued by the Union Home Ministry.
(c) It aims to end illicit cannabis and poppy cultivation in identified areas by December 2030.
(d) Drug offences in India are governed by the NDPS Act, 1985.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d

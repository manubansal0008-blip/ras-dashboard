RAS Current Affairs — 2 November 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 2 November 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Irrigation

1. With reference to the Gang Canal, whose centenary is being celebrated, consider the following statements:
(a) Construction of the Gang Canal was started on 5 December 1925 by Maharaja Ganga Singh of Bikaner.
(b) The Gang Canal carries Sutlej water from the Hussainiwala (Ferozepur) headworks.
(c) Water of the Gang Canal first reached Shivpur Head on 26 October 1927.
(d) The Gang Canal is fed by the Ravi river.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Gang Canal is fed by the Sutlej. Centenary celebrations run from 26 October 2026 to 26 October 2027.

Rajasthan — Canals

2. Match List-I (Canal) with List-II (Headworks/Origin) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Indira Gandhi Canal
(B) Gang Canal
(C) Gurgaon Canal
(D) Chambal Right Main Canal
List-II
(1) Hussainiwala (Ferozepur)
(2) Okhla (Yamuna)
(3) Harike Barrage
(4) Kota Barrage
Code:

  1. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  2. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: C. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4 — Indira Gandhi Canal: Harike Barrage (Sutlej–Beas confluence). Gang Canal: Hussainiwala. Gurgaon Canal: Okhla on the Yamuna. Chambal Right Main Canal: Kota Barrage.

Rajasthan — History

3. With reference to Maharaja Ganga Singh of Bikaner, consider the following statements:
(a) Sri Ganganagar city is named after Maharaja Ganga Singh.
(b) Maharaja Ganga Singh signed the Treaty of Versailles (1919) on behalf of India.
(c) Maharaja Ganga Singh was the first Chancellor of the Chamber of Princes.
(d) Maharaja Ganga Singh was the ruler of Jodhpur state.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: Ganga Singh ruled Bikaner (1887–1943). He built the Gang Canal, which turned Sri Ganganagar into a major farming region.

Environment — Biodiversity

4. With reference to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD), consider the following statements:
(a) COP17 of the CBD was held at Yerevan, Armenia, in October 2026.
(b) The '30x30' target of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework aims to conserve 30% of land and sea by 2030.
(c) The CBD was opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992.
(d) The Cartagena Protocol under the CBD deals with access and benefit-sharing of genetic resources.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: D. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the Cartagena Protocol covers biosafety (living modified organisms). Access and benefit-sharing falls under the Nagoya Protocol. COP17 adopted the 'Yerevan Pathway'.

Economy — Energy

5. With reference to Coal India Limited (CIL), consider the following statements:
(a) Coal India Limited was established on 1 November 1975.
(b) Coal India Limited functions under the Ministry of Coal.
(c) India is the world's largest exporter of coal.
(d) Coal India Limited has Maharatna status.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only b, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: India is the world's second-largest producer and a major importer of coal. CIL recorded its highest-ever monthly output (67.2 MT) in October 2026.

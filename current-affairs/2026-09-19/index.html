RAS Current Affairs — 19 September 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 19 September 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Mines — Welfare

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana was launched in 2015.
(b) PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana is implemented through District Mineral Foundations.
(c) District Mineral Foundations were set up under the MMDR Amendment Act, 2015.
(d) PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana is run by the Ministry of Coal.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: PMKKKY is run by the Ministry of Mines.

Technology — Semiconductors

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The India Semiconductor Mission works under the Ministry of Heavy Industries.
(b) The India Semiconductor Mission works under the Ministry of Electronics and IT.
(c) SEMICON India 2026 was held at Yashobhoomi.
(d) Yashobhoomi is located in Dwarka, New Delhi.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a and b
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a, b and c

Answer: A. Only b, c and d — Statement a is wrong: ISM is under MeitY. SEMICON India 2026 had the theme 'Silicon to Systems'.

Rural Livelihoods — Sakhis

3. Match List-I (Sakhi) with List-II (Role) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) BC Sakhi
(B) Pashu Sakhi
(C) Krishi Sakhi
(D) Pension Sakhi
List-II
(1) Livestock care
(2) NPS awareness and enrolment
(3) Doorstep banking
(4) Farm extension
Code:

  1. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  2. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  3. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: B. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2 — The Department of Rural Development and PFRDA signed an MoU for 'Pension Sakhi'.

Rajasthan — Industry

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The Bhiwadi–Neemrana belt is a major industrial hub of Rajasthan.
Reason (R): The Bhiwadi–Neemrana belt lies along the Delhi–Jaipur highway within the National Capital Region.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A. A PM-SETU ITI cluster was set up at Bhiwadi.

Regulators

5. Which one of the following pairs (Regulator — Sector) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. PFRDA — Pensions
  2. IRDAI — Insurance
  3. SEBI — Securities market
  4. NABARD — Regulation of commercial banks

Answer: D. NABARD — Regulation of commercial banks — The RBI regulates commercial banks. NABARD is a development bank for agriculture and rural areas.

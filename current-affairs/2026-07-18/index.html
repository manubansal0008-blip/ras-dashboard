RAS Current Affairs — 18 July 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 18 July 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Health Schemes

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Rajasthan Government suspended 51 hospitals from RGHS over financial irregularities.
(b) Drug licences of 14 allopathic pharmacy stores linked to RGHS were cancelled.
(c) RGHS is a scheme only for BPL families in rural areas.
(d) RGHS is implemented through the Rajasthan State Health Assurance Agency.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and b
  2. a, b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: RGHS is a health scheme for State government employees, pensioners and certain other groups.

Economy — Semiconductors

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) Semicon 2.0 has a financial outlay of ₹1,27,500 crore.
(b) The India Semiconductor Mission was first launched in 2021.
(c) India's first commercial semiconductor fab began production in 2024.
(d) Under ISM 1.0, 12 projects were approved in six States.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only b, c and d
  2. Only a, b and d
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only a and b

Answer: B. Only a, b and d — Statement c is wrong: India's first fab is expected to be commissioned only in 2028.

Health — Research

3. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The draft National Health Research Policy 2026 seeks to raise health research spending sharply.
Reason (R): India spends only about 0.024% of GDP on health research, far below high-income countries.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A.

International — Codex

4. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Codex Alimentarius Commission was set up by FAO and WHO.
(b) India has chaired the Codex Committee on Spices and Culinary Herbs since 2014.
(c) The Spices Board of India is the secretariat of CCSCH.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b and c
  2. Only a and b
  3. Only a and c
  4. Only b and c

Answer: A. a, b and c — All three are correct. Codex adopted standards for large cardamom, coriander and vanilla at its 49th session.

Railways — Firsts

5. Which one of the following pairs (item — fact) is NOT correctly matched?

  1. Jind–Sonipat — Haryana
  2. NaMo Green Rail — Hydrogen fuel cells
  3. Vande Bharat — Semi-high-speed train
  4. Hydrogen train by-product — Carbon monoxide

Answer: D. Hydrogen train by-product — Carbon monoxide — A hydrogen fuel-cell train emits only water vapour and heat.

RAS Current Affairs — 3 October 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 3 October 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994 came into force in April 1994.
(b) The minimum age to contest Panchayat elections is 25 years.
(c) Rajasthan introduced a minimum educational qualification for PRI elections in 2015, which was later withdrawn.
(d) The State Election Commission of Rajasthan was constituted in 1994.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a and c
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, c and d
  4. a, b, c and d

Answer: C. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: the minimum age is 21 years (Article 243F). The 2026 polls cover 41 Zila Parishads, 457 Panchayat Samitis and 14,403 Gram Panchayats.

Governance — Rural Employment

2. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): Some States objected to the law that replaced MGNREGA.
Reason (R): The new VB-G RAM G Act removed Mahatma Gandhi's name from the rural job scheme.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A — Both are true and R explains A: the name change was the main reason for the objections.

Geography — Straits

3. Match List-I (Strait) with List-II (Water bodies it connects) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Strait of Hormuz
(B) Strait of Malacca
(C) Bab-el-Mandeb
(D) Palk Strait
List-II
(1) Bay of Bengal and Palk Bay
(2) Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman
(3) Red Sea and Gulf of Aden
(4) Andaman Sea and South China Sea
Code:

  1. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  2. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1
  3. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  4. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2

Answer: B. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1 — Iran's closure of the Strait of Hormuz led the G7 to release 100 million barrels of oil.

Internal Security — Manipur

4. Consider the following statements:
(a) The Suspension of Operations pact in Manipur is between the Centre, the State government and Kuki-Zo groups.
(b) Manipur shares an international border with Bangladesh.
(c) Loktak Lake is located in Manipur.
(d) Keibul Lamjao, the floating national park, is in Manipur.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, c and d
  2. Only a and c
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, c and d — Statement b is wrong: Manipur borders Myanmar, not Bangladesh.

International Groupings

5. Which one of the following countries is NOT a member of the G7?

  1. Russia
  2. Canada
  3. Italy
  4. Japan

Answer: A. Russia — The G7 members are the USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada. Russia was suspended from the G8 in 2014.

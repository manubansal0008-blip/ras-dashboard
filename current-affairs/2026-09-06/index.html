RAS Current Affairs — 6 September 2026

Daily current-affairs MCQs for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 6 September 2026 — with answers and explanations.

Health — Regulation

1. Consider the following statements:
(a) The National Council for Clinical Establishments is constituted under the Clinical Establishments Act, 2010.
(b) The national mortuary standards bar morgues in basements.
(c) The national mortuary standards prescribe cold storage at 4–8 °C.
(d) Public health is a subject in the Union List.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. a, b, c and d
  2. Only b, c and d
  3. Only a, b and c
  4. Only a and b

Answer: C. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: public health is in the State List (Entry 6).

Environment — Big Cats

2. Consider the following statements:
(a) The International Big Cat Alliance was launched by India in April 2023.
(b) The International Big Cat Alliance is headquartered in India.
(c) The International Big Cat Alliance covers seven big cats, including the jaguar and the puma.
(d) All seven big cats covered by the International Big Cat Alliance are found in India.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. Only a, b and c
  2. Only a and b
  3. a, b, c and d
  4. Only b, c and d

Answer: A. Only a, b and c — Statement d is wrong: the jaguar and the puma are found only in the Americas. IBCA signed an MoU with the ADB.

Geography — Islands

3. Match List-I (Island) with List-II (State/UT) and select the correct answer using the code given below:
List-I
(A) Agatti
(B) Swaraj Dweep (Havelock)
(C) Majuli
(D) Elephanta
List-II
(1) Assam
(2) Andaman and Nicobar Islands
(3) Maharashtra
(4) Lakshadweep
Code:

  1. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  2. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

Answer: C. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3 — 'Mission Rangeen Machhli 2031' for ornamental fisheries was released at Agatti.

Rajasthan — Transport

4. Read the following and choose the correct answer:
Assertion (A): The Delhi–Gurugram–SNB–Alwar RRTS corridor will serve the Bawal–Behror region.
Reason (R): RRTS projects are implemented by the National Capital Region Transport Corporation.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A — Both are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.

Organisations — Headquarters

  2. WWF International — Gland
  3. UNEP — Nairobi
  4. Asian Development Bank — Bangkok

Answer: D. Asian Development Bank — Bangkok — The ADB is headquartered in Manila.

RAS News Digest — Today's News — 2 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated Today's News — 2 July 2026.

Economy

Commercial LPG cut by up to ₹183.50 — first reduction of 2026

  • Oil companies cut the 19-kg commercial LPG cylinder by up to ₹183.50 from 1 July; Delhi's rate fell to ₹2,930 (from ₹3,113.50).
  • First cut of 2026 after four hikes totalling ₹1,345 since 7 March amid the West Asia (Iran–Israel) conflict.
  • Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) was cut ₹5/litre; the 14.2-kg domestic LPG cylinder was left unchanged.
  • The move followed easing of Strait of Hormuz supply concerns (ICRA); private retailer Nayara also cut petrol and diesel.

Source: The Hindu / Rajasthan Patrika, 2 Jul 2026

Economy

June GST up 13.9% to ₹1.95 lakh crore — highest rise in 13 months

  • June 2026 gross GST collection was ₹1.95 lakh crore, the highest year-on-year growth in 13 months.
  • Growth was driven mainly by imports (~35% rise); domestic-transaction GST grew only about 6.5%.
  • GST has completed nine years; imports' share of collections is rising, which experts flagged as a concern.
  • Analysts urged fixing the inverted duty structure and input-tax-credit issues.

Source: The Hindu, 2 Jul 2026

Polity & Governance

MGNREGA replaced by VB-G RAM G; national floor wage ₹300/day

  • The Viksit Bharat Guarantee for Rozgar & Ajeevika Mission (Gramin), 'VB-G RAM G', took effect 1 July, replacing MGNREGA (2005).
  • A central floor wage of ₹300/day was fixed; guaranteed workdays were raised from 100 to 125.
  • 21 States/UTs paying below ₹300 got hikes (UP ₹48, Bihar ₹45, MP ₹39, Rajasthan ₹19).
  • The funding pattern shifts more cost to States; critics cited the 2019 Satpathy panel's ₹375/day recommendation.

Source: The Hindu / Indian Express, 2 Jul 2026

International Relations

Japanese PM Takaichi visits India; FS Misri gets one-year extension

  • Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi began a state visit to India themed 'Trusted Partners, Shared Vision for Growth & Prosperity'.
  • Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri (1989-batch IFS) was given a one-year extension till 14 July 2027.
  • India said MoS External Affairs Pabitra Margherita would represent it at Ayatollah Khamenei's funeral in Iran.
  • The visit set the stage for major economic, technology and defence announcements.

Source: Indian Express / The Hindu, 2 Jul 2026

Defence

New Army Chief Gen Dhiraj Seth unveils the 'VIJAY' roadmap

  • Gen Dhiraj Seth, the 31st Chief of the Army Staff, unveiled the roadmap 'VIJAY' a day after taking charge.
  • VIJAY stands for Vigilance, Innovation, Jointness/Integration, Atmanirbharta and Yodha First.
  • It aims to build a technology-enabled, future-ready, multi-domain force through a 'Whole-of-Nation' approach.
  • Fresh senior appointments were made across the Air Force and the integrated defence staff.

Source: The Hindu, 2 Jul 2026

Science & Technology

UN's Independent Scientific Panel on AI warns of 'catastrophic' risks

  • The UN's Independent International Scientific Panel on AI released its first preliminary report in Geneva.
  • Co-chaired by Yoshua Bengio, the 40-member panel is envisioned as an 'IPCC for AI'.
  • It warned that rapid AI capability growth could pose catastrophic risks and cited 'growing evidence of deceptive AI behaviour'.
  • It flagged near-term constraints from shortages of energy and high-quality data.

Source: Indian Express, 2 Jul 2026

Polity & Governance

High-Level Committee on Demographic Changes to question States

  • The High-Level Committee on Demographic Changes (HLCDC) will seek State data on population and settlement changes since Census 2011.
  • PM Modi announced the demography mission on 15 August 2025; the committee was constituted on 26 May 2026.
  • It will also seek Election Commission data on names deleted during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR).
  • The next Census (2027) will come after a 16-year gap.

Source: The Hindu, 2 Jul 2026

RAS News Digest — 31 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 31 July 2026.

Industry — Telecom

India's first Telecom Manufacturing Zone to come up in Gwalior

  • The Department of Telecommunications and Madhya Pradesh will set up India's first Telecom Manufacturing Zone.
  • Phase-I of the Telecom Manufacturing Zone will be located in Gwalior.
  • The zone aims to promote indigenous telecom design and manufacturing.
  • The zone targets equipment makers, MSMEs and start-ups.
  • The zone is part of the Aatmanirbhar Bharat goal.
Polity — National Symbols

Parliament passes Bill extending legal protection to Vande Mataram

  • Parliament passed the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026.
  • The Bill extends the protection of the 1971 Act to the National Song, Vande Mataram.
  • Section 3 of the 1971 Act punishes obstructing the singing of the National Anthem with up to three years' imprisonment.
  • On 24 January 1950, Dr. Rajendra Prasad said Vande Mataram would be honoured equally with Jana Gana Mana.
  • Article 51A(a) makes respect for the National Flag and Anthem a Fundamental Duty.
Environment — Judiciary

Supreme Court quashes 2021 memorandum allowing ex post facto environmental clearances

  • The Supreme Court quashed the Environment Ministry's 2021 Office Memorandum on retrospective clearances.
  • The Court held that prior environmental clearance under the EIA Notification, 2006 is mandatory.
  • The Court said the memorandum promoted a 'pollute and then pay' approach violating Articles 14 and 21.
  • Retrospective regularisation can come only through a statutory notification under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.
  • Clearances already granted remain valid unless individually challenged.

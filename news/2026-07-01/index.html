Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated Today's News — 1 July 2026.

Economy

RBI holds repo rate at 5.25%; trims growth, raises inflation forecast

  • The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) kept the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25% and retained a neutral stance.
  • It LOWERED the FY27 GDP growth forecast to 6.6% (from 6.9%) and RAISED the inflation projection to 5.1% (from 4.6%).
  • The decision cited risks from the West Asia conflict and elevated energy prices.
  • A neutral stance keeps room to move rates either way as data evolves.

Source: The Hindu / Indian Express, 1 Jul 2026

Polity & Governance

Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026 to raise judges' strength

  • The Bill seeks to increase the sanctioned strength of Supreme Court judges to ease pendency.
  • The number of SC judges is fixed by Parliament under the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956 — Article 124 leaves it to Parliament.
  • The strength has earlier been raised by amendments in 1977, 1986, 2008 and 2019.
  • It is ordinary legislation, not a constitutional amendment.

Source: The Hindu, 1 Jul 2026

Governance / Technology

Digital India marks 11 years: DBT, fibre and services milestones

  • Digital India (launched 1 July 2015) completed 11 years; over ₹51 lakh crore has been transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  • About 42 lakh route km of optical fibre has been laid, connecting nearly 2.2 lakh villages.
  • 70+ crore DigiLocker users; Poshan Tracker monitors 8.9+ crore mothers and children; 2,500+ government services are on mobile.
  • Startup India and the Atal Innovation Mission drive deep-tech, with 12 semiconductor plants taking shape.

Source: Indian Express (Digital India), 1 Jul 2026

Defence

Gen Upendra Dwivedi retires as Army Chief; Gen Dhiraj Seth takes over

  • Gen Upendra Dwivedi demitted office as the 30th Chief of the Army Staff; Gen Dhiraj Seth took over.
  • He said future wars will be more 'joint, integrated and theatre-oriented'.
  • The remark revives the push for Integrated Theatre Commands, driven by the CDS and the Department of Military Affairs.
  • India today has 17 single-service commands and only two tri-service commands.

Source: Indian Express, 1 Jul 2026

International Relations

India joins G7 2026 outreach at Évian; India–EU FTA talks concluded

  • The G7 Summit 2026 was hosted by France at Évian-les-Bains; India attended as an invited partner in the Outreach Session (not a G7 member).
  • India and the European Union announced the conclusion of negotiations for their Free Trade Agreement (FTA).
  • The India–EU FTA is among India's most significant trade deals, covering a large market.
  • The G7 comprises the US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada.

Source: The Hindu, 1 Jul 2026

Environment & Ecology

India crosses 100 Ramsar sites — the most in Asia

  • India now has 100 Ramsar (wetland) sites — the highest in Asia.
  • Tamil Nadu leads all states with 20 Ramsar sites.
  • The Sundarbans holds all five designations: Ramsar site, UNESCO World Heritage Site, Tiger Reserve, National Park and Biosphere Reserve.
  • Ramsar recognition supports wetland conservation and 'wise use'.

Source: The Hindu, 1 Jul 2026

Science & Technology

ISRO sets up panel to review PSLV anomaly; launches 3rd Space Hackathon

  • ISRO constituted a national-level expert committee to review an anomaly observed in the PSLV.
  • It also launched the third edition of the Indian Space Hackathon.
  • The PSLV uses solid and liquid stages and mainly places satellites in polar / sun-synchronous orbits.
  • Heavier communication satellites to GEO are launched by the GSLV / LVM3 with cryogenic upper stages.

Source: The Hindu, 1 Jul 2026

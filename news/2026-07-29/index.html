RAS News Digest — 29 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 29 July 2026.

Environment

Seventh draft notification on the Western Ghats Eco-Sensitive Area

  • The Environment Ministry reissued the Western Ghats ESA draft for the seventh time.
  • The proposed ESA covers 56,825.7 sq km across six States.
  • Karnataka has the largest share of the proposed ESA, and Gujarat the smallest.
  • The draft bans commercial mining, quarrying and new thermal power plants in the ESA.
  • The Gadgil Committee (2011) and the Kasturirangan Committee (2013) earlier studied the Western Ghats.
Environment — Wildlife

Global Tiger Day: India's tiger count at 3,682 across 58 reserves

  • Global Tiger Day is observed on 29 July.
  • Global Tiger Day traces back to the 2010 St. Petersburg Tiger Summit.
  • India's tiger population is 3,682, and India has 58 tiger reserves.
  • Project Tiger was launched in 1973.
  • The All India Tiger Estimation is conducted every four years.
Labour — International

ILO adopts Convention No. 193 on decent work in the platform economy

  • The International Labour Organization adopted Convention No. 193 at the 114th International Labour Conference in Geneva.
  • Convention No. 193 is described as the first binding international treaty for app-based gig and platform workers.
  • Convention No. 193 requires human oversight of significant automated decisions.
  • India's government delegates abstained in the vote.
  • India's Code on Social Security, 2020 already recognises gig and platform workers.

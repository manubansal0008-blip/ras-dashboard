RAS News Digest — 14 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 14 November 2026.

Economy

Retail inflation rises to 6.09% in October, above RBI's upper tolerance limit

  • CPI inflation for October 2026 (released by MoSPI on 12 November) was 6.09%, up from 5.54% in September. This is the highest level since October 2024 (6.21%).
  • Food inflation rose to 8.94%. The sharpest increases were in onion, garlic, ginger and lemon. Rural CPI was 6.63% and urban CPI 5.41%.
  • Under the flexible inflation targeting framework, the target is 4% ±2% (2–6%). Inflation above 6% for three consecutive quarters counts as a failure, and the RBI must then report to the Centre (Section 45ZN, RBI Act).
Governance & Schemes

Cabinet extends Stand-Up India and Mudra credit guarantee fund to 2031

  • Stand-Up India runs to 31 March 2031 with a ₹4,111 crore credit guarantee corpus. It gives bank loans of ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore to SC, ST and women entrepreneurs for greenfield units. It was launched on 5 April 2016.
  • The Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) under PM Mudra Yojana is continued to 31 March 2031 with a ₹5,000 crore corpus. It is managed by NCGTC.
  • The Cabinet also approved Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) rates for P&K fertilisers for Rabi 2026-27 (₹62,370 crore) and the ₹3,230 crore 'Yuge Yugeen Bharat' National Museum in the North and South Blocks, New Delhi.
Economy — Trade

45th India International Trade Fair begins; Rajasthan a partner state

  • Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal inaugurated IITF 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 13 November. It runs from 14 to 27 November with the theme 'Vikas aur Virasat'.
  • The partner states are Bihar, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh. The focus states are Assam, Delhi, Jharkhand and West Bengal.
  • It is organised by ITPO (Ministry of Commerce & Industry). About 3,500 exhibitors are taking part, and there is a One District One Product pavilion.
Environment — Index

Climate Change Performance Index 2027: India slips to 28th

  • The index was released at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, by Germanwatch, NewClimate Institute and CAN International. India ranked 28th with a 'Medium' rating, down from 23rd last year.
  • No country was rated 'Very High', so the first three places were left empty. Denmark is 4th. Iran is last (68th), and the USA is 67th.
  • There are four components: GHG emissions (40%), renewable energy (20%), energy use (20%) and climate policy (20%).
Health

WHO Global TB Report 2026: India's TB deaths down 38% since 2015

  • India's TB mortality fell 38% from 2015 levels, against a global fall of 35%. The treatment success rate for drug-susceptible TB is 94%.
  • India still has about 25% of the world's new TB cases, the highest of any country. It is followed by Indonesia, the Philippines, China and Pakistan.
  • Under the TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan, over 25 crore people were screened between December 2024 and November 2026. The SDG target is to end TB by 2030, and India's own target is 2025.
Art & Culture

57th IFFI Goa from 20 November; Spain is Country of Honour

  • The festival runs from 20 to 28 November 2026 with the theme 'Stories Without Borders'. Ashutosh Gowariker is the Festival Director.
  • Spain is the Country of Honour, as 2026 is the Spain-India Dual Year. Russia is the partner country. Over 150 films from 86 countries will be screened.
  • It is organised by the Directorate of Film Festivals/NFDC (Ministry of I&B) with the Goa government. Goa has been its permanent venue since 2004.
Defence & Security

Sea Vigil-26 coastal defence exercise under way

  • The 5th edition of this biennial pan-India coastal defence exercise runs from 11 to 19 November 2026, with the Indian Navy as the lead agency.
  • The Coast Guard, coastal police, Customs, fisheries departments and coastal communities are taking part. The sea phase is on 18–19 November.
  • The exercise was first held in January 2019, drawing on the lessons of the 26/11 Mumbai attacks.
Important Days

14 November: Children's Day and World Diabetes Day

  • Children's Day marks the birth anniversary of Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru (born 1889, Allahabad). Universal Children's Day (UN) is on 20 November.
  • World Diabetes Day is on the birthday of Frederick Banting, co-discoverer of insulin. It was started by the IDF and WHO in 1991 and became a UN Day in 2006.
  • In Rajasthan, the Panchayat Samiti and Zila Parishad members' vote for the 4th and final phase also took place today (see Rajasthan Specific).

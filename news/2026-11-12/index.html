RAS News Digest — 12 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 12 November 2026.

Economy / Social Justice

Stand-Up India scheme extended till 31 March 2031

  • The Union Cabinet (11 Nov) approved continuation for five more years.
  • Credit guarantee corpus of ₹4,111 crore; target of at least 2.5 lakh additional SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
  • The scheme supports bank loans for greenfield enterprises by SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
Economy / Employment

PLFS (Jul–Sep 2026): unemployment rate falls to 5.1%

  • MoSPI's quarterly bulletin (released 10 Nov) shows unemployment (age 15+) at 5.1%, down from 5.4%.
  • Labour Force Participation Rate 55.5%; Worker Population Ratio 52.7%.
  • Rural unemployment 4.3% versus urban 6.8%.
Science & Technology / Rankings

Global Innovation Index 2026: India ranked 38th

  • WIPO's 19th GII ranks 139 economies; India scored 40.1.
  • Switzerland, Sweden and the USA are the top three.
  • Theme: 'Powering Entrepreneurs at the Frontier of Science'.
International Relations / Energy

India re-elected President of the International Solar Alliance for 2026–28

  • Announced at the 9th ISA Assembly in New Delhi; France is Co-President.
  • The Bahamas became the 116th member of the ISA on 4 November 2026.
  • ISA was launched by India and France in 2015 at COP21, Paris; its headquarters is in Gurugram.

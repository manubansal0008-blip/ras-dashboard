RAS News Digest — 13 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 13 July 2026.

International Relations

Iran strikes West Asian states after U.S. attack; declares Strait of Hormuz closed

  • After the U.S. struck Iran early on Sunday — a strike on a container ship in the Strait of Hormuz left one Indian sailor missing — Iran retaliated with attacks on several West Asian states hosting U.S. forces: Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Jordan and Oman.
  • Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz 'closed'; the U.S. and its Central Command (CENTCOM) insisted traffic was still flowing and that it alone must control the strait.
  • The Strait of Hormuz — between Iran and Oman, linking the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman — is the world's most important oil chokepoint, carrying about a fifth of global petroleum-liquids trade.

Source: Ministry of External Affairs / press, July 2026

International Relations / Agriculture

Taliban administration seeks deeper farm ties with India

  • The Taliban administration of Afghanistan expressed a desire to deepen agricultural cooperation with India — covering agri-trade, infrastructure, irrigation, food processing and climate-resilient, bio-fortified crops — during a six-day visit by its Agriculture, Irrigation and Livestock Minister.
  • The Afghan delegation met Union Ministers (Agriculture, Food Processing) and institutions including NABARD, the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) and the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI).
  • Afghanistan is a landlocked country; India accesses it via the Chabahar port route in Iran, and has long supported its agriculture and development.

Source: Ministry of External Affairs, July 2026

Economy / Strategic Resources

China temporarily bans helium exports, spotlighting a strategic resource

  • On July 10, China's Ministry of Commerce and customs administration temporarily banned helium exports — a resource on which China depends heavily (it imports over 80% of its needs and produces only about 1.6% of the world's helium).
  • Helium is a non-renewable, non-manufacturable noble gas; the U.S. is the largest producer (about 43% of supply), followed by Qatar, Russia, Canada and Algeria.
  • With the lowest boiling point of any element (about -269°C), helium is a critical coolant for MRI machines and semiconductor fabrication, and is used to pressurise rocket fuel tanks (by ISRO, NASA, SpaceX).

Source: General Administration of Customs, China / press, July 2026

Governance / Urban

Delhi seeks Rs 100 crore to speed up PM-UDAY ownership rights for unauthorised colonies

  • The Delhi Chief Minister sought Rs 100 crore from the Centre to accelerate the PM-UDAY scheme, which provides property/ownership rights to residents of 1,511 unauthorised colonies in the national capital.
  • PM-UDAY (Pradhan Mantri Unauthorised Colonies in Delhi Awas Adhikar Yojana), launched in 2019, is implemented through the Delhi Development Authority (DDA).
  • It aims to regularise long-standing unauthorised colonies and give residents a recognised legal title to their homes.

Source: Delhi Development Authority / PM-UDAY, July 2026

Polity / Local Governance

Debate over District Development Councils and local democracy in J&K

  • The District Development Councils (DDCs) — a directly elected tier introduced in Jammu & Kashmir in 2020 — completed their five-year term in February 2026 with no fresh elections held, reviving debate on their role.
  • Critics argue the DDCs function as a parallel administrative authority that bypasses the panchayat/municipal system envisaged by the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments.
  • The 73rd and 74th Amendments (1992) created a three-tier rural Panchayati Raj and urban local bodies; the District Planning Committee under Article 243ZD is meant to coordinate their plans.

Source: The Hindu explainer / static polity, July 2026

Polity / Judiciary

CJI stresses the need for women-centric infrastructure in judicial buildings

  • The Chief Justice of India stressed the need for women-centric infrastructure — such as safe, accessible facilities for women litigants, lawyers and staff — in court buildings across the country.
  • The push aligns with wider efforts to make courts more accessible and inclusive, including for women's participation in the legal profession.
  • It also connects to concerns over judicial infrastructure and case pendency, with over 5 crore cases pending across Indian courts.

Source: Supreme Court of India, July 2026

Science & Technology / Space

ISRO successfully carries out tests of Gaganyaan systems

  • ISRO successfully carried out tests of systems for the Gaganyaan mission — India's first human spaceflight programme — moving it closer to a crewed launch.
  • Gaganyaan aims to send a crew of astronauts ('gaganyatris') to a low-Earth orbit of about 400 km for up to three days and bring them back safely.
  • It uses the human-rated LVM3 launch vehicle and an Orbital Module comprising a Crew Module and a Service Module, with a series of abort and recovery tests preceding the crewed flight.

Source: ISRO, July 2026

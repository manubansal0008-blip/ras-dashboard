RAS News Digest — 07 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 07 July 2026.

Awards & Recognition

President confers Padma Awards 2026; Dharmendra (posthumous) & N. Rajam get Padma Vibhushan

  • The President conferred the Padma Awards 2026 at Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi — 2 Padma Vibhushan, 6 Padma Bhushan and 58 Padma Shri, taking the list to 66.
  • Actor and former MP Dharmendra Singh Deol (posthumous) and violin maestro Dr. (Smt.) N. Rajam — famed for the 'Singing Violin' — received the Padma Vibhushan.
  • Rajasthan figured prominently in the Padma Shri list: Algoza folk musician Taga Ram Bheel (Jaisalmer), Swami Brahamdev (Sri Ganganagar) and Bhapang exponent Gafaruddin Mewati Jogi (Deeg/Bharatpur).

Source: Padma Awards 2026, Rashtrapati Bhavan / PIB

Defence & Security

INS Mahendragiri, sixth indigenous Project 17A stealth frigate, to be commissioned on July 11

  • The Indian Navy will commission INS Mahendragiri (F38), the sixth Project 17A stealth frigate, at Visakhapatnam on 11 July.
  • Designed by the Navy's Warship Design Bureau and built by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd, Mumbai, it has over 75% indigenous content.
  • Named after a peak in the Eastern Ghats, it carries advanced stealth features and surface-to-surface and surface-to-air missile systems.

Source: The Indian Express, 7 July 2026

Economy & Environment

E20 becomes the standard petrol nationwide, five years ahead of the 2030 target

  • India has transitioned to 20% ethanol-blended petrol (E20 — 80% petrol, 20% ethanol) as the standard fuel nationwide, five years ahead of the 2030 target.
  • The government plans to move towards E25 and introduce E85 fuel for flex-fuel vehicles; the Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) studied E20's impact on engine materials.
  • Higher ethanol lowers carbon emissions but has a lower calorific value, causing a mileage drop that consumers ultimately bear.

Source: The Indian Express, 7 July 2026

International Relations

Questions over Quad's future as US reverses Indo-Pacific Command to Pacific Command

  • Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi visited New Delhi for the 16th India-Japan Annual Summit, reaffirming a free and open Indo-Pacific.
  • On 16 June, the Pentagon renamed the Indo-Pacific Command back to the U.S. Pacific Command, reversing a change made in 2018.
  • Analysts debate the Quad's relevance; its cooperation spans security, supply-chain resilience, critical minerals and health.

Source: The Hindu, 7 July 2026

Modern History

Nation marks 125th birth anniversary of Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee

  • Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee's birth anniversary is observed on 6 July; he was born in 1901 in Kolkata.
  • An educationist, he became the youngest Vice-Chancellor of Calcutta University at the age of 33 and was independent India's first Minister for Industry and Supply.
  • He founded the Bharatiya Jana Sangh in 1951 and opposed separate provisions for Jammu & Kashmir with the slogan 'Ek desh mein do vidhan, do nishan, do pradhan nahi chalenge.'

Source: Dainik Bhaskar, 7 July 2026

Sports

Australia win Women's T20 World Cup for 7th time, beat England in final

  • Australia won the Women's T20 World Cup, defeating England by 7 wickets in the final.
  • It is Australia's 7th Women's T20 World Cup title, extending their long-standing dominance in the format.
  • The victory underlines Australia's status as the most successful side in Women's T20 World Cup history.

Source: Dainik Bhaskar, 7 July 2026

National / Internal Security

Two Assam Rifles personnel killed in militant ambush in Manipur's Ukhrul

  • Two Assam Rifles personnel were killed in an ambush by suspected militants in Ukhrul district, Manipur.
  • They were part of a 40 Assam Rifles convoy attacked on National Highway-202 near Nungshong Kong village.
  • It is the fourth fatal ambush so far this year in the conflict-hit state.

Source: The Indian Express, 7 July 2026

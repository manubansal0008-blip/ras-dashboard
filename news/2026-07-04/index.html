Rajasthan / Economy

PM dedicates HPCL Rajasthan Refinery at Pachpadra

  • PM Modi dedicated the greenfield HPCL Rajasthan Refinery (HRRL) at Pachpadra, Balotra — India's first integrated refinery-cum-petrochemical complex and its 23rd refinery.
  • Capacity 9 MMTPA refining + 2.4 MMTPA petrochemicals; investment about Rs 79,459 crore; Nelson Complexity Index 17.0 (highest in India).
  • Part of a ~Rs 1.05 lakh crore package: also the Yamuna water project MoA, Jaipur Metro Phase-2 foundation and 50,000+ job appointment letters.
  • Expected to give Rajasthan ~Rs 21,000 crore a year and anchor a Petrozone and plastic park.

Source: Rajasthan Patrika, 4 Jul 2026

International Relations

Indus Waters Treaty stays 'in abeyance'; India rejects talks

  • India reiterated the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty (IWT) remains in abeyance until Pakistan credibly ends cross-border terrorism.
  • India placed it in abeyance after the April 2026 Pahalgam terror attack; the MEA called Pakistan's stance the 'weaponisation of water'.
  • The World Bank-brokered treaty gives the eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India and the western rivers (Indus, Chenab, Jhelum) to Pakistan.
  • Pakistan called the move 'baseless'; India says talks depend on Pakistan abjuring terrorism.

Source: Times of India, 4 Jul 2026

Economy / Governance

Four China-linked firms exempted from Press Note 3 for power bids

  • The Finance Ministry exempted four China-linked firms (TBEA Energy, Nanjing Electric India, New Northeast Electric India, Taikai Electric India) from Press Note 3 (2020) for government power procurement.
  • PN3 requires prior clearance for bidders from land-bordering nations; it was brought in after the 2020 Galwan (LAC) clash.
  • The two-year exemption was cleared by a Committee of Secretaries on the DPIIT panel's recommendation; the firms run transmission units in India.
  • The Ministry said it should not be treated as a precedent.

Source: Indian Express, 4 Jul 2026

Science & Technology / Security

Govt orders Apple, Google to pull 7 apps misused to switch off e-rickshaws

  • MeitY directed Apple and Google to remove seven China-origin battery-management apps (e.g., BAT-BMS, Lossigy, Smart BMS) misused to remotely disable e-rickshaws.
  • The apps can switch off a vehicle's battery-management system via Bluetooth even while moving, a safety and cybersecurity risk.
  • BAT-BMS was originally built by China's Shenzhen Grenergy Technology.
  • The government indicated it may also draft a separate law to regulate AI.

Source: Indian Express / Times of India, 4 Jul 2026

Health / Governance

Centre bans 16 fixed-dose combination (FDC) drugs

  • The Government banned 16 FDC drugs — mainly antibiotic combinations and certain dermatological/herbal products — whose claimed benefits lack scientific justification.
  • Irrational FDCs can raise antimicrobial resistance, adverse effects and drug interactions.
  • India has reviewed thousands of FDCs through an expert committee since 2016.
  • ICMR scientist Dr Kamini Walia was cited on the AMR risks.

Source: Indian Express, 4 Jul 2026

Environment & Ecology

Centre unveils roadmap to revive struggling tiger reserves

  • Marking 18 years of tiger reintroduction at Sariska (Alwar, Rajasthan), the Centre unveiled a plan to consolidate 'source populations' and strengthen weak reserves.
  • India's tigers rose from 1,411 (2006) to 3,682 (2022); 58 of ~110 reserves hold about 36% of tigers.
  • 12 reserves have under 3 tigers; three (Kawal, Kamlang, Dampa) have none.
  • Sariska lost all its tigers by 2008 before reintroduction; Panna (MP) followed in 2009.

Source: Indian Express, 4 Jul 2026

International Relations

Iran begins funeral week; Khamenei lies in state in Tehran

  • Ayatollah Ali Khamenei's body lay in state in Tehran as Iran began a week of mass funeral processions; burial is set for Mashhad on 9 July.
  • His decades-long tenure ended in an Israeli strike during the war that began 28 February 2026.
  • An Indian delegation — Bihar Governor (Retd) Syed Ata Hasnain and MoS External Affairs Pabitra Margherita — paid tribute.
  • The delegation included Hindu, Sikh, Muslim and Christian leaders.

Source: Times of India / Indian Express, 4 Jul 2026

RAS News Digest — Today's News — 6 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated Today's News — 6 July 2026.

Rajasthan / Governance

PM Modi dedicates projects worth over Rs 1.05 lakh crore to Rajasthan; 53,977 appointment letters given

  • On 5 July 2026 PM Narendra Modi dedicated/laid foundation for projects worth over Rs 1,05,000 crore in Rajasthan from Pachpadra (Balotra/Barmer).
  • Key items: the Rajasthan Refinery (HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Ltd, cost over Rs 79,000 crore) dedicated; foundation of Jaipur Metro Phase-2 (over Rs 13,000 crore); new terminal at Jodhpur Airport; and an MoA on the Yamuna Water (Kishau dam) project.
  • Seven energy, road and railway projects worth Rs 13,443 crore were also part of the package.
  • A simultaneous state-wide 'District-Level Employment Festival' distributed government appointment letters to 53,977 youth (e.g., Jaipur 5,852; Sikar 2,505; Dausa 2,405).

Source: Times of India / Dainik Navjyoti, 6 July 2026

Economy / Energy

OPEC+ clears 188,000 bpd output hike for August; India moves to diversify oil and gas sourcing

  • OPEC+ approved a production increase of 188,000 barrels per day (bpd) for August 2026 — the fifth such hike since the recent West Asia conflict.
  • India imports nearly 90% of its crude oil; state oil firms plan to double LPG imports from the US and are exploring new suppliers such as Algeria.
  • Brent crude eased near $72 a barrel while the Indian basket softened to about $67-68 a barrel.
  • Lower prices could let oil marketing companies break even on petrol and diesel within about ten days if the trend holds.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

Economy / Financial Regulation

RBI Financial Stability Report flags rising early exits from life insurance, points to mis-selling

  • The RBI's Financial Stability Report (FSR) flagged a rise in early surrenders and withdrawals from life-insurance policies, signalling mis-selling risk.
  • Total benefits paid by life insurers rose from about Rs 5 lakh crore (2021-22) to Rs 7.3 lakh crore (2025-26), a 16.1% jump over FY25.
  • Private insurers' commission ratio has nearly doubled in four years; surrenders/withdrawals now form about 38.3% of total pay-outs.
  • The RBI publishes the FSR twice a year; it also carries the Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC) sub-committee's assessment of systemic risks.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

Polity / Elections

Special Intensive Revision removes over 20 lakh names from Odisha electoral rolls

  • The Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in Odisha removed over 20 lakh names from a pre-revision base of about 3.3 crore electors.
  • Of these, roughly 8 lakh were deceased, about 10 lakh had shifted, about 1 lakh (0.5%) were enrolled at multiple places, and around 14,000 were removed for other reasons.
  • The revision drive was conducted between 30 May and 28 June 2026, followed by publication of the draft roll.
  • The exercise triggered political friction, with the ruling and opposition parties differing over its accuracy; the Supreme Court has broadly endorsed the SIR process.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

Science & Technology

Japan's Hayabusa2 probe skims near-Earth asteroid Torifune in planetary-defence test

  • Japan's space probe Hayabusa2 (of JAXA, the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency) flew past a near-Earth asteroid named Torifune in a planetary-defence technology test.
  • The probe passed within about 860 metres of the roughly 160-metre-wide asteroid while travelling at about 18,000 km/h.
  • It builds on JAXA's earlier work and on NASA's 2022 DART mission, which deliberately struck the asteroid Dimorphos to alter its orbit — the first demonstration of asteroid deflection.
  • Planetary defence aims to detect and deflect asteroids that could threaten Earth, using techniques such as the kinetic impactor.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

International Relations

Next round of US-Iran nuclear talks likely in Islamabad; focus on Strait of Hormuz

  • Islamabad is emerging as the likely venue for the next round of US-Iran negotiations, expected around 11 July 2026.
  • The two sides signed a Memorandum of Understanding on 18 June 2026 to restore peace in West Asia; earlier technical talks were held in Switzerland with Pakistan and Qatar as mediators.
  • Talks are to cover Iran's nuclear programme, sanctions relief, frozen Iranian assets and the stability of the Strait of Hormuz.
  • The Strait of Hormuz, between Iran and Oman, is the world's most important oil chokepoint, carrying a large share of seaborne crude and LNG.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

Governance / IT & Child Safety

MeitY issues 'zero tolerance' notice to Meta over child-abuse ads on Instagram

  • The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued a notice to Meta to immediately disable paid advertisements and content on Instagram promoting access to child sexual exploitation and abuse material (CSEAM).
  • Meta was given seven days to explain how such advertisements were allowed and what pre-publication checks were carried out.
  • The government stressed a 'zero tolerance' stance; reports said the ads misused leaked personal data such as names, phone numbers and identity details.
  • The action falls under the intermediary due-diligence framework of the IT Act and IT Rules, which require platforms to remove such unlawful content promptly.

Source: Times of India, 6 July 2026

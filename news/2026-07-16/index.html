RAS News Digest — 16 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 16 July 2026.

International Relations

US and Iran return to conflict over the Strait of Hormuz; Iran threatens a regional oil blockade

  • Weeks after an interim peace understanding, the US and Iran launched fresh strikes across West Asia over shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards threatened to halt all oil and gas exports from the region — "for everyone or for no one".
  • Brent crude traded near $85 a barrel, more than 15% above the pre-war level but below the near-$120 conflict peak.
  • India, dependent on Gulf energy and trade corridors, has urged restraint; it had co-sponsored UN Security Council Resolution 2817 (2026) condemning attacks on Gulf shipping.

Source: Ministry of External Affairs / UN Security Council, July 2026

Economy & Technology

Cabinet clears 'Semicon 2.0', a urea self-reliance policy and a mobile-manufacturing scheme

  • The Union Cabinet approved a package of key projects worth about Rs 2.19 lakh crore to boost manufacturing and self-reliance.
  • The centrepiece is 'Semicon 2.0', a roughly Rs 1.27 lakh crore push to deepen India's semiconductor ecosystem under the India Semiconductor Mission.
  • A new urea policy aims at self-sufficiency in the fertilizer, alongside a scheme to expand mobile-phone component manufacturing.
  • The moves extend the government's Make-in-India and Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) strategy in strategic, import-heavy sectors.

Source: Union Cabinet / PIB, July 2026

Economy

India's June trade: deficit widens on costlier imports, but merchandise exports stay strong

  • India's June trade deficit widened sharply, driven mainly by higher-value imports of crude oil, gold, fertilizers and electronic goods.
  • Crude oil import value rose about 40% and fertilizer imports over 200% by value, reflecting the West Asia crisis and a squeeze on gas supplies.
  • Merchandise exports still grew 15.5% in June (16% in the first quarter), with non-petroleum exports up a robust 16.5%.
  • Services exports grew more slowly; the Chief Economic Adviser warned against complacency in areas like Global Capability Centres.

Source: Ministry of Commerce & Industry, July 2026

Polity & Governance

NDA renews push for a two-thirds majority on the Delimitation Bill

  • The ruling alliance has renewed efforts to secure the two-thirds majority needed to advance a Bill linked to delimitation.
  • Delimitation is the redrawing of Lok Sabha and Assembly constituency boundaries based on the latest census population.
  • It is overseen by an independent Delimitation Commission, and Articles 82 and 170 of the Constitution govern the exercise.
  • The number of Lok Sabha seats was frozen by the 84th Amendment until the first census after 2026, making the issue politically sensitive between States.

Source: Government of India / Parliament, July 2026

Science, Tech & Security

Reported data leak at Kudankulam nuclear plant sparks a security scare

  • A ransomware group reportedly accessed thousands of files from a contractor's server linked to the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant (KKNPP).
  • The Nuclear Power Corporation of India Ltd (NPCIL) said the information relates to "conventional balance-of-plant" facilities and not to any nuclear safety or security systems.
  • KKNPP runs two 1,000 MWe VVER reactors built with Russian collaboration and is constructing four more units.
  • The Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) and NPCIL are investigating, underscoring cyber risks to critical infrastructure.

Source: NPCIL / Department of Atomic Energy, July 2026

Rajasthan — Education

Classroom plaster collapses in a Rajasthan government school; students injured

  • Plaster from the ceiling of a dilapidated classroom fell during class at a government senior secondary school in Heerawala, Bassi block (Jaipur district).
  • A boy and a girl were injured and taken to the community health centre; the building had reportedly been flagged as unsafe earlier.
  • The incident revives concern over ageing, unsafe government-school buildings and the state of school infrastructure.
  • Safety audits, timely repair of condemned buildings and adequate maintenance funds are the recurring demands after such accidents.

Source: Rajasthan School Education Department, July 2026

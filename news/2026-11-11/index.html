RAS News Digest — 11 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 11 November 2026.

Infrastructure / Urban

Cabinet approves Noida Metro Aqua Line extension

  • Approved on 11 November 2026: Sector-51 to Greater Noida Sector-04, 8.449 km with 5 elevated stations.
  • Estimated cost about ₹2,390 crore.
  • Interchange with Delhi Metro's Blue Line at Sector-61; total Aqua Line network becomes 71.878 km.
Economy / Urban Finance

Patna Municipal Corporation lists municipal bonds on NSE — a first for Bihar

  • Raised ₹199.99 crore for 10 years at a 9.20% coupon; rating AA (Stable).
  • Funds will go to 15 urban redevelopment projects.
  • Municipal bonds help cities borrow from markets instead of depending only on state grants.
Economy / Insurance

IRDAI forms working groups to align risk-based capital with Ind AS

  • Two working groups were set up on 10 November 2026 — for life and non-life insurers.
  • They have one month to prepare an exposure draft.
  • Risk-based capital links the capital an insurer must hold to the risks it carries.

RAS News Digest — 15 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 15 July 2026.

Economy

Retail inflation climbs to a 17-month high, crosses 4%; RBI rate cut hopes fade

  • India's retail (CPI) inflation rose to its highest level in 17 months, moving above the 4% mark.
  • The Reserve Bank may consider raising rates at its August review; home and auto loans could turn costlier.
  • Prices of potato, ginger and vehicles rose, while gold and silver jewellery became cheaper.
  • Under the flexible inflation-targeting framework, the RBI must keep CPI inflation at 4% within a band of +/- 2%.

Source: RBI / Ministry of Statistics (CPI), July 2026

International Relations

Power shift in Iran: Khamenei's grandson Ali emerges as a rising figure

  • Amid uncertainty over the succession to Iran's Supreme Leader, Ali Khamenei — a grandson of Ayatollah Khamenei — is being cited as a rising figure with the backing of hardline factions.
  • Iran's Supreme Leader holds ultimate authority over the state, the armed forces and the judiciary.
  • US President Donald Trump threatened a 20% toll on ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz, a chokepoint for global oil and gas.
  • Roughly a fifth of the world's traded oil moves through the Strait of Hormuz, so any friction there feeds energy-price volatility.

Source: International agencies, July 2026

Rajasthan — Polity

Rajasthan Assembly marks 75 years; the Amrit Mahotsav opens amid falling sittings

  • The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly began its 75-year Amrit Mahotsav, honouring former MLAs and the state's democratic journey.
  • The number of sittings has fallen sharply — from 303 in the first Assembly (1952) to around 147 in recent terms; the current (16th) Assembly has met about 84 times so far.
  • Landmark laws to be recalled include the Jagir/land-reform Acts (1952), Panchayat Samiti & Zila Parishad Act (1959), the Lokayukt Act (1973) and the Public Services Guarantee Act (2011).
  • Rajasthan is credited as a pioneer of the Right to Information movement, before the central RTI Act of 2005.

Source: Rajasthan Vidhan Sabha, July 2026

Rajasthan — Health

FCM anaemia injection missing from Rajasthan government hospitals for five months

  • Ferric Carboxymaltose (FCM), a modern iron-replacement injection used to treat severe anaemia in pregnant women, has been unavailable in the state's government hospitals for about five months.
  • With the injection costing roughly Rs 2,500-6,000 in the open market, poor women are the worst hit; a maternal death was reported from Bikaner.
  • Anaemia among pregnant women in Rajasthan remains very high, making a reliable public supply of iron therapy critical.
  • The Medical Minister said a five-day campaign with a strict monitoring SOP would begin across the state.

Source: Rajasthan Medical & Health Department, July 2026

Rajasthan — Governance

CAG flags 29 kg gold and 972 kg silver lying idle in Rajasthan's treasury

  • The Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) found about 29 kg of gold and 972 kg of silver — worth roughly Rs 64.44 crore — locked in the government treasury, and recommended it be auctioned.
  • The valuables, largely seized from criminals, have not been disposed of; the CAG flagged delays under the Rajasthan Treasury Rules, 2012.
  • The report also noted that over 35 years the government could not account for about Rs 29.79 crore across 17 cases.
  • The CAG is a constitutional authority (Article 148) that audits the accounts of the Union and the States.

Source: CAG report / Rajasthan, July 2026

Society & Skilling

World Youth Skills Day (15 July) puts the spotlight on employable skills

  • World Youth Skills Day is observed on 15 July every year, declared by the UN General Assembly in 2014 to stress skilling young people for jobs and entrepreneurship.
  • Rajasthan's Skill, Employment & Entrepreneurship Department marked the day around the theme of building the state's identity through skills.
  • Skilling links directly to the demographic dividend — a large working-age population is an asset only if it is employable.
  • Key national platforms include the Skill India Mission, PMKVY, ITIs and the National Skill Development Corporation.

Source: UN / Ministry of Skill Development, July 2026

Education

College enrolment in Rajasthan falls up to 4.23% — a second year of negative growth

  • Enrolment in state colleges fell by up to 4.23%, the second consecutive year of negative growth, as more youth are drawn to gig work over classrooms.
  • The decline is sharpest in arts and law-and-science streams; the Udaipur division bucked the trend with high enrolment.
  • Falling enrolment can pull down the Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER), a key higher-education indicator.
  • The trend raises questions about course relevance, campus jobs linkage and the pull of short-term earning over degrees.

Source: Rajasthan Higher Education Department, July 2026

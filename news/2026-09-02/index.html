RAS News Digest — 2 September 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 2 September 2026.

Economy

Q1 FY27 GDP grows 7.8% on new 2022-23 base

  • NSO estimated real GDP for April–June 2026 at ₹81.36 lakh crore, up 7.8% (6.9% a year earlier).
  • Nominal GDP rose 10.3% to ₹88.27 lakh crore.
  • It is the first quarterly release on the revised National Accounts series with base year 2022-23.
Federalism

Bihar and Jharkhand settle Sone water dispute

  • An MoU signed on 31 August 2026 ended a dispute running since Jharkhand's creation in 2000.
  • Of the 7.75 MAF allotted to undivided Bihar under the 1973 Bansagar Agreement, Bihar gets 5.75 MAF and Jharkhand 2 MAF.
  • Sone is a right-bank tributary of the Ganga rising in the Amarkantak region.
Environment

UNCCD COP17 held at Ulaanbaatar

  • COP17 of the UN Convention to Combat Desertification met in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, from 17 to 28 August 2026.
  • Its theme was 'Restoring Land. Restoring Hope.'
  • Egypt will host COP18 in 2028 at Sharm El-Sheikh.

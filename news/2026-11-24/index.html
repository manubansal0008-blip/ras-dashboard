RAS News Digest — 24 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 24 November 2026.

International Relations

India–Nepal: ₹15,000 crore package and a new vision document after PM Balendra Shah's visit

  • Nepal PM Balendra Shah made his first bilateral visit abroad to India on 22–23 November 2026.
  • India announced ₹15,000 crore: a ₹3,500 crore grant for post-flood reconstruction and an ₹11,500 crore rupee Line of Credit for energy projects.
  • The Sarayu–Kamala Express between Ayodhya and Janakpur was launched; foundation stones were laid for the Banbasa Integrated Check Post and the Jhapa terminal of the Siliguri–Jhapa pipeline.
  • Nepal's transit cargo will get access to Deendayal (Kandla) and Paradip ports, and Nepal is to get an offshore IIT campus.
  • The vision document is titled 'Viksit Bharat and Samunnat Nepal: Re-imagining the Partnership'.
Polity

Monsoon Session not prorogued for 100+ days; Winter Session may be skipped

  • The Monsoon Session of Parliament has not been prorogued for a record of over 100 days.
  • The Winter Session has been skipped only four times: during the Emergency, after the early dissolution of the Sixth Lok Sabha, after Indira Gandhi's assassination, and during COVID-19.
  • Under Article 85, six months must not pass between the last sitting of one session and the first sitting of the next.
  • Ordinances under Article 123 can be issued only when the Houses are not in session, so non-prorogation blocks ordinances.
Environment

COP31 in Antalya ends without a cover text

  • About 190 countries at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, failed to agree on an overarching political 'cover text'.
  • The Presidency (Türkiye, with Australia leading negotiations) proposed that electricity supply 35% of global final energy consumption by 2035.
  • The European Union objected because the text did not explicitly mention fossil fuels.
  • India insisted that developed countries honour their climate-finance commitments.
Economy

RBI retains SBI, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank as D-SIBs in 2026

  • RBI's 2026 list keeps SBI, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank as Domestic Systemically Important Banks in the same buckets as 2025.
  • SBI is in bucket 4 (additional CET1 of 0.80% of risk-weighted assets), HDFC Bank in bucket 2 (0.40%) and ICICI Bank in bucket 1 (0.20%).
  • SBI was named a D-SIB in 2015, ICICI Bank in 2016 and HDFC Bank in 2017.
  • IRDAI has set up an expert committee to review proposed reforms on insurance distribution and commissions.
Governance

Census 2027: self-enumeration for Phase II open till 30 November

  • Self-enumeration for Population Enumeration is open on se.census.gov.in till 30 November in Goa, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and non-snow-bound Uttarakhand.
  • Residents answer 40 questions and receive a self-enumeration ID; house-to-house enumeration runs from 1 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.
  • Enumerators in Uttar Pradesh report people refusing to share OTPs and doubting the question on bank accounts.
  • Census 2027 is India's first digital Census and the first since Independence to collect caste data.
Health

Over 1 crore HPV vaccine doses; ICMR's SAMPADA survey finds lower anaemia

  • India has given over 1 crore HPV vaccine doses in under nine months of the national campaign.
  • The campaign was launched from Ajmer, Rajasthan, on 28 February 2026 and gives a free single dose of Gardasil-4 to about 1.15 crore girls aged 14 each year.
  • ICMR's SAMPADA survey (earlier DABS-I) used venous blood samples across 183 districts and found lower anaemia prevalence than earlier national surveys.
  • India's first dedicated Human Metabolic Chamber facility opened at CMC Vellore.
Economy & Infrastructure

Coal rule changes, barrier-free tolling gains and MoSPI's new data series

  • The Coal Ministry amended the Mineral Concession Rules, 1960 to extend accreditation of prospecting and mining-plan agencies from three to five years.
  • It also issued Guidelines for Mining Plan and Mine Closure Plan for Coal and Lignite Mines, 2026.
  • NHAI's Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF) barrier-less tolling raised net toll revenue by 19.84% at operational plazas; Rajasthan's Daulatpura plaza on NH-48 uses it.
  • MoSPI explained the new series of GDP (base 2022-23), CPI (base 2024) and IIP (base 2022-23) at a data users' conference in Mumbai.
Social Sector

Nayi Chetna 5.0 from 25 November; Yuva Samvad on Constitution Day

  • Nayi Chetna 5.0, the DAY-NRLM campaign against gender-based violence, launches on 25 November, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women.
  • The campaign is run through women's self-help groups and began in 2022.
  • MY Bharat will hold a Yuva Samvad on Constitution Day (26 November) in over 600 districts on the theme 'Viksit Bharat@2047'.
  • Constitution Day has been observed since 2015 to mark the adoption of the Constitution on 26 November 1949.
Sports

Gukesh begins World Chess Championship defence against Sindarov in Geneva

  • D. Gukesh (India) defends his world title against Javokhir Sindarov (Uzbekistan) in Geneva, Switzerland, with Game 1 on 24 November.
  • The match has 14 classical games, and both players are 20, making it the youngest title match in history.
  • Gukesh became the 18th world champion in Singapore in 2024; Sindarov won the 2025 FIDE World Cup in Goa and the 2026 Candidates in Cyprus.
Mapping

Strait of Hormuz: Iran vows to counter the U.S. counter-blockade

  • Iran said it would use all available means to counter a U.S. naval blockade of its ports.
  • The Strait of Hormuz lies between Iran and Oman's Musandam peninsula and links the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman.
  • It is the world's most important oil chokepoint and a key route for India's crude imports; Air India has suspended Gulf flights till 30 November.

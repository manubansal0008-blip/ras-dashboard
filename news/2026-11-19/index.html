RAS News Digest — 19 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 19 November 2026.

Internal Security — Narcotics

PM calls for a 'united and sustained' drive against drugs; NCB targets illicit cannabis and opium farms

  • At a Council of Ministers meeting, PM Narendra Modi called the narcotics trade a 'social evil' and sought a united, sustained government effort against it, saying that if left-wing extremism can be ended, so can drugs.
  • The Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), with State police and other agencies, has run operations against illegal cannabis and opium cultivation in Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Rajasthan and Jharkhand.
  • Aim: to eliminate illicit cannabis and poppy cultivation in identified areas by December 2027, under the Union Home Ministry's 'Vision Document on Narcotics Control (2026-2029)'.
  • Provisions: mapping of hotspots, coordinated destruction drives through a whole-of-government approach, and training of security personnel to identify cultivation sites.
  • Ministry concerned: Ministry of Home Affairs (NCB); challenges include remote forest and hill sites, cross-border supply and the livelihood needs of growers.
  • Static GK: Drug offences are governed by the NDPS Act, 1985; the NCB was set up in 1986; the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan began in 2020.
Geography — Cyclone

Cyclone Akvan changes course; landfall expected between Narsapur and Puri on 20 November

  • Severe cyclonic storm Akvan has changed its track and is expected to make landfall between Narsapur (Andhra Pradesh) and Puri (Odisha) on 20 November.
  • North Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha have been put on alert because the track kept shifting.
  • Experts link the uncertainty to El Niño conditions and mid-level moisture, and say the system may weaken before landfall.
  • Static GK: The IMD names a severe cyclonic storm when winds reach 89–117 km/h; cyclone names in the north Indian Ocean come from a list prepared by 13 WMO/ESCAP member countries.
Defence

₹1,976 crore contract with Israel to upgrade Navy's Barak missile system

  • The Defence Ministry signed a ₹1,976.40 crore contract with Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI) on 18 November.
  • It covers the upgrade and obsolescence management of the Barak point-defence missile system on Indian Navy warships.
  • The contract was signed under the 'Other Capital Procurement' procedure in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh.
  • Static GK: Barak is a ship-borne surface-to-air missile system used for close-in air defence of warships.
Infrastructure — Rail

First bullet-train section, Surat–Bilimora, to be energised on 20 November

  • The first section of the Mumbai–Ahmedabad bullet train project, between Surat and Bilimora, will be energised on 20 November 2026.
  • Energising means switching on live current in the overhead equipment on the 50-km elevated stretch.
  • The corridor uses a 2×25 kV overhead traction system with 14 traction substations along its 508 km.
  • Static GK: The project is executed by the National High-Speed Rail Corporation Ltd (NHSRCL) with Japanese Shinkansen technology and JICA financing.
Economy — Energy & Prices

October crude import bill jumps 87.5%; food inflation hits 8.9%

  • India's crude oil import bill rose 87.5% year-on-year to $19.5 billion in October, as West Asia tensions pushed up global prices.
  • Crude imports rose to 23.3 million tonnes from 21 million tonnes a year ago, and the net oil-and-gas import bill rose 60% to $16 billion.
  • Food inflation climbed to 8.9% in October, the highest in the new CPI series, and the average cost of a thali rose about 16% in ten months.
  • The rupee touched a seven-month high of 93.28 per dollar before settling at 93.50.
  • Static GK: Oil data are released by the Petroleum Planning and Analysis Cell (PPAC) of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; India imports over 85% of its crude oil.
Health — Schemes

Jan Aushadhi network crosses 20,500 outlets; experts urge wider reach

  • Aim: Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) makes quality generic medicines available at low prices to cut out-of-pocket health spending.
  • Provisions: its basket covers 2,110 medicines and 315 surgical items; outlets grew to 20,547 by July 2026 from 17,990 in December 2025, against a target of 25,000 by March 2027.
  • Citizens saved an estimated ₹9,000 crore in 2025-26, and the Kendras receive about 10–12 lakh visitors a day.
  • Ministry concerned: Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, through the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI).
  • Challenges: low awareness, doubts about quality, few doctors prescribing generic names, and a small share compared with about 12 lakh chemists.
  • Static GK: The scheme began in 2008, was revamped in 2015 and renamed PMBJP in 2016; Jan Aushadhi Diwas is observed on 7 March.
Art & Culture — Music

49 years in space: Kesarbai Kerkar's raga travels on with Voyager 1

  • As Voyager 1 crossed one light-day from Earth, attention returned to the Indian music it carries on the 'Golden Record'.
  • The record includes 'Jaat Kahan Ho', a piece in raga Bhairavi sung by Kesarbai Kerkar, about 3.5 minutes long, sent on Voyager 1 and 2 in 1977.
  • Kesarbai Kerkar (born 1892, Keri, Goa) was a leading singer of the Jaipur-Atrauli gharana and a disciple of its founder, Ustad Alladiya Khan.
  • She received the Padma Bhushan in 1969 and the title 'Surshri' in 1948.
  • Static GK: The Golden Record was curated by a committee chaired by astronomer Carl Sagan and carries sounds and music meant to represent life on Earth.
Rajasthan — Medical Education

Spate of ragging cases in medical colleges; SMS anti-ragging guidelines to apply statewide

  • After ragging and harassment of first-year MBBS students in several medical colleges since mid-October, Health Minister Gajendra Singh Khimsar ordered preventive action.
  • The anti-ragging guidelines developed by SMS Medical College, Jaipur, will be applied in all medical colleges of the State.
  • In Barmer, 80 students faced action and six were expelled from the hostel; in Jodhpur's S.N. Medical College, 19 students were suspended.
  • Colleges must activate anti-ragging committees and squads, inspect regularly, and ensure first-year students install the 'Raj Cop' police app to report incidents.
  • Static GK: Rajasthan has a Prohibition of Ragging in Educational Institutions Act, 2001; UGC anti-ragging regulations date from 2009, and the NMC has its own anti-ragging rules for medical colleges.
Rajasthan — Heritage

662 Shekhawati havelis to be conserved; CM says no heritage haveli will be demolished

  • Aim: the Shekhawati Haveli Conservation Scheme seeks to save the region's painted havelis and boost heritage tourism.
  • Provisions: more than 662 historic havelis in Sikar, Jhunjhunu and Churu districts have been identified, and a joint expert committee is to be formed.
  • Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma said not a single heritage haveli would be allowed to be demolished.
  • Department concerned: Tourism and Art & Culture departments; challenges include private ownership, neglect, absent owners and pressure for redevelopment.
  • Static GK: Shekhawati is famous for its fresco-painted havelis in towns like Mandawa, Nawalgarh, Fatehpur and Ramgarh; the havelis were recently placed on UNESCO's Tentative List.

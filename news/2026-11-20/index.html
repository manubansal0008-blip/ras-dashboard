RAS News Digest — 20 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 20 November 2026.

International Relations

U.S., China and Russia issue 'peace declaration' after first trilateral summit in Shenzhen

  • The leaders of the U.S., China and Russia — Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin — held their first-ever trilateral summit in Shenzhen on 19 November, on the sidelines of APEC.
  • They released a 'peace declaration' and agreed to start a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks.
  • The three countries said they would raise the production and supply of refined petroleum products for all nations.
  • They also said the International Criminal Court and similar bodies must not abuse their jurisdiction and stressed immunity of State officials.
  • Static GK: The ICC was set up under the Rome Statute and began work in 2002; India, the U.S., China and Russia are not its members.
International — India-Japan

India–Japan 2+2: Japanese forces to train in Indian firing ranges for the first time

  • India and Japan held their fourth 2+2 Foreign and Defence Ministers' dialogue in Tokyo on 19 November.
  • A Letter of Intent will, for the first time, allow Japan's Self-Defence Forces to use Indian test ranges for live-fire training, including surface-to-ship missiles.
  • The two sides moved forward on India receiving Japan's UNICORN (Unified Complex Radio Antenna) naval radar system.
  • They also discussed revisions in Japan's national security and defence policies.
  • Static GK: The first India–Japan 2+2 was held in New Delhi in 2019; JIMEX (navy) and Dharma Guardian (army) are bilateral exercises.
International Law

ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

  • In a landmark judgment on 19 November, the International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya during the 2017 military operation in northern Rakhine State.
  • The 15-judge court decided by 9–6 and ordered Myanmar to allow those who fled to return home.
  • ICJ President Yuji Iwasawa said Myanmar violated its obligations under the Genocide Convention, rejecting its claim that the campaign targeted insurgents.
  • The case was brought by The Gambia in 2019.
  • Static GK: The ICJ, the UN's principal judicial organ, sits at The Hague and has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms; the Genocide Convention was adopted in 1948.
Economy — Social Security

EPFO approves systematic withdrawal plan for retirees

  • Aim: to let retired members draw a regular income from their provident fund while the rest of the corpus keeps earning interest.
  • Provisions: under the Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP), retirees can take monthly, quarterly or half-yearly payouts instead of a lump sum; it starts with retirees above 55 years who have more than ₹5 lakh in their EPF.
  • Members can also withdraw only the interest, or take the full balance later; if a member dies during SWP, the family or nominee continues to get the facility, and the EPF pension is not affected.
  • For members below 55, the minimum balance is cut from 25% to 10%, so up to 90% can be withdrawn; the waiting period for the unemployed is cut from 12 months to two months.
  • Ministry concerned: Ministry of Labour and Employment; the EPFO's Central Board of Trustees, chaired by Labour Minister Mansukh Mandaviya, approved it.
  • Scale and challenges: about 8 crore active members with deposits of around ₹32 lakh crore; tax treatment of payouts and member awareness remain issues.
  • Static GK: The EPFO works under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.
Health — TB

WHO report: India still carries about a quarter of global TB; missed 2025 elimination target

  • The WHO's Global Tuberculosis Report, released on 12 November 2026, estimates that 10.6 million people developed TB worldwide in 2025.
  • India still accounts for nearly a quarter of the global TB burden.
  • India's estimated TB death rate fell 38%, from 28 per lakh in 2015 to 18 per lakh in 2025, and 25.3 lakh cases were notified in 2025.
  • India had set a target of eliminating TB by 2025, five years ahead of the global SDG target of 2030, but missed it.
  • Static GK: India's TB programme runs as the National TB Elimination Programme; Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana gives nutritional support to patients.
Disaster Management

Odisha shifts 3 lakh people as Cyclone Akvan nears landfall

  • Odisha has moved about 3 lakh people to safe shelters ahead of severe cyclonic storm Akvan.
  • The storm is expected to cross the coast between Narsapur (Andhra Pradesh) and Puri (Odisha) on 20 November.
  • North Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha districts are on alert, with disaster response teams deployed.
  • Static GK: Odisha set up the Odisha State Disaster Management Authority after the 1999 super cyclone; its 'zero casualty' approach was praised during Cyclone Phailin (2013).
Health — AMR

Health Ministry proposes treating antimicrobials as 'New Drugs' to curb AMR

  • Aim: tighter scrutiny before licences are given for antimicrobial products, to fight antimicrobial resistance (AMR).
  • Provisions: an amendment to the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, would include 'antimicrobials' in the definition of 'New Drug'; separate application and licence forms are proposed for selling bulk drugs under the Drugs Rules, 1945.
  • Ministry concerned: Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, with the drug regulator (CDSCO).
  • Challenges: over-the-counter sale of antibiotics, misuse in livestock and weak enforcement.
  • Static GK: India's National Action Plan on AMR was launched in 2017; the Kerala-led 'Red Line' campaign marks antibiotic packs that need a prescription.
Awards

Graca Machel receives the Indira Gandhi Prize for Peace, Disarmament and Development

  • Mozambican women's rights advocate Graca Machel received the 2025 Indira Gandhi Prize for Peace, Disarmament and Development on 19 November.
  • The date marked Indira Gandhi's 109th birth anniversary.
  • The award, presented by the Indira Gandhi Memorial Trust, carries a citation and ₹1 crore.
  • Static GK: Graca Machel was Mozambique's first Education Minister and is the widow of Samora Machel and Nelson Mandela; the prize was instituted in 1986.
Rajasthan — Energy

Rawatbhata: AERB clears 700 MW RAPP Unit-8 for first criticality

  • The Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB) on 19 November allowed the 700 MW Unit-8 of the Rajasthan Atomic Power Project at Rawatbhata to begin its first approach to criticality.
  • Permission came after safety reviews and inspections; criticality is a key step in commissioning the reactor.
  • After successful criticality and tests, the unit will move to commercial power generation.
  • Fuel loading in Unit-8 had begun in September 2026; it is India's fourth indigenous 700 MW pressurised heavy water reactor, after Kakrapar 3 and 4 and RAPP-7.
  • Static GK: Rawatbhata is in Chittorgarh district on the Chambal river; the plant is run by NPCIL under the Department of Atomic Energy.
Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

Panchayat polls: results for 37 zila parishads and 419 panchayat samitis today

  • Counting for Rajasthan's zila parishad and panchayat samiti members takes place on 20 November, deciding the fate of 29,177 candidates.
  • Results cover 1,275 wards in 37 zila parishads and 7,543 wards in 419 panchayat samitis — 8,818 wards in all.
  • Voting was by EVM, and results are expected by the afternoon; contests for zila pramukh and pradhan posts follow.
  • The results are seen as a test of rural support for the BJP and the Congress about two years before the Assembly elections.
  • Static GK: Rajasthan follows a three-tier Panchayati Raj system; zila pramukhs and pradhans are elected indirectly by the elected members.
Rajasthan — Governance

Rajasthan Chief Secretary V. Srinivas leads IIAS at its 2026 conference in Seoul

  • The International Institute of Administrative Sciences (IIAS) annual conference was held in Seoul, South Korea, from 17 to 20 November 2026.
  • Its theme was 'Growth for Society', focusing on collaborative governance and social innovation.
  • Rajasthan Chief Secretary V. Srinivas, a 1989-batch Rajasthan-cadre IAS officer, is the IIAS President for 2025–2028.
  • He became the first Indian elected to head the IIAS, defeating Austria's nominee in June 2025.
  • Static GK: The IIAS, founded in 1930, is headquartered in Brussels; India is a member state.
Rajasthan — Employment

PLFS: only about 16 in 100 people in Rajasthan have regular salaried jobs

  • The latest annual and quarterly Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) reports show the share of regular wage/salaried jobs in Rajasthan at about 16–18%, against a national average of 23.6%.
  • About 65% of people depend on farming, trade, wage labour and animal husbandry for a living.
  • Unemployment among youth aged 15–29 in the State is about 11–13%; among graduates and above it is 15–18%, nearly double the national average.
  • Women in villages fare better: rural female unemployment is about 3% while urban female unemployment has reached about 12.5%; 55–58% of rural women are employed against 24–27% of urban women.
  • Women earn about 30–40% less than men on average.
  • Static GK: PLFS is conducted by the National Statistics Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI); it began in 2017.
Awards & Lists

TIME releases its first country-specific list, 'TIME100 Next India'

  • TIME magazine has released 'TIME100 Next India', the first edition of its TIME100 Next list focused on a single country, made in partnership with Reliance.
  • It profiles emerging Indians whose work points to the future; TIME notes that by 2030 one in every five working-age people in the world will be Indian.
  • Those named include Shaheen Malik, who founded the Brave Souls Foundation in 2021 to help acid-attack survivors, and astronaut Shubhanshu Shukla, now training for India's first human spaceflight (Gaganyaan) expected in 2027.
  • Actor and doctor Sai Pallavi is also on the list.
  • Static GK: Shubhanshu Shukla flew on Axiom Mission-4 (2025) and became the first Indian to reach the International Space Station.
Social Issues — Child Rights

World Children's Day (20 Nov): child labour remains a key concern

  • World Children's Day is observed on 20 November, the date the UN General Assembly adopted the Declaration of the Rights of the Child (1959) and the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC, 1989); India ratified the UNCRC in 1992.
  • Law — Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 (amended 2016). Aim: end child labour and protect adolescents.
  • Provisions: bans employment of children under 14 in all occupations (except helping family enterprises after school hours and as child artists); bans adolescents (14–18) in hazardous work; offences are cognizable, with jail of 6 months–2 years and/or a fine of ₹20,000–50,000.
  • Ministry: Ministry of Labour and Employment; complaints and rescue are tracked on the PENCIL portal; Article 24 bars children under 14 in factories, mines and hazardous work, and Article 21A gives free education for ages 6–14.
  • Challenges: hidden work in homes, farms and dhabas, migration, poverty, weak inspection and low conviction rates.
Economy — Taxation

Online gaming GST: High Court stays a demand as valuation dispute continues

  • The Allahabad High Court on 16 November stayed a GST demand against the online gaming firm Skillwin, in a dispute over how the 28% tax is to be valued.
  • The issue is whether tax applies on the full amount deposited by players or only on the platform's fee; firms say Rule 31B (full face value) was wrongly applied to periods when Rule 31A applied.
  • Large demands are pending against other firms, such as about ₹6,691 crore on Sporta and about ₹5,720 crore on Play Games 24x7.
  • Background: the GST Council fixed 28% on the full face value of bets from 1 October 2023, and the Supreme Court upheld the levy in May 2026.
  • Law — Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025. Aim: promote e-sports and social games and ban online money games. Provisions: ban on offering, advertising and financing real-money games; an Online Gaming Authority. Ministry: Electronics and IT (MeitY). Challenges: past tax demands, job losses and offshore betting apps.

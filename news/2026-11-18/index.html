RAS News Digest — 18 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 18 November 2026.

International — Quad

Quad summit in Manila opposes 'coercion'; India to host first Quad Business Forum

  • The Quad Leaders' Summit was held in Manila, the Philippines, on 17 November 2026, the first time it met in a non-member country.
  • It was hosted by U.S. President Donald Trump and attended by PM Narendra Modi, Australian PM Anthony Albanese and Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi.
  • The joint statement opposed economic and maritime 'coercion' in the Indo-Pacific.
  • PM Modi proposed steps on maritime security, critical-mineral supply chains and trade, and said India will host the first Quad Business Forum in Mumbai in 2027.
  • Four decisions were taken: a Quad Business Leaders' Forum from next year (first in Mumbai, 2027); an AI accelerator programme worth about ₹8,000 crore to give the Indo-Pacific access to trusted AI; the first Quad-level maritime military exercise in 2027; and sharing of cyber-threat intelligence to protect critical infrastructure.
  • On critical minerals, the four countries signed new agreements to end China's dominance over rare earths; President Trump claimed the Quad would produce rare earths on a par with China within ten months.
  • This was the 7th Quad Leaders' Summit and the second Modi–Trump meeting in six months (they met earlier in France in June).
  • History: the Quad grew out of the 'Tsunami Core Group' of 2004, was proposed by Japanese PM Shinzo Abe in 2007, revived in Manila in 2017 and raised to leaders' level in 2021; the Quad Critical Minerals Initiative was launched in 2025. It is not a formal military alliance.
  • Static GK: The Quad (India, U.S., Japan, Australia) began in 2007, was revived in 2017, and held its first leaders' summit (virtual) in March 2021.
International — East Asia Summit

At the East Asia Summit, Modi proposes a seafarers' safety initiative

  • PM Modi attended the ASEAN-India Summit, the East Asia Summit and the Quad meeting back-to-back in Manila.
  • At the East Asia Summit he proposed a seafarers' safety initiative and flagged cyber fraud as a shared regional threat.
  • He called for 'peace, stability and predictability' and said India will back every effort for peace in Ukraine and West Asia.
  • Static GK: The East Asia Summit began in 2005 (Kuala Lumpur); India is a founding member, along with ASEAN countries, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia and New Zealand; the U.S. and Russia joined in 2011.
Defence — Agnipath

Revamp of Agnipath under discussion: 75% absorption and a 6-year term

  • A revamp of the Agnipath scheme is under discussion that could absorb up to 75% of Agniveers as regular soldiers, against 25% now.
  • Agniveers may be offered a two-year extension, making the term six years instead of four.
  • Multi-skilling and proficiency in new technology and weapon systems are being considered as criteria for absorption.
  • Over 2,700 Agniveers, including 142 women, recently completed training at INS Chilka in Odisha.
  • Static GK: Agnipath was launched in June 2022 for recruitment into the Army, Navy and Air Force for four years, with up to 25% retained.
Geography — Cyclone

Cyclone Akvan slows, set to become very severe over the Bay of Bengal

  • Cyclonic storm Akvan is over the central parts of the south and west-central Bay of Bengal.
  • The IMD expects it to become a severe cyclonic storm on 18 November and a very severe cyclonic storm the next day.
  • The system has slowed and shifted its track slightly, delaying the earlier forecast.
  • Static GK: The IMD grades cyclones by wind speed: cyclonic storm (62–88 km/h), severe (89–117 km/h) and very severe (118–166 km/h).
Health — Malaria

President hails progress in malaria control at ICMR-NIMR golden jubilee

  • President Droupadi Murmu opened the golden jubilee celebrations of the ICMR-National Institute of Malaria Research in New Delhi.
  • Malaria cases and deaths in India fell by nearly 80% between 2015 and 2025.
  • High-burden districts fell from 155 to 33, and 160 districts reported zero indigenous cases during 2022-2025.
  • India exited the WHO's 'High Burden to High Impact' (HBHI) group in 2024.
  • Static GK: India aims to eliminate malaria by 2030 under the National Framework for Malaria Elimination.
Economy — Energy

Amazon to invest $7 billion more in Indian renewables by 2030

  • Amazon will invest an additional $7 billion in renewable energy projects in India by 2030.
  • This is on top of the $4 billion it had already committed, adding new wind and solar capacity.
  • The company said the investment supports India's energy security and its net-zero goal.
  • Static GK: India's net-zero target year is 2070, announced at COP26 in Glasgow (2021); its target is 500 GW of non-fossil capacity by 2030.
International — India-Japan

Rajnath Singh in Tokyo for the fourth India-Japan 2+2 dialogue

  • Defence Minister Rajnath Singh is visiting Japan on 18–19 November 2026.
  • He will co-chair the fourth India-Japan Foreign and Defence Ministers' (2+2) Dialogue in Tokyo with External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar and their Japanese counterparts.
  • The talks focus on defence cooperation, maritime security and defence-industrial partnerships.
  • Static GK: The first India-Japan 2+2 ministerial dialogue was held in New Delhi in 2019.
Science — Space

Voyager 1 becomes one light-day away from Earth

  • On 18 November 2026, NASA's Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth, so a radio signal takes a full day to reach it.
  • This distance is nearly 26 billion km, and the spacecraft travels at about 61,000 km/h.
  • Voyager 1 has been the farthest human-made object from Earth since 17 February 1998.
  • Static GK: Voyager 1 was launched on 5 September 1977 and entered interstellar space in August 2012.
Environment — Wildlife

Black-necked crane numbers in Ladakh rise about 25%

  • Ladakh's black-necked crane population rose to 108 in 2026 from 87 last year, an increase of nearly 25%.
  • The breeding-season survey counted 86 adults and 22 juveniles.
  • The black-necked crane is the State bird of Ladakh and is listed as Near Threatened by the IUCN.
  • Static GK: It breeds in high-altitude wetlands of the Changthang plateau, such as Hanle and Tso Kar.
Rajasthan — Heritage

Jaipur enters its 300th year on Foundation Day

  • Jaipur celebrated its Foundation Day on 18 November 2026 and entered its 300th year; it completes 300 years in 2027.
  • The State government has planned year-long celebrations leading up to the tercentenary.
  • Jaipur was founded on 18 November 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II of the Kachwaha dynasty, who shifted his capital from Amber.
  • It was planned by Vidyadhar Bhattacharya on a grid of nine blocks, following Vastu and Shilpa Shastra, and is regarded as India's first planned city.
  • Static GK: The Walled City of Jaipur became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2019; Jantar Mantar (2010) and Amber Fort, as one of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan (2013), are also listed.
  • Static GK: National Geographic has named Jaipur in its 'Best of the World 2027' list.
Rajasthan — Judiciary & Law

High Court gives government 10 more days to decide on the Advocates Protection Bill, 2023

  • The Rajasthan High Court has given the State government 10 more days to take a final decision on the Rajasthan Advocates Protection Bill, 2023.
  • If no decision is taken, the Chief Secretary must appear in person on 2 December and explain on affidavit why no decision has been taken since May 2025.
  • The interim order was passed by Justices Mahendra Kumar Goyal and Bhuwan Goyal on a petition by a former president of the Bar Association, Jaipur.
  • Aim: to protect advocates from assault, grievous hurt, criminal force and criminal intimidation while discharging their professional duties.
  • Key provisions: assault punishable with up to 2 years' jail and ₹25,000 fine, grievous hurt with up to 7 years and ₹50,000 fine; complaints investigated by an officer not below Deputy SP within 7 days; police protection for the complainant advocate; compensation and medical costs from the offender; and punishment for misuse or false complaints.
  • Department concerned: Law and Legal Affairs Department, Government of Rajasthan; the Bar Council of Rajasthan is to be informed of cases.
  • Challenges: the Bill, passed in March 2023, is still not in force; critics fear it creates a special class of protected persons and could be misused, while advocates point to rising attacks.
  • Static GK: Rajasthan was the first State to pass such a law; Karnataka's law came into force in 2024 and Telangana's in 2026.
Rajasthan — Education

Banasthali Vidyapith to host BRICS Women Leadership Summit (19–23 Nov)

  • Banasthali Vidyapith will host the BRICS Women Leadership Summit from 19 to 23 November 2026.
  • The summit, held in Russia for about ten years, is being hosted by another country for the first time.

Rajasthan — Urban Transport

1,150 e-buses for nine Rajasthan cities under PM e-Bus Sewa; Jaipur gets 550

  • Rajasthan has approval for 1,150 electric buses across nine cities under the PM e-Bus Sewa scheme, up from 675 for eight cities earlier.
  • Jaipur gets the most (550), followed by Jodhpur (125), Ajmer and Kota (100 each) and Bikaner (75).
  • Bhilwara, Alwar, Udaipur and Sikar get 50 buses each; Sikar was added later.
  • About 250 buses have arrived so far, and 103 have been handed over to Jaipur City Transport Services Limited.
  • Static GK: PM e-Bus Sewa was approved in August 2023 to run 10,000 e-buses in cities on a public-private partnership model.
Rajasthan — Energy

India's first discom sodium-ion battery storage pilot in Jaipur

  • Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (JVVNL) will set up a 250 kW / 250 kWh sodium-ion battery energy storage system at K.L. Saini Stadium, Jaipur.
  • It is being supported by the Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet (GEAPP).
  • The pilot will test whether sodium-ion batteries can handle local peak demand, use rooftop solar better and reduce load on transformers in Rajasthan's extreme heat.
  • Sodium-ion batteries use abundant sodium instead of lithium, reducing import dependence.
  • Static GK: Rajasthan has three power distribution companies: Jaipur (JVVNL), Ajmer (AVVNL) and Jodhpur (JdVVNL).
Rajasthan — Policing

Jaipur Police starts 'Know Your Police Beat' QR-code initiative

  • The Jaipur Police Commissionerate has launched a 'Know Your Police Beat' initiative using QR codes.
  • By scanning the code, residents can find their beat officer and contact details.
  • It was launched by Jaipur Police Commissioner Sachin Mittal and began in the Mansarovar police station area.
  • Mansarovar police station had earlier been described as a fully digital model police station of the State.
  • Static GK: Rajasthan introduced the police commissionerate system in Jaipur and Jodhpur on 1 January 2011.

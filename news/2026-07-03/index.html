Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated Today's News — 3 July 2026.

International Relations

India–Japan seal 129 MoUs; Japan eyes ~$1 trillion investment

  • PM Modi and Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi called a free, rules-based Indo-Pacific (FOIP) a shared priority.
  • The two sides signed 129 MoUs spanning technology, investment and artificial intelligence.
  • Japan pledged to work toward about $1 trillion (₹5.92 lakh crore) of investment in India over the coming decade.
  • Key deliverables: a first defence co-development pact, an AI-ecosystem partnership and a bio-manufacturing pact.

Source: The Hindu / Indian Express, 3 Jul 2026

Polity & Governance

Supreme Court warns against AI-'hallucinated' case law in rulings

  • The Supreme Court set aside an NCLT order that had relied on fictitious, AI-generated legal precedents.
  • A Bench of Justices P.S. Narasimha and Alok Aradhe demanded a 'zero-tolerance' approach.
  • It asked the Bar Council of India to frame guidelines against citing AI-fabricated judgments.
  • The court is publishing Draft Regulations for Use of AI in Courts, 2026 for public consultation.

Source: The Hindu / Indian Express, 3 Jul 2026

Governance / Economy

CAG audit ordered into 3 Delhi discoms over ₹38,500 crore

  • The Delhi government sought a CAG audit of BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna and Tata Power Delhi.
  • The focus is about ₹38,500 crore of accumulated 'regulatory assets' (deferred costs) recoverable from consumers.
  • Delhi's power tariffs have not been revised since 2014-15.
  • The order cites an August 2025 Supreme Court direction on scrutiny of regulatory assets.

Source: The Hindu, 3 Jul 2026

Environment & Ecology

Green nod for coal mining in Chhattisgarh's Hasdeo forests

  • The Environment Ministry cleared the Kente Extension coal block in the Hasdeo-Arand forests on 24 June.
  • Hasdeo — 'Central India's green lungs' — hosts about 640 plant species and roughly 400 elephants.
  • About 60,000 trees are to be felled; the coal is earmarked for Rajasthan (Chhabra) and Surat power plants.
  • The WII and ICFRE flagged ecological impacts and local communities oppose the diversion.

Source: Indian Express, 3 Jul 2026

Defence

INS Trikand foils a piracy attempt in the Gulf of Aden

  • The Navy's stealth frigate INS Trikand intervened during a piracy attack on the bulk carrier MV Golden Arsenal.
  • The incident occurred about 100 nautical miles north-east of Djibouti.
  • The ship's 100-member crew, including one Indian, was kept safe.
  • It reinforces India's sustained anti-piracy presence in the Gulf of Aden and the Arabian Sea.

Source: Indian Express, 3 Jul 2026

Geography / Environment

Monsoon covers Rajasthan and Delhi, about five days late

  • The IMD declared monsoon onset over Delhi and Rajasthan, about five days later than normal, covering the whole country.
  • Heavy rain lashed Mumbai and Gujarat as the system advanced.
  • Very heavy rain was forecast for many States over 3-6 July, with red and orange alerts.
  • Konkan, Madhya Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Odisha were among the alert zones.

Source: Rajasthan Patrika / The Hindu, 3 Jul 2026

Internal Security (Rajasthan)

Rajasthan ATS arrests JeM-linked operatives; Jaipur woman held

  • The Rajasthan ATS arrested four alleged operatives with cross-border handler links accused of reconnaissance of sites in Delhi and Punjab.
  • Weapons, including a pistol, were recovered.
  • Separately, the ATS arrested Babita Dhakad (38) of Jaipur under the UAPA for online contact with Jaish-e-Mohammed.
  • She was allegedly cultivated through social media.

Source: Rajasthan Patrika / Indian Express, 3 Jul 2026

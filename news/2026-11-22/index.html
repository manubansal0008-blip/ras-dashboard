RAS News Digest — 22 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 22 November 2026.

International Relations — ASEAN

Manila summits: India offers free tourist visas to all ASEAN citizens; Quad plans first naval exercise

  • PM Modi attended the ASEAN-India Summit, the East Asia Summit and the Quad Summit in Manila (Philippines, ASEAN chair 2026).
  • He announced free tourist visas for all ASEAN citizens visiting India, and agreements on disaster management and maritime cooperation.
  • No progress was made on revising the ASEAN-India Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA). ASEAN accounts for about 11% of India's trade ($128 billion in 2025-26).
  • The East Asia Summit agreed to a Seafarers' Safety Initiative; the Quad announced a Quad Business Summit and its first naval exercise with P-3/P-8 patrol aircraft for 2027.
Environment — Climate Change

COP31 in Antalya runs into overtime; draft drops fossil-fuel phase-out and $120-billion adaptation target

  • The COP31 climate conference at Antalya, Türkiye, was due to end on Friday but went into overtime without a final text.
  • Europe and developing countries are divided over a timeline to phase out fossil fuels and over climate finance.
  • The latest draft cover text removed references to fossil-fuel phase-out and net-zero dates, and also a firm $120 billion adaptation-finance target sought by developing countries.
  • At COP29 (Baku), developed nations agreed to lead in mobilising at least $300 billion a year for developing countries by 2035.
International — Disarmament

UN talks agree to develop rules on 'killer robots' within two years; outcome weakened by Russia's opposition

  • UN talks in Geneva under the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) ended with a pledge to develop new rules on lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) within two years.
  • Around 100 countries wanted legally binding rules quickly, but major arms producers — the US, Russia and Israel — opposed this.
  • The final deal leaves open whether future measures will be legally binding.
  • The UN Secretary-General, the Pope and the Red Cross have called for strong binding rules, as autonomous weapons are used in Ukraine, Sudan and West Asia.
International — Shipping & Climate

India a swing vote as IMO meets on shipping decarbonisation; vote expected on 4 December

  • The International Maritime Organization (IMO) meets in London from 23 November; a vote on a net-zero framework for global shipping is expected on 4 December.
  • In October 2025, the US disrupted the earlier vote, threatening retaliation and sanctions, and forced a one-year postponement.
  • India had voted for the adjournment last year and is now seen as a swing state; officials say it has not yet decided its position.
  • The April 2025 framework had been agreed by a simple majority at the IMO's environment committee.
International — Technology

US tightens curbs on advanced AI chips reaching China through overseas data centres

  • On 16 November, the US Commerce Department issued an interim final rule requiring licences before advanced American AI chips are supplied to data centres abroad that serve businesses headquartered in two dozen countries, including China, Iran and Russia.
  • Shipments of high-end AI chips to five countries, including Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore, now need explicit US approval.
  • China called it an abuse of export controls; the rule came days before the Trump–Xi meeting at Shenzhen on the sidelines of APEC (18–19 November).
  • US tech export controls draw on the Export Control Reform Act, 2018.
Polity — Local Bodies

Ladakh's first panchayat poll after becoming a Union Territory records 70% turnout

  • Ladakh recorded a turnout of 70.03% in its panchayat elections and 52.4% in municipal council polls on 21 November.
  • These are the first panchayat elections since Ladakh became a Union Territory.
  • Several villages boycotted the polls; activist Sonam Wangchuk said the polling booth had been shifted from his village.
  • Static: Ladakh became a UT (without legislature) on 31 October 2019 under the J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019.
Energy — Hydropower

Amit Shah: local fears over 11,200-MW Siang Upper project will be addressed through dialogue

  • Union Home Minister Amit Shah said concerns about the proposed 11,200-MW Siang Upper Multipurpose Project in Arunachal Pradesh would be addressed through dialogue.
  • He assured Siang Valley communities that their culture, traditions and religious beliefs would be respected and the project would not be forced.
  • He spoke at the President's Police Colour Award ceremony at Banderdewa, near Itanagar.
  • The project is seen as India's response to China's mega dam on the Yarlung Tsangpo (Brahmaputra) in Tibet.
Health — Vaccination

Over one crore HPV vaccine doses given in nine months; Rajasthan crosses 85% coverage

  • India has given more than one crore doses of the human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine in under nine months, covering about 87% of the annual target of 14-year-old girls.
  • The national cervical cancer prevention campaign began in February 2026; the single-dose vaccine is free and voluntary, with parental consent.
  • UP leads with 29.33 lakh girls vaccinated, followed by Bihar (13.70 lakh) and Gujarat (7.75 lakh). Six states (Gujarat, UP, Andhra Pradesh, Mizoram, MP, Bihar) reached 100% coverage.
  • Rajasthan has reached 85.23% coverage. The U-WIN platform tracks beneficiaries and issues digital certificates.
Infrastructure — Bridges

PM opens 9.76-km six-lane 'Nitish Setu' over the Ganga between Patna and Vaishali

  • PM Modi inaugurated the 9.76-km six-lane cable-based bridge 'Nitish Setu' on the Ganga, built at over ₹5,500 crore.
  • It connects Kacchi Dargah (Patna district) with Bidupur (Vaishali district); it is named after former Bihar CM Nitish Kumar.
  • He also laid foundation stones for projects worth ₹34,470 crore at the Jafarpatti Industrial Area, Vaishali.
  • The event marked one year of the NDA government in Bihar.
Geography — Cyclones

Three cyclones in three weeks over the Bay of Bengal: Arnab, Murasu and Akvan

  • Three cyclones formed over the Bay of Bengal within 23 days this year.
  • Cyclone Arnab made landfall near Yanam (Andhra Pradesh coast) on 28 October, Murasu near Ongole on 3 November, and Akvan near Gopalpur (Odisha) on 20 November.
  • Post-monsoon conditions favour cyclones: weak vertical wind shear, warm waters and disturbances from the northeast monsoon.
  • Such a run is uncommon but not unique: IMD recorded three cyclones in 32 days in 1976 and 1987; Helen and Lehar hit Andhra within a week in November 2013.
Environment — Biodiversity

India's only island-dwelling cobra, the Andaman cobra, faces conservation challenges

  • The Andaman cobra (Naja sagittifera) is the only cobra species in India that lives in an island ecosystem; it is endemic to the Andaman Islands.
  • It was first described in 1913 by herpetologist Frank Wall in the Journal of the Bombay Natural History Society, yet little is known about it.
  • Small isolated populations face inbreeding risk, and no outside population exists for recovery.
  • It is highly venomous and can spit venom, so people often kill it out of fear; deforestation and farming expansion add to the threat.
Economy — Tax & Cyber Security

Metro Rail to get a place in e-Way Bill system; CERT-In flags payment frauds beyond the financial sector

  • GST rules will explicitly include Metro Rail in the e-Way Bill system, since 'railways' is not defined and metro systems are legally distinct from Indian Railways.
  • DMRC plans a pilot depot-to-depot cargo service using the metro as middle-mile connectivity. India's metro network spans about 1,170 km across 26 cities.
  • CERT-In issued a 'Critical' advisory on unauthorised payments at organisations outside the financial sector, including tech firms supporting government-linked payments.
  • Attackers altered business records or approval settings, so fraudulent payments cleared routine checks.
Economy — Philanthropy

EdelGive Hurun India Philanthropy List 2026: Shiv Nadar family tops with ₹2,593 crore; 219 Indians gave ₹11,554 crore

  • 219 Indians donated ₹11,554 crore in FY 2025-26, as per the EdelGive Hurun India Philanthropy List 2026.
  • The Shiv Nadar family is the top donor with ₹2,593 crore — 22.4% of all donations — followed by the Gautam Adani family (₹877 crore), Bajaj family (₹632 crore) and Mukesh Ambani family (₹620 crore).
  • The top 10 donors account for about 57% of total giving; education received the most (₹4,188 crore), then health (₹1,768 crore) and art, culture and heritage (₹1,377 crore).
  • Only four of the 10 richest Indians in the Hurun Rich List also feature among the top 10 donors.
Rajasthan — Politics

RLP emerges as a strong third force in Rajasthan rural polls with 51 zila parishad and 497 panchayat samiti seats

  • Nagaur MP Hanuman Beniwal's Rashtriya Loktantrik Party (RLP) won 497 panchayat samiti seats and 51 zila parishad seats in the panchayat polls.
  • The eight-year-old party got 6.78% of votes in panchayat samiti polls; it won in Nagaur, Bikaner, Jodhpur, Churu, Didwana-Kuchaman, Barmer and even Sanganer (CM Bhajan Lal Sharma's constituency).
  • Elections for zila pramukhs (25 November) and pradhans (27 November) have triggered efforts to keep members together.
  • The 277-km Bharatpur–Beawar greenfield expressway (₹8,656 crore) will pass through 210 villages of five districts; about 2,600 hectares will be acquired.
Rajasthan — Events

Om Birla opens five-day BRICS Women Leadership Summit at Banasthali, Tonk

  • Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla inaugurated the five-day BRICS Women Leadership Summit at Banasthali (Tonk district).
  • About 120 women leaders from different countries — parliamentarians, policymakers, academics and entrepreneurs — are taking part; it ends on 23 November.
  • 81 institutions worldwide had applied to host the event.
  • Birla called the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam an important step for women's participation in decision-making.
Sports

KL Rahul becomes first Indian opener to score Test centuries in all SENA countries; India women beat Korea in Asian Champions Trophy

  • KL Rahul scored a century in the Wellington Test against New Zealand, becoming the first Indian opener to score Test tons in all SENA countries (South Africa, England, New Zealand, Australia).
  • Shubman Gill fell for 98; India led by 118 runs with six wickets in hand.
  • In the Women's Asian Champions Trophy at Bhopal, India beat South Korea 2–1 (goals by Mumtaz Khan and Sunelita Toppo) to go top of the standings.

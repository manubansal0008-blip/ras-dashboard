RAS News Digest — 23 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 23 November 2026.

International Relations — India-Nepal

Modi–Balen Shah talks today: energy and infrastructure pacts, new Ayodhya–Janakpur train on the table

  • Nepal PM Balendra (Balen) Shah reached New Delhi on 22 November on his first bilateral visit abroad since taking office in March 2026; EAM S. Jaishankar called on him.
  • He meets PM Modi on 23 November. Agreements are expected mainly in energy, hydropower, railways, connectivity and infrastructure; India plans to raise FDI in Nepal.
  • The two PMs are expected to launch a weekly train between Ayodhya and Janakpurdham (Nepal) — 604 km; it will leave Janakpurdham every Saturday and Ayodhya Cantt every Monday.
  • Nepal sold electricity worth about ₹1,800 crore to India last year. The boundary dispute over Lipulekh, Limpiyadhura and Kalapani remains; India treats these areas as part of Uttarakhand.
Environment — Climate Change

COP31 ends without agreement as countries differ on fossil fuel future

  • For the first time in over 15 years, the annual UN climate conference failed to reach consensus on the main political agreement, at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye.
  • The European Union and over 80 other countries, mainly small island states, refused to endorse a draft that did not mention any phase-out of fossil fuels.
  • Oil-dependent economies like Saudi Arabia kept fossil-fuel language out with support from large economies like India and China; only routine agenda items were cleared.
  • India's Environment Minister said India engaged constructively and remains committed to dialogue to build consensus. COP31 was presided over by Türkiye's Murat Kurum.
Economy — Food Security

Study flags 13% 'inclusion error' in PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana; West Bengal tops, Rajasthan among lowest

  • An evaluation study commissioned by the Department of Food and Public Distribution found a 13.2% inclusion error among beneficiaries of PMGKAY, the free foodgrain scheme under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013.
  • The study, conducted in 2025-26, covered 51,303 households across all 36 states and UTs.
  • Highest error: West Bengal (39.5%), Tripura (39.2%), Tamil Nadu (38.7%). Lowest: Madhya Pradesh (0.5%), Rajasthan (1.8%) and Uttar Pradesh (6%).
  • Ineligible households included those with pucca houses of three or more rooms, air conditioners, motor vehicles or government jobs.
Economy — Social Security

EPFO 3.0 likely from February: PF cover for gig and unorganised workers, full tech overhaul

  • EPFO plans to launch EPFO 3.0 in February with a complete overhaul of its technology, enabling direct UAN-linked PF contributions for all worker categories.
  • It will allow PF contributions for unorganised sector and gig workers, in line with the Code on Social Security, under which aggregators contribute 1–2% of turnover, capped at 5% of the amount payable to gig workers.
  • A UPI facility to withdraw PF through the BHIM app is in its final stages (expected by December-end); a WhatsApp service for PF balance and passbook was launched this month.
  • EPFO has nearly 8 crore active members and over 34 crore total members, with a retirement corpus of nearly ₹32 lakh crore.
Economy — Trade

India–EU free trade agreement likely to be signed on 16 December

  • India and the 27-nation European Union are likely to sign their free trade agreement (FTA) on 16 December, an official said.
  • The two sides concluded negotiations on 27 January 2026.
  • EU Ambassador to India Jean-Eric Paquet recently said the FTA will be signed during PM Modi's visit.
  • India–EU FTA talks were first launched in 2007 (as the Broad-based Trade and Investment Agreement), stalled in 2013 and relaunched in 2022.
Economy — Poverty

PM: 50 crore Indians have come out of poverty; FM writes on 'the decade India turned the tide on poverty'

  • PM Narendra Modi, at a 'Sneh Milan' programme in Gujarat, said about 50 crore people have come out of poverty, though some do not want to talk about it.
  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman credited Direct Benefit Transfer, over 59 crore Jan Dhan accounts and MUDRA loans for taking benefits directly to people without middlemen.
  • World Bank estimates show extreme poverty in India fell from 27.1% in 2011-12 to 5.3% in 2022-23.
  • NITI Aayog estimated that 24.82 crore people escaped multidimensional poverty between 2013-14 and 2022-23.
Defence — Indigenisation

Indigenous 'Bagheera AT1' anti-tank missile system hits targets day and night in Pokhran trials

  • Adani Defence & Aerospace successfully tested its vehicle-mounted anti-tank guided missile (ATGM) system 'Bagheera AT1' at the Pokhran Field Firing Range, Rajasthan, on 20 November.
  • All four missiles hit their targets; the trials tested day and night engagement, firing two missiles on a single laser beam, and hitting an aerial target at long range.
  • The demo was done on a 'no cost, no commitment' basis before officials of the Army, the Department of Military Affairs and the Defence Ministry.
  • The Army currently uses second-generation Milan-2T and Konkurs-M along with Spike missiles; procurement of Javelin is in progress.
International — World Roundup

Russian drone attack kills four near Kyiv; Russian cargo ship struck in Gulf of Finland; Indian-origin Navdeep Bains leads Ontario Liberals

  • At least four people were killed in Russian drone attacks on Ukraine's Kyiv region on 22 November, President Zelenskyy said.
  • The Russian cargo vessel Sparta II, bound for Kaliningrad, was struck by drones in the Gulf of Finland; Finland's military said the strike was likely carried out by Ukraine. Finland and Estonia are NATO members.
  • Russian President Vladimir Putin praised India's ancient civilisation and linguistic diversity in an interview.
  • Indian-origin Navdeep Bains was elected leader of the Ontario Liberal Party in Canada; South Korea's Education Minister resigned over an AI cheating scandal.
Rajasthan — Electoral Rolls

Women's share rises in Rajasthan voter list: gender ratio up from 909 to 913 in SSR-2027 draft

  • The gender ratio (women per 1,000 men) in Rajasthan's electoral roll has risen to 913 in the draft roll of the Special Summary Revision (SSR-2027).
  • It was 909 in the SIR-2026 draft and 911 in the final SIR roll published on 21 February 2026; the SSR-2027 draft released on 12 November 2026 shows 913 — a rise of 4 points overall.
  • The roll has over 5.26 crore voters — about 2.51 crore women and 2.75 crore men.
  • Rajasthan is better than Hindi-speaking states like UP and Bihar, but behind southern and north-eastern states.
Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

Panchayat samiti results: Congress–BJP gap widens from 41 to 222 samitis in five years

  • In 2020-21, Congress held 180 panchayat samitis and BJP 139 — a gap of 41.
  • In 2026, Congress and its supported candidates reached 301 samitis (229 own + 72 supported), while BJP and supported candidates were limited to 79 (51 own + 28 supported).
  • BJP's own samitis fell by 88 (139 to 51); Congress's own rose by 49 (180 to 229) — a swing of 181 samitis.
  • Zila pramukh elections are on 25 November and pradhan elections on 27 November.
Rajasthan — Weather & Culture

Mount Abu at 0°C for second day; Bundi Festival on 27–29 November

  • Cold northerly winds intensified winter in Rajasthan; Mount Abu recorded a minimum of 0°C for the second straight day, with a thin layer of frost on vehicles and plants.
  • Jaipur recorded 14.6°C at night and 27.2°C in the day; the Met department expects dry weather for a week.
  • The Tourism Department and District Administration, Bundi, will host the Bundi Festival on 27–29 November.
  • Rajasthan's pavilion at the 45th India International Trade Fair in New Delhi is showcasing handicrafts, investment and tourism.
Sports

Pant's century takes India to 720/9 in Wellington; India women thrash Thailand 7–0; World Chess Championship from 24 November

  • Rishabh Pant scored 128 as India declared at 720/9 in the first Test against New Zealand at the Basin Reserve, Wellington, a lead of 352 over NZ's 368; India need eight wickets to go 1–0 up.
  • Defending champions India beat Thailand 7–0 in the Women's Asian Champions Trophy at Bhopal to seal a semi-final berth; Deepika scored a hat-trick.
  • The World Chess Championship between D. Gukesh and Javokhir Sindarov will be held in Geneva from 24 November to 12 December.

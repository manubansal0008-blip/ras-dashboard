RAS News Digest — 09 July 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 09 July 2026.

Economy

RBI keeps repo rate unchanged at 5.25% with a neutral stance

  • The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) kept the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25% and retained a neutral stance, staying vigilant on the balance of risks.
  • It projected CPI inflation of about 4.6% for the current financial year, flagging elevated energy prices due to the West Asia conflict and possible El Niño conditions as upside risks.
  • The MPC is a six-member committee; under the flexible inflation-targeting framework the RBI must keep CPI inflation at 4% within a band of +/- 2%.

Source: Reserve Bank of India — Monetary Policy Committee, July 2026

Infrastructure / Aviation

Modified UDAN scheme to extend regional air connectivity over the next decade

  • The Union Cabinet approved a Modified UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) scheme with an outlay of about Rs 29,000 crore over ten years, aligned with the Viksit Bharat 2047 vision.
  • Since its launch in 2016, UDAN — a Regional Connectivity Scheme — has operationalised 669 routes and connected 95 airports, heliports and water aerodromes.
  • It has benefited over 1.66 crore passengers by making flying affordable through viability-gap funding and capped fares on selected routes.

Source: PIB / Ministry of Civil Aviation, 2026

Education / Digital Governance

APAAR IDs cross 26 crore as India builds a lifelong academic credit ledger

  • APAAR (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry) is a unique 12-digit ID for students, linked to the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC).
  • As of 2 July 2026, over 26.35 crore verified APAAR IDs had been generated.
  • APAAR flows from the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020's idea of credit accumulation and transfer, enabling multiple entry and exit across institutions.

Source: Ministry of Education, July 2026

Science & Technology

ISRO readies GISAT-class geo-imaging satellite for disaster monitoring

  • ISRO is preparing to launch a GISAT-class geo-imaging (earth-observation) satellite designed for near-continuous observation of the Indian sub-continent.
  • Placed in a geostationary orbit and launched on a GSLV, it can provide quick imaging for monitoring natural hazards and disasters.
  • A geostationary satellite orbits at about 36,000 km, matching Earth's rotation so it appears fixed over one region — ideal for continuous regional watch.

Source: ISRO, 2026

Polity / Judiciary

Gujarat High Court upholds convictions in the 2008 Ahmedabad serial blasts case

  • The Gujarat High Court upheld the conviction of 49 accused in the 2008 Ahmedabad serial bomb blasts case — confirming 38 death sentences and 11 life imprisonments.
  • The coordinated blasts of July 2008 had killed and injured scores of people across Ahmedabad within a short span.
  • The confirmation of death sentences by the High Court is a stage of appellate scrutiny before the matter can travel to the Supreme Court.

Source: Gujarat High Court, July 2026

Environment

NGT pulls up Rajasthan over Chandlai Lake; bans commercial units near protected areas

  • The National Green Tribunal (NGT) sharply criticised Rajasthan authorities for failing to protect Chandlai Lake — cited as a Ramsar site — from encroachment and pollution by textile units.
  • In a separate order, the NGT imposed a blanket ban on commercial operations within one kilometre of protected areas, in a case linked to a resort near the Sariska Tiger Reserve.
  • The NGT is a statutory body set up under the National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 for effective and speedy disposal of environmental cases.

Source: National Green Tribunal, 7 July 2026

Rural Livelihoods / Women

SHE-LEAPS platform launched under LokOS to power women-led rural enterprises

  • SHE-LEAPS was launched under LokOS, a web-and-mobile platform for end-to-end digitisation of Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations under the DAY-NRLM.
  • It is a dedicated platform for creating and tracking women-led farm and non-farm enterprises, covering records, savings, lending and convergence.
  • DAY-NRLM (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana-National Rural Livelihoods Mission) works to organise rural poor women into SHGs and improve their access to finance and markets.

Source: Ministry of Rural Development — DAY-NRLM, 2026

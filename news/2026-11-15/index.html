RAS News Digest — 15 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 15 November 2026.

International — Technology

Communication failure halts US air traffic; first nationwide ground stop since 9/11

  • A failure in the communication system of US Air Traffic Control halted flights across the country for about four hours on Friday night.
  • The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) ordered a nationwide 'ground stop', the first since the 9/11 attacks 25 years ago.
  • About 10,000 flights were affected; nearly 8,000 were cancelled and over 2,000 were delayed, hitting more than 15 lakh passengers.
  • US Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said a telecommunication outage caused the ground stop, and President Donald Trump ruled out a cyber attack.
  • Separately, Microsoft restored Outlook, Teams and Copilot after a 36-hour outage that hit services in 74 countries; Air India advised passengers to check flight status.
Sports — Chess

Anuj Shrivatri becomes India's 100th chess Grandmaster

  • Anuj Shrivatri, aged 22, became India's 100th Grandmaster and Madhya Pradesh's first.
  • Anuj Shrivatri completed the title at the 7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open Chess Championship in the Philippines.
  • Viswanathan Anand became India's first Grandmaster in 1988.
  • Tamil Nadu accounts for 38 of India's 100 Grandmasters.
  • Only four of India's 100 Grandmasters are women: Koneru Humpy, Harika Dronavalli, R. Vaishali and Divya Deshmukh.
Sports — Cricket

India beat Pakistan by one wicket chasing 338 to win the U-19 Asia Cup

  • In the U-19 Asia Cup final, Pakistan scored 337/9 and India reached 339/9 to win by one wicket.
  • India's last two wickets added 87 runs, including 25 runs in the final two overs.
  • Mohit Ulwa (49 not out) was named Player of the Match.
  • Aryavir Sehwag, son of Virender Sehwag, was named Player of the Tournament.
  • In the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy, 15-year-old Vaibhav Suryavanshi became the youngest captain in T20 history while leading Bihar.
Sports — Awards

Lalremsiami becomes Mizoram's first Arjuna Award winner

  • President Droupadi Murmu presented the Arjuna Award to hockey player Lalremsiami.
  • Lalremsiami, aged 26, is the first player from Mizoram to receive the Arjuna Award.
  • Lalremsiami belongs to Kolasib district in northern Mizoram, bordering Assam.
  • Lalremsiami was the first Indian woman hockey player to win the FIH 'Rising Star of the Year' award (2020).
  • Lalremsiami played at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, where India's women's team finished fourth.
Economy — Industry

NITI Aayog assesses the impact of PLI schemes; four sectors on track

  • NITI Aayog released an 'Impact Assessment of PLI Schemes' covering all 14 notified sectors.
  • IIM Ahmedabad was the knowledge partner for the PLI assessment.
  • PLI schemes attracted ₹2.4 lakh crore of investment, about 76% of the target.
  • Large-scale electronics, pharmaceutical drugs, telecom products and food products were found on track.
  • Production Linked Incentive schemes were launched in 2020 to boost domestic manufacturing.
Agriculture

Nutrient-Based Subsidy rates fixed for Rabi 2026–27; DAP stays at ₹1,350 a bag

  • The revised Nutrient-Based Subsidy (NBS) rates apply from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.
  • The tentative outlay for NBS in Rabi 2026–27 is ₹62,370 crore, 64% more than the previous Rabi season.
  • DAP will remain available at a fixed price of ₹1,350 per bag.
  • A new sulphur package reimburses 20% of manufacturers' procurement cost of sulphur and sulphuric acid.
  • The NBS scheme has been in force since 2010 for phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers.
International — Health

First BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting held in Colombo

  • Health ministers of the seven BIMSTEC members met for the first time in Colombo, Sri Lanka.
  • The meeting adopted the Revised BIMSTEC Plan of Action on Public Health (2026–2030).
  • India will set up a BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine at ITRA, Jamnagar.
  • Thailand will host the second BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting in 2028.
  • BIMSTEC was formed in 1997 and its secretariat is in Dhaka.
Personalities — Rajasthan

Bunker Roy, founder of Tilonia's Barefoot College, passes away

  • Padma Shri awardee Sanjit 'Bunker' Roy died at the age of 81 at Tilonia in Ajmer district.
  • Bunker Roy founded the Social Work and Research Centre, known as Barefoot College, at Tilonia in 1972.
  • Barefoot College's 'Solar Mamas' programme trained rural women from many countries as solar engineers.
  • Bunker Roy received the Padma Shri in 1986.
  • TIME magazine named Bunker Roy among the 100 most influential people in 2010.
International Relations

Pakistan minister to attend SCO Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi; first ministerial visit in three years

  • Pakistan's Climate Change Minister Musadik Masood Malik will attend the SCO Civilisational Dialogue forum in New Delhi.
  • This will be the first visit to India by a Pakistani minister in more than three years.
  • India placed the Indus Waters Treaty 'in abeyance' after the April 2025 Pahalgam terrorist attack.
  • The Indus Waters Treaty was signed in 1960 with the World Bank as a signatory.
  • The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) was founded in 2001, and India became a full member in 2017.
International

Paraguay's Senate removes President Santiago Peña after a bank collapse

  • Paraguay's Senate voted to remove President Santiago Peña for misconduct in office.
  • The impeachment followed the collapse of Ueno Bank, a private lender holding savings of nearly half the population.
  • Santiago Peña was placed under house arrest as prosecutors investigate the bank collapse.
  • Paraguay is a landlocked country in South America; its capital is Asunción.
International

South Sudan postpones its first national elections to February

  • South Sudan postponed its first national elections from 22 December to February amid ethnic and political conflict.
  • The legislative and presidential elections had already been delayed several times.
  • South Sudan is the world's newest country; it became independent from Sudan in 2011.
  • Juba is the capital of South Sudan.

Rajasthan / Water

Rajasthan and Haryana ink long-awaited Yamuna water-sharing agreement

  • A Memorandum of Agreement was signed in New Delhi in the presence of Union Home Minister Amit Shah, with CMs Bhajan Lal Sharma (Rajasthan) and Nayab Singh Saini (Haryana).
  • Rajasthan will receive 577 MCM of its allocated Yamuna share each year, carried from the Hathnikund Barrage via three underground pipelines (each over 3.6 m diameter).
  • Beneficiaries are the Shekhawati districts of Sikar, Churu and Jhunjhunu; Phase 1 supplies drinking water (Jul-Oct) and Phase 2 irrigates ~1.05 lakh hectares.
  • It builds on a tripartite MoU of 17 February 2024 and fulfils a 32-year demand first raised under former CM Bhairon Singh Shekhawat in 1994.
  • Shah called it a model of 'cooperative federalism'; the Renukaji, Lakhwar and Kishau projects could add ~201 MCM more.

Source: Hindustan Times (Rajasthan), 30 Jun 2026

Rajasthan / Energy

Pachpadra refinery to be dedicated July 4; MP says petrochemical complex incomplete

  • PM Modi is scheduled to dedicate the HPCL Rajasthan Refinery at Pachpadra, Barmer, to the nation on 4 July 2026.
  • Barmer-Jaisalmer MP Ummedaram Beniwal said the main refinery unit is ready but the petrochemical complex may need another 2-2.5 years.
  • He claimed the project cost has risen from an initial ~₹36,000 crore to nearly ₹80,000 crore.
  • The MP also sought public release of the inquiry report into a recent fire at the refinery site.
  • The refinery was first inaugurated in 2013 and re-inaugurated in 2018.

Source: Hindustan Times (Rajasthan), 30 Jun 2026

Rajasthan / Urban

Rajasthan plans ₹15,800-crore urban infrastructure push under Urban Challenge Fund

  • UDH & LSG Minister Jhabar Singh Kharra announced a programme to expand roads, sewerage, drainage, solid-waste management, street lighting and civic infrastructure.
  • The push aligns with the Centre's Urban Challenge Fund, for which ₹1 lakh crore has been earmarked nationally.
  • The Directorate of Local Bodies signed an MoU with HUDCO for technical assistance and DPR preparation.
  • A key feature is its focus on Tier-II and Tier-III cities to spread modern civic infrastructure beyond big cities.
  • The minister linked it to PM Modi's vision of a Viksit Bharat by 2047.

Source: Hindustan Times (Rajasthan), 30 Jun 2026

Rajasthan / Crime

'Last Chambal dacoit' Jagan Gurjar found murdered in Ajmer high-security jail

  • Jagan Gurjar (56), a feared interstate dacoit across Rajasthan, MP and UP, was found murdered in his barrack on Monday.
  • He had 128 criminal cases (FIRs) to his name and was a native of Dholpur.
  • Preliminary probe points to personal enmity rather than gang rivalry; a barrack-mate is under suspicion.
  • A judicial inquiry has been ordered and a medical board tasked with the post-mortem.
  • It is the first such killing in Ajmer's high-security jail since it opened in 2015.

Source: The Times of India (Jaipur), 30 Jun 2026

Rajasthan / Accident

Three workers killed, three hurt as septic-tank wall collapses at Jaipur hotel site

  • A wall of an under-construction septic tank collapsed at a hotel site near Tala Mod on the Jaipur-Delhi Highway (NH-48), Chandwaji.
  • Three labourers, including two women, were killed and three others injured.
  • Over 20 labourers were working inside the tank when the accident occurred around 12:50 pm.
  • Civil Defence teams led the rescue; the injured were shifted to NIMS Hospital.
  • Authorities have launched a probe into the cause.

Source: The Times of India (Jaipur), 30 Jun 2026

International

Mediators set up de-escalation channels ahead of US–Iran talks in Doha

  • Iranian and US technical teams are to meet in Doha to implement an interim peace deal signed on 17 June 2026.
  • The focus is on managing the Strait of Hormuz and de-escalating tensions.
  • The 14-point MoU ended four months of conflict and reopened the strait, through which about a fifth of the world's oil and LNG transits.
  • Earlier closure had sent oil prices above $100 a barrel, stoking global inflation.
  • Mediators have set up communication channels to keep both sides in touch.

Source: Hindustan Times, 30 Jun 2026

Education

CBSE gives one-time relief on three-language rule for classes 7, 8 and 9

  • CBSE relaxed a circular (dated 15 May) that required these classes to study three languages, of which at least two must be native Indian languages.
  • Students who had already opted for two non-native languages may continue, but must add one Indian language.
  • There will be no CBSE Board exam in this added third language for these batches at Class 10.
  • For the 2026-27 Class 10 batch, the existing two-language system continues unchanged.
  • New Class 6 entrants from 2026-27 will follow the three-language rule in full.

Source: Hindustan Times, 30 Jun 2026

RAS News Digest — 28 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 28 November 2026.

Polity — Delimitation

Revised Delimitation Bill: 50% seat hike, inter-State freeze till 2051, women's quota by 2029

  • The revised Bills, to be taken up in the special sitting from 30 November, propose a uniform 50% increase in Lok Sabha and Assembly seats; a 50% rise on 543 gives about 815 Lok Sabha seats, and the House can go up to a maximum of 850.
  • The freeze on inter-State distribution of Lok Sabha seats on the basis of the 1971 Census would be extended for 25 more years, till 2051; boundaries within each State would be redrawn on the 2011 Census.
  • The exercise would operationalise 33% reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and Assemblies (106th Amendment, 2023) before the 2029 election; about 272 of 815 Lok Sabha seats would be reserved for women.
  • Unlike the 1976 and 2001 freezes, the new seat share is no longer linked to the goal of population stabilisation.
  • Tamil Nadu's share of about 7.18% of Lok Sabha seats would stay unchanged; the NCP(SP) said it would back the revised Bill.
  • Background: India's population has grown at least 2.6 times since the 1971 Census, on which the last delimitation (1973) was based.
Economy — Banking

RBI cuts minimum daily CRR maintenance to 90%; Governor sees $490–685 billion AI value for MSMEs

  • The RBI reduced the minimum daily maintenance of the cash reserve ratio from 99% to 90% of the requirement, effective from the fortnight beginning 1 December 2026.
  • The move is expected to release more than ₹40,000 crore into the banking system for lending; banks still have to meet 100% of CRR on a fortnightly average basis.
  • RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra said AI adoption could unlock $490–685 billion of value for MSMEs, but only about 15% of small businesses use it now.
  • Static: CRR is the share of net demand and time liabilities that banks keep with the RBI as cash, under Section 42 of the RBI Act, 1934; it earns no interest.
Defence

Navy receives Vindhyagiri, seventh and final Nilgiri-class (Project 17A) stealth frigate

  • Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata delivered Vindhyagiri (Yard 3024) on 27 November, completing the Project 17A delivery programme.
  • Project 17A has seven frigates: four built by Mazagon Dock (Mumbai) — Nilgiri, Udaygiri, Taragiri, Mahendragiri — and three by GRSE — Himgiri, Dunagiri, Vindhyagiri.
  • They are follow-ons of the Shivalik class (Project 17), designed by the Navy's Warship Design Bureau, with stealth features and BrahMos missiles.
  • Vindhyagiri is named after the Vindhya range.
International Organisations

India to host ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2030 (PP-30)

  • India's bid to host the 2030 Plenipotentiary Conference — the ITU's highest policy-making body — was formally accepted at PP-26, which ended at Doha, Qatar on 27 November, after earlier approval by the ITU Council in Geneva.
  • Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia invited member states through a video message.
  • Static: the ITU (founded 1865, HQ Geneva) is the UN specialised agency for ICT; the Plenipotentiary Conference meets every four years to set policy, elect leadership and adopt the strategic plan.
  • India hosted the ITU World Telecommunication Standardization Assembly (WTSA) in New Delhi in October 2024.
Environment

NBA disburses ₹13.76 crore in Access and Benefit Sharing funds across 28 States and 4 UTs

  • The National Biodiversity Authority released ₹13.76 crore from Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) contributions paid by AYUSH product makers for commercial use of medicinal plants.
  • The money goes to biodiversity conservation and to the communities that conserve these bio-resources.
  • Static: the NBA (Chennai, set up 2003) works under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (amended 2023); State Biodiversity Boards and local Biodiversity Management Committees form the three-tier structure. ABS flows from the Nagoya Protocol (2010) under the CBD.
Infrastructure

Breakthrough in 6.59-km Tunnel-14 of the Rishikesh–Karnaprayag rail line

  • Indian Railways achieved the main tunnel breakthrough of the 6.59-km Tunnel-14 in Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand.
  • The breakthrough joined 4.7 km dug from Portal-1 (Sumerpur) and 1.79 km from Portal-2 (Marora).
  • Static: the 125-km Rishikesh–Karnaprayag line is being built by Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd; most of it runs through tunnels and it is meant to improve access to the Char Dham region and border areas.
Social Justice — Women & Children

Two years of Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat; NAVYA 2.0 launched for adolescent girls

  • The WCD Ministry marked two years of the Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan on 27 November at Vigyan Bhawan with States, Child Marriage Prohibition Officers and Child Welfare Committees.
  • The WCD and Skill Development Ministries launch the second phase of NAVYA (Nurturing Aspirations through Vocational Training for Young Women and Adolescent Girls) at Haridwar on 28 November.
  • NAVYA links adolescent girls to vocational and job-oriented skills under the Skill India framework.
  • Static: the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006 sets 18 years for women and 21 for men.
Art & Culture

Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 begins; 39 Naga ancestral remains return from Oxford

  • The fifth Kashi Tamil Sangamam begins in Varanasi with the theme 'Thirukkural Payilvom' (Let us learn Thirukkural), the classic by Thiruvalluvar.
  • The Sangamam, under Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat, celebrates links between Tamil Nadu and Kashi; the first edition was in 2022.
  • A programme in Kohima welcomes 39 Naga ancestral human remains repatriated from Oxford University's Pitt Rivers Museum after nearly a century.
  • Static: Thirukkural has 1,330 couplets in three books — Aram (virtue), Porul (wealth) and Inbam (love).
Polity — Reservation

Allahabad HC sets aside UPPSC PCS-2025 prelims result over wrong exclusion of meritorious reserved candidates

  • The High Court held that UPPSC wrongly kept meritorious reserved-category candidates out of the unreserved list while preparing category-wise merit lists.
  • It directed the Commission to redraw the preliminary merit list and hold the mains afresh on the revised results; the petitioners were OBC and EWS candidates.
  • Principle: the 'open category' is open to all on merit; a reserved candidate who qualifies on merit must be counted in the general list (Indra Sawhney, 1992; Saurav Yadav v. State of UP, 2020).
  • Rajasthan link: the same 'meritorious reserved candidate' rule is often litigated in RPSC recruitments.
Sports

India in Women's Asian Champions Trophy final; tops BIMSTEC U-23 athletics; Gukesh vs Sindarov for world chess title

  • Hockey: India beat Malaysia 6-2 at Bhopal to reach the final of the Women's Asian Champions Trophy, with Deepika starring in drag-flicks.
  • Athletics: India topped the BIMSTEC Under-23 International Athletics Meet at Kalinga Stadium, Bhubaneswar with 47 medals.
  • Chess: D. Gukesh is defending his world title against Javokhir Sindarov (Uzbekistan) at Cologny, Geneva; Gukesh became the youngest world champion in 2024.

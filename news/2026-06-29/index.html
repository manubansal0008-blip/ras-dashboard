International Relations

India and Seychelles unveil 19 outcomes; Modi calls Indian Ocean 'our shared home'

  • PM Modi held talks with President Patrick Herminie in Victoria and addressed the National Assembly of the Seychelles.
  • The 19 outcomes span defence, maritime security, space, healthcare, agriculture, digital payments and connectivity.
  • India announced the refit of PS Zoroaster for the Seychelles Coast Guard and a Dornier glass-cockpit upgrade.
  • Outcomes included an extradition treaty, outer-space cooperation and UPI-based digital payments.
  • Modi was conferred 'Guardian of the Blue Horizon' and India gifted a fast patrol vessel and Laser Radial class boats.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Economy

India–UK CETA to enter into force on 15 July 2026

  • The Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement makes 99% of UK tariff lines duty-free for Indian products.
  • India will remove or reduce tariffs on about 90% of its tariff lines for UK products.
  • Spanning 30 chapters, it is forecast to add £5.1 billion to India's GDP and £4.8 billion to the UK's, and raise bilateral trade by £25.5 billion a year.
  • It includes India's first-ever standalone chapters on anti-corruption, gender and development.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Geography

Tungabhadra dam's replacement gates inaugurated, marking inter-State cooperation

  • Located in Koppal district, Karnataka, the dam on the Tungabhadra river is a lifeline of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
  • It irrigates about 16.4 lakh acres — 9.26 lakh in Karnataka, 6.25 lakh in Andhra Pradesh and 87,000 in Telangana.
  • New high-grade steel gates, expected to last 60 years, were installed at about ₹51 crore after a gate was washed away in August 2024.
  • Siltation has reduced the dam's storage capacity from 133 tmc ft to about 106 tmc ft.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Environment

Suspected explosive kills elephant; spotlight on Kerala's mortality hotspots

  • A tuskless makhna elephant died in the Malayattoor forests of Ernakulam, the second such case in two months.
  • A study led by then Chief Wildlife Warden Pramod G. Krishnan found 744 wild elephants died between April 2019 and March 2025.
  • Electrocution was the leading unnatural cause; human-induced causes accounted for 77 deaths.
  • Juvenile elephants fall victim to explosive traps set for wild boar in the Malayattoor sector.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Polity & Law

One year on, Delhi's eOffice platform speeds up public service delivery

  • Launched on 1 July 2025, the eOffice system now covers 235 departments and offices, with over 75% using it regularly.
  • Over 1.4 lakh e-files and more than 9.2 lakh e-receipts have been processed since July 2025.
  • The number of active users stands at 15,748; 177 of 235 offices use the platform regularly.
  • CM Rekha Gupta said it has accelerated file disposal and improved transparency and accountability.

Source: The Hindu, 29 Jun 2026

Rajasthan

Rajasthan's water self-reliance push: Yamuna MoU with Haryana and a 265-km Shekhawati pipeline

  • Rajasthan has only about 1.16% of India's surface water while spanning nearly 10% of its land area, making water self-reliance a priority.
  • A Rajasthan–Haryana MoU on Yamuna water sharing was signed in the presence of the Union Home Minister; Rajasthan gets its Hathnikund Barrage share per the 1994 agreement.
  • A 265-km underground pipeline (about Rs 32,000 crore) is planned to carry water to the drought-prone Shekhawati districts — Sikar, Jhunjhunu and Churu.
  • Conservation continues through the CM Water Self-Reliance Campaign 2.0, the Vande Ganga Water Conservation Campaign and the Amrit Sarovar scheme.

Source: ANI News, June 2026

Rajasthan

On International MSME Day, Rajasthan unveils RIPS-2024 incentives and new industrial policy

  • On 27 June 2026 the CM launched the Rajasthan Industrial Development Policy and the RAMP (Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance) Portal in Jaipur.
  • Rajasthan was described as the country's fourth-largest MSME base with more than 33 lakh enterprises.
  • New measures include support for 25 startups via the Rajasthan Venture Capital Fund and reducing the RIPS capital subsidy period from 10 to seven years.
  • Ease-of-business reforms: land-use conversion approval cut from 60 to 30 days and industrial-operation permission from 120 to 30 days.

Source: IANS, June 2026

RAS News Digest — 25 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 25 November 2026.

Economy

Banks wrote off ₹18.08 lakh crore of loans in 10 years; FY26 write-offs fall 8.2%

  • Indian banks wrote off ₹1.44 lakh crore of loans in FY26, down 8.2% from ₹1.57 lakh crore in FY25, as per RBI data obtained under RTI.
  • Between 2016-17 and 2025-26, banks wrote off a total of ₹18.08 lakh crore.
  • Write-offs peaked at ₹2.39 lakh crore in 2018-19 and stayed above ₹2 lakh crore in the next two years.
  • A write-off removes a bad loan from the bank's books and lowers reported NPAs, but the borrower's liability is not extinguished and recovery continues.
  • A loan becomes a Non-Performing Asset when interest or principal stays overdue for more than 90 days.
Polity & Governance

Supreme Court clears the way for bike taxis in Karnataka

  • On 20 November 2026 the Supreme Court dismissed Karnataka's appeal against the High Court order allowing motorcycles to be registered as transport vehicles.
  • In January 2026 a Division Bench of the Karnataka High Court had held that motorcycles fall within 'transport vehicles' under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
  • Karnataka had argued that bike taxis are unsafe and lack insurance cover for passengers.
  • CSE estimated that urban bus availability nationwide is about 70% short of the MoHUA benchmark.
Environment

Former bureaucrats seek scrapping of Demwe Lower hydro project on the Lohit

  • A group of former civil servants has sought cancellation of the Demwe Lower Hydroelectric Project on the Lohit river in Arunachal Pradesh.
  • The project needs diversion of 1,416 hectares of forest land, would submerge about 1,590 hectares and fell around 43,000 trees.
  • The letter was also sent to the Arunachal CM and the Standing Committee of the National Board for Wildlife.
  • The Lohit is a major tributary of the Brahmaputra, and the Parshuram Kund pilgrimage site lies on its banks.
Art & Culture

'Dancing Girl' of Mohenjodaro to anchor first gallery of Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum

  • The first gallery of the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum, titled 'Adornment', will display the bronze 'Dancing Girl' of Mohenjodaro.
  • It will also show the Nizam's jewellery from RBI vaults and gemstone ornaments from the Taxila excavations — over 1,000 pieces in all.
  • The gallery is to open on the lower level of North Block, New Delhi, by August 2027.
  • The 'Dancing Girl' is a roughly 4,500-year-old bronze made by the lost-wax technique and is kept in the National Museum, New Delhi.
Science & Technology

BepiColombo begins orbiting Mercury; India's space economy to grow to USD 40–45 billion

  • BepiColombo, a joint mission of the European Space Agency and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, has begun orbiting Mercury.
  • It is only the third spacecraft to visit Mercury and the second to orbit it; ESA reported a thruster glitch on 22 November.
  • Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh said India's space economy is set to grow to USD 40–45 billion, driven by the private sector.
  • Indian start-ups GalaxEye and Bellatrix Aerospace signed what was described as the largest B2B deal of its kind on satellite systems.
Important Days

Lachit Diwas, Dev Deepawali and National Milk Day

  • Lachit Diwas was observed on 24 November to honour Ahom general Lachit Borphukan, the hero of the Battle of Saraighat (1671) against the Mughals.
  • Dev Deepawali was celebrated on Kartik Purnima in Varanasi, with lakhs of lamps lit on 84 ghats of the Ganga.
  • National Milk Day falls on 26 November, the birth anniversary of Dr. Verghese Kurien, 'Father of the White Revolution'; this year's national event is in Patna, Bihar.
  • The NDDB was formed in 1965 and launched Operation Flood in 1970.
Social Issues

Women spend over 5 hours a day on unpaid domestic work, men just 56 minutes

  • Research by 'Data for India' shows women in India spend more than 5 hours a day on domestic work and caregiving, against 56 minutes for men.
  • Unpaid care work is a major reason for India's low female labour force participation.
  • A Professor's op-ed in The Hindu argues that the revamped monthly PLFS, with a much larger sample, gives monthly estimates that do not differ significantly across most months.
  • The PLFS is conducted by the National Statistics Office under MoSPI.
Sports

Gukesh holds Sindarov in Game 1; Sooryavanshi hits record 28 sixes

  • Defending champion D. Gukesh drew Game 1 of the World Chess Championship against Javokhir Sindarov in Geneva after a time-pressure battle.
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi (Bihar) hit a double century off 52 balls with 28 sixes for India in the Asia Cup Rising Stars in Doha.
  • His 28 sixes broke the record of 23 sixes in a single innings set by Australia's D'Arcy Short in 2018.
  • The Indian Olympic Association will elect a new president on 5 December as P.T. Usha is not in the fray.
Mapping

Lohit river — in news for the Demwe Lower project

  • The Lohit rises in eastern Tibet (Zayul region) and enters India in Arunachal Pradesh near Kibithoo.
  • It flows through the Lohit and Anjaw districts and joins the Siang and Dibang near Sadiya in Assam to form the Brahmaputra.
  • The Dhola–Sadiya (Bhupen Hazarika) bridge, among India's longest river bridges, spans the Lohit.
  • Parshuram Kund on the Lohit draws pilgrims on Makar Sankranti.

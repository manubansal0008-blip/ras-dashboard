Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 28 June 2026.

International Relations

PM Modi hands 'Made in India' patrol vessel PS LESPWAR to Seychelles

  • During his State Visit, PM Modi handed over a Made-in-India Fast Patrol Vessel (FPV), PS LESPWAR, to Seychelles.
  • The vessel was built by Goa Shipyard Limited; India also gave 6 ambulances, 10 utility vehicles and 5 laser radial boats.
  • The ceremony was held with Seychelles President Patrick Herminie at the Coast Guard Base, Victoria, to boost EEZ surveillance.

Source: The Hindu / MEA, 28 June 2026

Polity & Governance

ECI's Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls underway

  • The Election Commission of India is carrying out a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, with Booth Level Officers (BLOs) verifying crores of electors.
  • SIR Phase-III, announced on 14 May 2026, covers 16 states including Karnataka and Maharashtra.
  • In May 2026 the Supreme Court had upheld the SIR as consistent with the Representation of the People Act.

Source: The Hindu / ECI, 28 June 2026

Environment & Ecology

Protecting the Asiatic elephant safeguards India's forest carbon stores: study

  • A new scientific study finds that protecting the endangered Asiatic elephant indirectly safeguards India's forests, which act as powerful carbon stores.
  • A linked modelling study projects India's vegetation carbon could rise about 35%, 62% or 97% by 2100 under low, medium or high emission pathways, driven by higher rainfall and CO2.
  • The Asiatic elephant is listed as 'Endangered' on the IUCN Red List and is India's National Heritage Animal.

Source: The Hindu, 28 June 2026

National

Ram Janmabhoomi Trust: general secretary Champat Rai resigns amid donation case

  • Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust general secretary Champat Rai and member Anil Mishra submitted resignations amid a donation-embezzlement case.
  • Champat Rai is also a vice-president of the Vishwa Hindu Parishad (VHP).
  • Trust treasurer Govind Dev Giri said the trust would ensure a fair investigation; the Ram Temple is at Ayodhya.

Source: The Hindu, 28 June 2026

Economy

Amit Shah criticises app-based cab aggregators over 'unfair' model

  • Union Cooperation Minister Amit Shah criticised private app-based cab aggregators such as Uber and Ola, alleging their model exploits drivers and commuters.
  • He pitched cooperative-based mobility as an alternative; the Ministry of Cooperation was created in 2021.
  • Amit Shah holds both the Home and the Cooperation portfolios in the Union Cabinet.

Source: The Hindu, 28 June 2026

Education

Delhi University opens UG admissions for ~71,600 seats

  • Delhi University opened the first phase of its three-phase undergraduate (UG) admission process for the 2026-27 session.
  • Admissions are for nearly 71,600 seats across 67 colleges.
  • Admission is through the Common University Entrance Test (CUET-UG) based CSAS portal.

Source: The Hindu, 28 June 2026

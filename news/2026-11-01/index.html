RAS News Digest — 1 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 1 November 2026.

Defence / Appointments

Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit takes charge as the 29th Chief of the Air Staff

  • He assumed charge on 31 October 2026, after serving as Vice Chief of the Air Staff from July 2026.
  • He is a fighter pilot and earlier served at HQ Integrated Defence Staff.
  • Also on 1 November, Air Marshal Mehtab Singh Deswal took over as AOC-in-C, Eastern Air Command.
Science & Technology / Space

ISRO's GSLV-F18 places NVS-03 navigation satellite in orbit, strengthening NavIC

  • GSLV-F18 lifted off from Sriharikota on 30 October 2026; the first orbit-raising manoeuvre was done on 31 October.
  • NVS-03 is a second-generation NavIC satellite carrying an indigenous atomic clock.
  • NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) is India's regional satellite navigation system.
Economy / Poverty

World Bank: India's extreme poverty at 2.6% (2023)

  • At the international extreme-poverty line of $3 a day, 2.6% of Indians were poor in 2023 (Poverty and Equity Brief).
  • At the lower-middle-income line of $4.20 a day, poverty was 16.1% in 2023, estimated at 14.2% in 2026.
  • The World Bank described India's poverty reduction as remarkable progress.
Economy / Social Security

EPFO turns 74: PF services on WhatsApp and auto-settlement of small inoperative accounts

  • Union Labour Minister Mansukh Mandaviya launched the services in New Delhi on 1 November 2026.
  • Aadhaar-verified inoperative accounts with up to ₹1,000 will be settled without a claim — about 1 lakh accounts worth ₹5 crore.
  • EPFO serves about 8 crore contributing members and 85 lakh pensioners.
Important Days

1 November: Foundation Day of Haryana, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh; Haryana enters its 60th year

  • Haryana was carved out of Punjab on 1 November 1966 under the Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966; Chandigarh became a Union Territory the same day.
  • Madhya Pradesh was formed on 1 November 1956 (States Reorganisation) and Chhattisgarh on 1 November 2000.
  • President Droupadi Murmu attended Haryana's Hirak Jayanti event at Kurukshetra.

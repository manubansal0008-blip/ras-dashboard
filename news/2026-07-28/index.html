Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 28 July 2026.

Awards — Science

Fields Medal 2026: Hong Wang becomes the third woman winner

  • The Fields Medal 2026 was awarded to Yu Deng, John Pardon, Jacob Tsimerman and Hong Wang.
  • The Fields Medal is awarded by the International Mathematical Union every four years.
  • The Fields Medal is given to mathematicians under the age of 40.
  • Hong Wang is the third woman to win, after Maryam Mirzakhani (2014) and Maryna Viazovska (2022).
  • Indian-origin winners include Manjul Bhargava (2014) and Akshay Venkatesh (2018).
Health

World Hepatitis Day observed on 28 July

  • World Hepatitis Day is observed on 28 July every year.
  • The 2026 theme was 'Hepatitis: Let's Break It Down'.
  • India has an estimated 29 million people with chronic hepatitis B.
  • India's National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme was launched in 2018.
  • Hepatitis B can be prevented by vaccination, including a birth dose.
Industry

Cabinet approves BHAVYA Rasayan scheme for three chemical parks

  • The Union Cabinet approved the BHAVYA Rasayan scheme to set up three dedicated chemical parks.
  • The total outlay of BHAVYA Rasayan is ₹3,030 crore.
  • The Centre will give up to ₹1,000 crore per park, with at least ₹500 crore from the State.
  • The scheme runs for five years, from FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31.
  • Each park needs at least 2,000 acres of contiguous land.

RAS News Digest — 27 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 27 November 2026.

Polity — Parliament

Special sitting of Parliament from 30 November to take up amended Delimitation Bill and women's quota

  • The Centre has called a special sitting of both Houses: Lok Sabha from 30 November, Rajya Sabha from 2 December, both ending 3 December. The Monsoon Session had been adjourned sine die on 13 August without prorogation (104 days earlier).
  • Reported features of the amended Bill: a uniform 50% increase in each State's Lok Sabha seats; a 25-year extension of the freeze on using the 1971 Census for inter-State seat shares (till about 2051); and use of the 2011 Census to redraw constituencies within States.
  • The package aims to make 33% reservation for women in the Lok Sabha, State Assemblies and UTs effective before the 2029 general election; the Lok Sabha could expand from 543 to as many as about 850 seats.
  • A constitutional amendment needs a special majority (Article 368): a majority of total membership and two-thirds of members present and voting in each House.
  • Static: Art. 81 (composition of Lok Sabha, now capped at 550 elected seats), Art. 82 (readjustment after each census), Art. 170 (Assemblies). The 42nd Amendment (1976) froze seat shares on the 1971 Census till 2001; the 84th (2001) extended it till the first census after 2026. The 106th Amendment (2023, Nari Shakti Vandan) links women's reservation to delimitation.
Polity — Judiciary

SC: PMLA Adjudicating Authority Bench must include a judicial member; FSSAI told to finalise front-of-pack labels in 4 months

  • A three-judge Bench headed by CJI Surya Kant held that the Adjudicating Authority under the PMLA, 2002 — which decides whether property is 'proceeds of crime' and confirms its attachment — must have at least one judicial member on the Bench.
  • The ruling came on petitions from various High Courts on the composition of the Authority, which can attach property even before a trial ends.
  • In a separate PIL by the NGO 3S and Our Health Society, a Bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K. Vinod Chandran gave FSSAI four months to finalise front-of-pack nutrition labelling rules for packaged food.
  • Static: FSSAI was set up under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and works under the Health Ministry; front-of-pack labels warn consumers about high sugar, salt and fat.
Federalism — Mining

Jharkhand Assembly seeks rollback of the amended MMDR Act, citing ₹13,000-crore annual revenue loss

  • The Jharkhand Assembly passed, by voice vote, a resolution moved by CM Hemant Soren seeking withdrawal of the amendments to the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 passed by Parliament in August.
  • The State says the changes curtail State governments' powers and could cost it about ₹13,000 crore a year, hurting welfare spending.
  • Static: 'Regulation of mines and mineral development' is in the Union List (Entry 54) to the extent Parliament declares by law; States can legislate under State List Entry 23 subject to that. Royalty is fixed by the Centre, but collected by States.
  • In Mineral Area Development Authority v. Steel Authority of India (July 2024), a nine-judge SC Bench held that royalty is not a tax and States can tax mineral rights.
Agriculture

Record foodgrain output of 378.53 million tonnes in 2025-26 (final estimates)

  • Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan released the fourth and final production estimates for 2025-26: foodgrains at a record 378.53 million tonnes.
  • This is 208 lakh tonnes (20.8 million tonnes) more than in 2024-25.
  • Static: estimates are prepared by the Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES), Ministry of Agriculture; foodgrains include cereals (rice, wheat, coarse/millets) and pulses.
  • Rajasthan link: Rajasthan is a leading producer of bajra, mustard (rapeseed) and guar.
Health

ICMR survey: nearly two-thirds of Indian adolescents are deficient in vitamin B12

  • A new national survey by ICMR finds nearly two-thirds of adolescents deficient in vitamin B12.
  • B12 is essential for brain development, nerve function and the formation of red blood cells; deficiency can cause anaemia and neurological problems.
  • The findings raise concern about effects across generations, especially among children, adolescents and pregnant women.
  • Static: B12 is found mainly in animal foods (milk, eggs, meat), so largely vegetarian diets raise the risk; Anaemia Mukt Bharat under POSHAN Abhiyaan targets adolescents with iron-folic acid supplementation.
Science & Technology

Chariot-1 sovereign foundation models launched under IndiaAI Mission; C-DAC targets indigenous GPU by mid-2028

  • MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan launched the Chariot-1 Foundation Models, developed by Chariot Artificial Intelligence Pvt. Ltd., at Bharat Mandapam (PIB, 26 Nov).
  • Chariot AI is among the first four teams selected under the IndiaAI Mission to build sovereign foundation models; Chariot-1 focuses on multilingual language processing and speech generation for Indian languages.
  • C-DAC aims to develop a full-fledged indigenous graphics processing unit (GPU) by mid-2028 and is seeking private partners for fabrication, packaging, testing and commercialisation.
  • Static: the IndiaAI Mission was approved in March 2024 with an outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore; its pillars include compute capacity, foundation models, datasets (AIKosh) and skilling.
Defence & IR

India–UAE agree to speed up co-development and co-production of defence equipment at 14th JDCC

  • The 14th India–UAE Joint Defence Cooperation Committee met in Abu Dhabi on 26 November, co-chaired by Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh and UAE Under Secretary of Defence Lt Gen Ibrahim Nasser M. Al Alawi.
  • It gave direction to the India–UAE Strategic Defence Partnership Framework signed in May 2026 during the PM's visit to the UAE.
  • Focus areas: defence industry, innovation and advanced technology, AI, cyber, military training and maritime safety, with faster co-development and co-production of next-generation equipment.
  • Static: India and the UAE have a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (2017) and a CEPA in force since May 2022; joint exercises include Desert Cyclone (army) and Desert Eagle (air force).
PIB in Brief

Constitution in all 22 Scheduled languages; WOAH evaluation of India's aquatic animal health services

  • Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan released updated editions of the Constitution of India in all 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule at Vigyan Bhawan on Constitution Day.
  • The Department of Fisheries began the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) Performance of Veterinary Services (PVS) evaluation of India's Aquatic Animal Health Services, to align them with international standards.
  • Static: WOAH (formerly OIE, founded 1924, HQ Paris) sets animal-health standards recognised by the WTO's SPS Agreement; India is the world's second-largest fish producer.
International

Iran's Revolutionary Guards say two oil tankers hit sea mines near Hormuz

  • Iran's IRGC said two oil tankers were hit by sea mines; a day earlier Iran had warned of mines in the Strait of Hormuz and asked ships to coordinate transit.
  • Static: the Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea; roughly a fifth of world oil trade passes through it.
  • India angle: a large share of India's crude and LPG imports from West Asia passes through the strait, so disruption raises freight, insurance and fuel costs.

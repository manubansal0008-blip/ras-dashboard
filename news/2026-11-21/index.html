RAS News Digest — 21 November 2026

Newspaper-based news digest for the RPSC RAS exam, dated 21 November 2026.

International — SCO

PM inaugurates first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi: 'cultural ties strongest guarantee for peace'

  • India hosted the first-ever SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi on 20 November; PM Narendra Modi inaugurated it.
  • PM Modi had proposed setting up this forum at the 2025 SCO Summit in Tianjin (China).
  • He said that at a time of war and unrest, cultural connections are the strongest guarantee for global peace, and urged members to use technology for connections and shared heritage.
  • All SCO members, observers and dialogue partners were invited. Pakistan's climate change minister Musadik Masood Malik led its delegation — the first Pakistani minister to visit India since Operation Sindoor.
  • MEA said India's position on the Indus Waters Treaty (1960), kept in abeyance after the Pahalgam attack, has not changed.
International Relations — India-Nepal

Nepal PM Balendra Shah to make first state visit to India on 22–23 November

  • MEA announced the state visit of Nepal PM Balendra Shah ('Balen') to India on 22–23 November.
  • It is his first visit to India since assuming office in March 2026; he will hold talks with PM Modi and call on President Droupadi Murmu.
  • MEA said the visit reflects India's 'Neighbourhood First' policy; the two sides will review trade, development partnership, energy, water resources and connectivity.
  • The visit follows India's relief and rescue assistance to Nepal after floods this year; the boundary issue is expected to be raised.
International — Nuclear Arms

Trump says nuclear arms limits with China and Russia 'very hard to achieve'

  • After the trilateral summit with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in China, US President Donald Trump said a mutual agreement to limit nuclear weapons would be hard but he would keep working on it.
  • The leaders' joint statement reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.
  • Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists: China had about 410 warheads in 2023 against 3,708 of the US; Russia has the world's largest arsenal, about 5,420 warheads (March 2026).
  • The Pentagon projects China will have more than 1,000 operational warheads by 2030.
International — UK-EU

UK and EU sign trade, emissions and youth-mobility deals, pledge to rebuild ties after Brexit

  • At a summit in Brussels on 20 November, the UK and the EU agreed deals on trade, carbon emissions and a youth movement scheme.
  • Checks on trade in plant and animal products will be scrapped, and the two sides will link their emissions trading schemes.
  • They pledged an 'ambitious and dynamic' post-Brexit relationship.
  • Static: the UK formally left the EU on 31 January 2020 after the 2016 referendum.
Economy — Core Industries

Core sector grows 10.9% in October, strongest in new series; forex reserves fall to $705.3 billion

  • Output of core industries grew 10.9% in October 2026 (September revised up to 8.2%) — the strongest in the new series.
  • Growth was led by iron ore (26.4%), electricity (21.6%), cement (15.4%) and steel (7.3%); natural gas fell 7.6%.
  • April–October growth was 6%, against 2.3% a year ago.
  • The new Index of Core Industries (base 2022-23), compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, added iron ore as the ninth industry; the nine together carry 32.88% weight in IIP.
  • RBI data: forex reserves fell by over $14 billion to $705.3 billion (week ended 13 November); the rupee recovered to 92.67 per dollar on record foreign inflows.
Health — Public Spending

National Health Accounts 2023-24: Sikkim spends most per person on health, Bihar least

  • Government health expenditure (GHE) was ₹4.1 lakh crore in 2023-24, or ₹2,959 per person.
  • GHE was 1.43% of GDP and 43.5% of total health expenditure.
  • Among states, Sikkim spent the most per person (₹16,026) and Bihar the least (₹1,126); UP spent ₹1,399. Among UTs, Chandigarh led with ₹24,187.
  • North-eastern states averaged about ₹3,501 per person and UTs about ₹7,209.
  • Experts link the gaps to fiscal capacity, population size, past investment and terrain.
Polity — UCC

Supreme Court asks Centre to consider a law to curb marriages between close relatives

  • The Supreme Court said the Union government must explore the legislative route to prevent marriages between close relatives, to protect children from genetic disorders.
  • The case was brought by a 20-year-old NLSIU law student, born to first-cousin parents, who suffers from serious genetic conditions.
  • The court noted such marriages are prevalent among Muslims and some Hindu communities in the south; the remarks were seen as a fresh push for a Uniform Civil Code.
  • Static: UCC is a Directive Principle under Article 44; Uttarakhand became the first state to implement a UCC (January 2025).
Judiciary — Key Rulings

SC: soldier hurt by own troops' firing gets injury pension; HP High Court: 12-week maternity leave even for third child

  • The Supreme Court ruled that a soldier injured by accidental firing from his own gun or by his own troops during an operation is also entitled to war injury pension, not only those hit by enemy fire.
  • The Himachal Pradesh High Court held that a minimum of 12 weeks' maternity leave is available even for the birth of a third child, helping a government teacher whose leave was rejected.
  • The Supreme Court, as a one-time measure, allowed lighting of the lamp on the Thiruparankundram hilltop in Tamil Nadu (Karthigai Deepam).
Elections — Electoral Rolls

Election Commission pauses Special Summary Revision of rolls in West Bengal

  • The Special Summary Revision (SSR) of electoral rolls in West Bengal, due to start on 23 November, has been postponed citing 'unavoidable circumstances'.
  • The draft roll will not be published on Monday, and the final roll, earlier due on 16 January, will be delayed.
  • Officials said rationalisation of polling stations, due by 13 November, was not complete; the exercise continues in 13 other states and three UTs.
  • Separately, opposition leaders want to use 'interrogatories' to seek answers from the EC on roll revision; the Supreme Court will decide which questions are allowed.
Education — Higher Education

UGC list of fake universities grows from 20 to 32 in three years; Delhi has the most

  • The University Grants Commission issued a fresh public advisory on 17 November, following Supreme Court directions, warning against unrecognised institutions.
  • Its list of fake universities has grown from 20 to 32 in three years; 18 of the 20 names flagged in March 2023 are still on the list.
  • Delhi accounts for the largest number of such institutions.
  • UGC asked students to verify recognition independently on official regulatory websites.
Society — Demography

Pew projections: three in four Indians will still be Hindu in 2070; Muslim share to rise to about 20%

  • Pew Research Center's 'Future of World Religions' projections say India will add about 17.6 crore Hindus and 12.1 crore Muslims between 2020 and 2070.
  • In 2070, Hindus are projected at 75.8% (from 79.4% in 2020) and Muslims at 19.6% (from 15.2%) of India's population.
  • India will have the world's second-largest Muslim population; India alone holds 94.5% of the world's Hindus today.
  • Worldwide, Hindus rise from 117.8 crore to 137.7 crore but their share of humanity slips from 14.9% to 13.6%. Falling fertility may make all these numbers too high.
Rajasthan — Panchayati Raj

Panchayat poll results: Congress wins majority in 23 of 37 zila parishads and 229 panchayat samitis

  • Counting was held for 37 of Rajasthan's 41 zila parishads and 419 panchayat samitis, which went to the polls in four phases.
  • Congress won a majority in 23 zila parishads, BJP in 5 and RLP in 1 (Nagaur). In panchayat samitis: Congress 229, BJP 51, RLP 14, BAP 11.
  • Of 1,271 zila parishad seats declared, Congress won 794 (62.3%) and BJP 357; Congress got 43.4% of votes against BJP's 37.8%.
  • Zila pramukhs will be elected on 25 November and pradhans on 27 November; repolling at 28 booths on 22 November due to EVM snags.
  • In the September urban local body polls, BJP had won more wards (4,179 vs 3,613).
Rajasthan — Heritage

Ahead of UNESCO's 1 December review, state reconstitutes two heritage committees for Jaipur's Walled City

  • UNESCO has sought details of measures to protect the Walled City by 1 December; if not satisfied, it could send a Reactive Monitoring Mission.
  • The state reconstituted the State Heritage Committee (headed by Chief Secretary V. Srinivas) and a Technical Heritage Committee, after the merger of Jaipur Greater and Jaipur Heritage municipal corporations.
  • Concerns include demolition of havelis, alterations to historic buildings and unauthorised construction.
  • Lok Manthan-2026 will be held at Jawahar Kala Kendra, Jaipur on 3–6 December (₹20 crore sanctioned); a Shekhawati Heritage Meet will be held at Khetri on 8 December.
Rajasthan — Fairs & Festivals

Pushkar religious fair begins with Panchtirth snan; Khatu Shyam birth anniversary draws lakhs

  • The five-day Pushkar religious fair began on Dev Uthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi with the first Panchtirth snan; over one lakh devotees bathed in Brahma Sarovar, more than 75% of them women.
  • A Shahi snan of saints will be held on Brahma Chaturdashi (23 November); the fair ends on Kartik Purnima.
  • At Khatushyamji (Sikar), lakhs of devotees celebrated Baba Shyam's birth anniversary on Dev Uthani Ekadashi, with padyatras from Ringas.
  • Static: Pushkar has one of the few temples of Lord Brahma; Khatu Shyam is identified with Barbarik.
Sports — Chess

Gukesh to defend world chess title against Javokhir Sindarov in Geneva from Tuesday

  • D. Gukesh will defend his World Chess Championship title against Uzbek grandmaster Javokhir Sindarov in Geneva, starting Tuesday (24 November).
  • Viswanathan Anand sent Gukesh advice ahead of the match.
  • Gukesh became the youngest undisputed world champion in December 2024, beating Ding Liren in Singapore.
  • Other sport: India 'A' beat Nepal by 306 runs; Satwik–Chirag entered the Hong Kong Open semi-finals.

Editorial Analysis — the way toppers read संपादकीय विश्लेषण
Pachpadra Refinery: 'Registhan to Refinesthan' · पचपदरा रिफाइनरी: 'रेगिस्तान से रिफाइनस्थान'
On 4 July 2026 PM Modi dedicated the HPCL Rajasthan Refinery at Pachpadra (Balotra) to the nation — India's first integrated refinery-cum-petrochemical complex — as part of a ~Rs 1.05 lakh crore package of works for Rajasthan.
4 जुलाई 2026 को प्रधानमंत्री मोदी ने पचपदरा (बालोतरा) की एचपीसीएल राजस्थान रिफाइनरी — भारत की पहली एकीकृत रिफाइनरी-सह-पेट्रोकेमिकल कॉम्प्लेक्स — राष्ट्र को समर्पित की, जो राजस्थान हेतु ~₹1.05 लाख करोड़ के कार्यों का हिस्सा है।
Key numbers · मुख्य आँकड़े
Nelson Complexity Index 17.0 — the highest of any Indian refinery; produces 14 grades of polyethylene and 21 of polypropylene (India's largest polypropylene unit).
A 1,095-km pipeline network feeds it (crude line Pachpadra-Mundra 489 km; raw-water line Nachna-Pachpadra 230 km) plus 1.5 MMTPA of local Barmer (Mangala) crude.
It anchors a downstream ecosystem — a Petrozone, plastic park and logistics hub — feeding plastics, packaging, textiles and auto-part industries.
Global context (IEA): world refining capacity may fall 1-1.5 million barrels/day by 2030 as older refineries in the US, Europe and China shut.
The debate — is a mega-refinery the right bet for arid Rajasthan?
✗ Concerns · चिंताएँ
  • Yet a water-hungry, emissions-heavy plant in an arid zone needs strict green safeguards.
  • And a global peak in oil demand could leave costly assets stranded over time.
✓ Merits · गुण
  • It cuts import dependence and captures value by making petrochemicals at home.
  • It industrialises western Rajasthan — ~Rs 21,000 cr/yr revenue and tens of thousands of jobs.
  • It builds a downstream cluster (plastics, textiles, auto) via the Petrozone and plastic park.
  • Global refining capacity is shrinking, so a modern high-complexity refinery is strategically timed.
  • It monetises local Barmer crude and deepens India's energy security.

Mains · 10 marks · 150 words
The Pachpadra refinery marks a turning point for Rajasthan's economy but raises sustainability concerns in an arid region. Discuss.
पचपदरा रिफाइनरी राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था हेतु मोड़ है किंतु शुष्क क्षेत्र में स्थिरता संबंधी चिंताएँ उठाती है। विवेचना कीजिए।
Your answer · आपका उत्तर
The dedication of the HPCL Rajasthan Refinery (HRRL) at Pachpadra, Balotra, is a landmark in Rajasthan's industrial journey and the state's first integrated refinery-cum-petrochemical complex — yet it forces a hard question about industrialising an arid region sustainably. Structurally, the Rs 79,459-crore plant is a 74:26 HPCL–Rajasthan joint venture that refines 9 MMTPA of crude and produces 2.4 MTPA of petrochemicals, with a Nelson Complexity Index of 17.0, among India's highest, enabling high-value products rather than plain fuels. Economically it offers import substitution, roughly Rs 21,000 crore in annual revenue and over 35,000 direct and indirect jobs, while a downstream Petrozone anchors plastics, packaging and textiles and local Barmer (Mangala) crude is monetised, deepening energy security. A roughly 1,095-km pipeline network ties it to the Barmer fields and the coast, giving it national, not merely state, significance. However, in a water-scarce, arid zone the refinery's water demand and carbon emissions demand strict safeguards, and a coming global peak in oil demand risks stranding such capital-heavy assets. The way forward lies in mandatory zero-liquid-discharge and recycled water, skilling locals for refinery and downstream jobs, and a progressive pivot toward specialty chemicals and green hydrogen. If ecological guardrails and a specialty-chemical pivot are built in from the start, Pachpadra can transform western Rajasthan's economy while respecting its fragile desert ecology — turning 'Registhan' into a genuine 'Refinesthan'.|

Assertion (A): Rajasthan became the first state in the country to launch 'Outbound Portability' under the Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana.
Reason (R): Under this facility, people of the state can avail cashless treatment even in other states.
Select the correct answer:
कथन (A): मुख्यमंत्री आयुष्मान आरोग्य योजना के अंतर्गत 'आउटबाउंड पोर्टेबिलिटी' आरंभ करने वाला राजस्थान देश का पहला राज्य बना।
कारण (R): इस सुविधा के तहत प्रदेश के लोग अन्य राज्यों में भी कैशलेस इलाज करा सकेंगे।
सही उत्तर चुनिए:
कठिनाई: कठिन
A Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of AA और R दोनों सही हैं तथा R, A की सही व्याख्या है
B Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of AA और R दोनों सही हैं परंतु R, A की सही व्याख्या नहीं है✓
C A is true but R is falseA सही है परंतु R गलत है
D A is false but R is trueA गलत है परंतु R सही है
Correct Answer · सही उत्तर: B
Why? · व्याख्या
The issue confirms that Rajasthan was the first state to launch Outbound Portability under the Ayushman Arogya Yojana, and that under it residents can get cashless treatment in other states too. Both A and R are factually correct. However, R describes what the facility does, not why Rajasthan became the 'first' state; being first is a matter of timing of launch, so R does not by itself explain the assertion of primacy. Hence both are true but R is not the correct explanation.
अंक पुष्टि करता है कि आयुष्मान आरोग्य योजना के अंतर्गत आउटबाउंड पोर्टेबिलिटी आरंभ करने वाला राजस्थान पहला राज्य बना और इसके तहत निवासी अन्य राज्यों में भी कैशलेस इलाज करा सकते हैं। A और R दोनों सही हैं, परंतु R यह बताता है कि सुविधा क्या करती है, न कि राजस्थान 'पहला' क्यों बना; 'पहला' होना आरंभ की समयबद्धता का विषय है। अतः दोनों सही पर R व्याख्या नहीं है।

RAS Exam Date 2026

The RPSC RAS 2026 Preliminary exam is on 6 December 2026 (Sunday) and the Main exam on 1–2 May 2027. Here is the full RAS 2026 schedule — notification, application window, Prelims, Mains and the stages that follow.

RAS Prelims 2026 date

The RAS Preliminary examination will be held on 6 December 2026 (Sunday). RPSC revised the date to 6 December, one week later than the originally proposed 29 November 2026. The Prelims is a single objective paper of 200 marks in 3 hours.

  • Date: 6 December 2026 (Sunday)
  • Single objective paper — 200 marks, 3 hours, 1/3 negative marking
  • Qualifying stage only — marks not counted in the final merit

RAS Mains 2027 date

Candidates who clear the Prelims will sit the Main examination on 1 and 2 May 2027 — four descriptive papers of 200 marks each (800 total).

  • Dates: 1–2 May 2027
  • Four descriptive papers, 200 marks each, 3 hours per paper
  • Followed by a 100-mark interview / personality test

Planning your prep around these dates

  • With Prelims on 6 December 2026, count backwards to set weekly targets for each subject.
  • Keep building Mains-level notes from now — the Mains is barely five months after Prelims (May 2027).
  • Dates can be revised by RPSC — always cross-check the latest notice on the official RPSC website.

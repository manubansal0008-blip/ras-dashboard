RAS Syllabus 2026: What's New and What's Removed

RPSC has revised the RAS syllabus for 2026. The Prelims pattern is unchanged, but the syllabus has been re-organised into 11 sections, with several new topics added and some named topics dropped. Here is a clear, section-wise comparison of the RAS 2026 syllabus with the 2024 syllabus.

The revised RAS syllabus was notified by RPSC, Ajmer on 9 January 2026 and applies to the RAS 2026 exam (Prelims on 6 December 2026). "Removed" below means the topic is no longer named explicitly — it may still be asked under a broader heading.

RAS 2026 syllabus changes at a glance

AreaWhat changed in 2026
Prelims patternNo change — 150 MCQs, 200 marks, 3 hours, 1/3 negative marking, screening only
Prelims syllabusRe-organised into 11 sections (A–K); new topics added and several named topics dropped (details below)
Big new additionRajasthan Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act, 2022 under Current Affairs
Modern Indian HistoryExtended up to 2000 (earlier up to 1964)
Sports & YogaSeparate 'Sports and Yoga' head dropped (sports now appear under Current Affairs)
Mains Paper IVNow in 3 parts — General Hindi 90 + General English 70 + Essay 40 (one ~600-word essay from 6 themes, in Hindi or English)
Mains & Interview totalsUnchanged — 4 papers × 200 = 800; Interview 100; final merit 900

Prelims 2026: section-wise what's new and what's removed

A. History, Art, Culture, Literature, Tradition & Heritage of Rajasthan

Newly added

  • + Sources of Rajasthan History
  • + Monuments
  • + Religious practices

Removed / no longer named

  • − Named dynasties (Guhila, Pratihar, Chauhan, Parmar, Rathore, Sisodiya, Kachchawa) — now 'rulers of major dynasties'
  • − Classical Dance

B. Indian History

Newly added

  • + Indian Knowledge and Value System
  • + British imperialism and resistance (Maratha, Mysore, Sikh)
  • + Revolt of 1857
  • + Post-independence nation-building, planning & economic reforms — modern period now extends to 2000 (earlier 1964)

Removed / no longer named

  • − Development of Language & Literature in Ancient India (Sanskrit, Prakrit, Tamil)

C. Geography of World and India

Newly added

  • + World: Natural Vegetation, Transport Network
  • + India: Irrigation

Removed / no longer named

  • − India: Power Resources
  • − India: National Highways & Transport Corridors

D. Geography of Rajasthan

Newly added

  • + Livestock
  • + Urbanization

Removed / no longer named

  • − Major Industries
  • − Power Resources
  • − Water Conservation
  • − Tourist Circuits

E. Indian Constitution, Political System & Governance

Newly added

  • + Citizenship
  • + Union–State Relations
  • + Emergency provisions
  • + Urban & Rural Local Government
  • + UPSC
  • + National Commission for Women
  • + NCPCR
  • + Social Audit
  • + Grievance Redressal

Removed / no longer named

  • − CAG
  • − Federalism
  • − Coalition Governments
  • − National Integration
  • − Legal Rights

F. Political and Administrative System of Rajasthan

Newly added

  • + Subordinate Courts
  • + Advocate General
  • + Board of Revenue
  • + State Women Commission
  • + Administrative hierarchy (Chief Secretary to Tehsildar)

Removed / no longer named

  • − State Human Rights Commission
  • − 'Public Policy, Legal Rights and Citizen's Charter' as a standalone item

G. Economic Concepts and Indian Economy

Newly added

  • + Fiscal Federalism
  • + Finance Commission
  • + Skill Development & Employment Generation
  • + Agriculture, industry & service-sector development
  • + Sustainable development and HDI-based measurement

Removed / no longer named

  • − Named items dropped: GST, banking, accounting, stock exchange & share market, subsidies/PDS, e-commerce, inflation, Happiness Index

H. Economy of Rajasthan

Newly added

  • + State Budget
  • + Rural Development
  • + Panchayati Raj & State Finance Commission
  • + Basic Social Services (education, health)
  • + Major Welfare Schemes of Government of Rajasthan (generalised)

Removed / no longer named

  • − Detailed beneficiary-wise scheme list
  • − 'Major Development Projects' and 'Growth, Development & Planning' not named

I. Science & Technology

Newly added

  • + Key Govt programmes & policies on S&T
  • + Recent advancements
  • + Contribution of Indians
  • + Indigenization of S&T
  • + Inheritance and variation

Removed / no longer named

  • − Blood Groups & Rh Factor

J. Reasoning & Mental Ability

Newly added

  • + Critical Reasoning
  • + Ranking and Sitting Arrangement
  • + Profit and Loss

Removed / no longer named

  • − Mirror / Water images

K. Current Affairs & Issues (with special reference to Rajasthan)

Newly added

  • + Major Awards, Publications & distinguished Authors
  • + Newly launched welfare & development schemes
  • + Economic and political developments
  • + Rajasthan Public Examination (Measures for Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act, 2022

Removed / no longer named

  • − Nothing explicitly removed

How to use these changes in your preparation

  • Add the Rajasthan Public Examination Act, 2022 to your notes now — it is short and almost certain to be asked.
  • Modern Indian History now runs to 2000: cover planning, economic reforms of 1991 and post-independence nation-building.
  • Polity gains citizenship, Union–State relations, emergency provisions and local government — standard topics, so revise them thoroughly.
  • Economy is framed around concepts (growth, HDI, fiscal federalism, Finance Commission). GST, banking and inflation are no longer named, but can still come under these broader headings — do not drop them entirely.
  • Reasoning adds critical reasoning, seating arrangement and profit & loss; mirror/water images are out.

Source: RPSC RAS Preliminary syllabus dated 09-01-2026 compared with the 2024 syllabus. Always cross-check with the official PDF on rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.

Frequently asked questions

When was the new RAS 2026 syllabus released?

RPSC released the revised RAS syllabus on 9 January 2026. It applies to the RAS 2026 Prelims (6 December 2026) and the Mains (1–2 May 2027).

Has the RAS Prelims exam pattern changed in 2026?

No. The Prelims is still one objective paper of 150 questions for 200 marks in 3 hours, with 1/3 negative marking. Only the syllabus content was revised.

What is the biggest new topic in RAS 2026 Prelims?

The Rajasthan Public Examination (Measures for Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act, 2022, added under Current Affairs. Modern Indian History has also been extended up to 2000.

Which topics were removed from the RAS Prelims syllabus?

Topics no longer named include named Rajasthan dynasties, classical dance, CAG, federalism, coalition governments, blood groups & Rh factor, mirror/water images, and items like GST, banking and inflation in economy. They may still be covered under broader headings.

What changed in RAS Mains Paper IV?

Paper IV (200 marks) is now in three parts: General Hindi 90, General English 70 and an Essay of 40 marks — one essay of about 600 words chosen from six themes, written in Hindi or English.

RAS Officer Salary 2026

An RAS (Rajasthan Administrative Service) officer is appointed at Pay Matrix Level 14 with an entry basic pay of ₹56,100. After Dearness Allowance and House Rent Allowance the gross crosses ₹1,00,000, with a monthly in-hand of roughly ₹84,000–₹88,000 for a new officer. Below is the full RAS salary structure — pay scales, allowances, probation pay and career growth.

Pay scale & levels

An RAS officer's career runs through four broad scales in the Rajasthan pay matrix, with five annual increments raising the basic pay within each level.

  • Ordinary (junior) scale — Level 14 (entry)
  • Senior scale — Level 16
  • Selection scale — Level 19
  • Super-time scale — Level 21

Allowances & perks

On top of the basic pay, an RAS officer receives dearness and housing allowances plus service perks; several field posts also carry official accommodation and a vehicle.

  • Dearness Allowance (DA) — ≈58% of basic, revised periodically
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA) — 20% in Y-cities (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Bikaner, Ajmer), 10% elsewhere — or official accommodation
  • Travel / conveyance allowance and medical benefits
  • Official vehicle, residence and staff for eligible field posts

In-hand salary & growth

The take-home pay rises steadily with annual increments and DA revisions across a career.

  • New officer in-hand: ≈₹84,000–₹88,000 / month
  • During the 2-year probation: fixed ≈₹35,370 / month
  • After ~5 years (Level 14): ≈₹92,000–₹99,000 / month in-hand

Job profile & posts

RAS officers hold key administrative posts in the Rajasthan government at the sub-division, district and secretariat levels.

  • Junior scale (L-14): SDM / ACEM / ACEO
  • Senior & Selection scale (L-16 / L-19): Project Director / OSD / ADM / CEO
  • Super-time scale (L-21): JS / DM
  • Eligible for promotion to the IAS over the career

Why the RAS salary attracts aspirants

  • Stable Level-14 pay with regular DA revisions that protect against inflation.
  • Strong allowances and perks, including accommodation and a vehicle on field posts.
  • A clear promotion ladder from Level 14 to Level 21, and eligibility for promotion to the IAS.
  • Job security, prestige and pension/NPS benefits of a state civil service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the basic pay of an RAS officer?

An RAS officer's entry basic pay is ₹56,100 at Pay Matrix Level 14.

What is the in-hand salary of an RAS officer per month?

A new RAS officer's in-hand salary is roughly ₹84,000–₹88,000 per month, varying with the current DA and city-wise HRA.

What is the RAS salary during probation?

During the 2-year probation, an RAS officer receives a fixed remuneration of about ₹35,370 per month.

What is the highest pay level an RAS officer can reach?

Through promotions an RAS officer can reach the super-time scale (Level 21), and is also eligible for promotion to the IAS.

Do RAS officers get a government house and vehicle?

Eligible field posts such as SDM and ADM carry official accommodation and a vehicle; otherwise HRA is paid in place of housing.

Folk Dances of Rajasthan

Rajasthan's folk dances are best learned by community and region, because that is exactly how the exam frames them. Two facts anchor the topic: Ghoomar is the state's best-known dance (Bhil in origin, later adopted by Rajput women), and Kalbeliya is on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list (2010) — the only Rajasthani dance with that status.

Ghoomar and Kalbeliya — the two you must not miss

Ghoomar takes its name from ghoomna (to spin). Women in flowing ghagras move in a circle, and the dance — Bhil in origin — was absorbed into Rajput court culture, especially in Marwar and Mewar. Kalbeliya belongs to the Kalbeliya community, traditionally snake-charmers; its sinuous, serpentine movements and the been and khanjari accompaniment earned it a place on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010.

  • Ghoomar — from 'ghoomna' (to spin); Bhil origin, adopted by Rajput women
  • Kalbeliya — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, 2010 (the only Rajasthani dance so listed)
  • Kalbeliya instruments: been (pungi) and khanjari; also called the Sapera dance
  • Ghoomar is performed on festivals like Gangaur and Teej
  • Both are women's dances, performed in a circular formation

The skill dances — Bhavai, Terah Taali, Chari

These are the balancing and precision dances. In Bhavai (Udaipur region), a woman dances while balancing a stack of eight or nine earthen pots on her head, often on the rim of a brass plate or a sword. Terah Taali is performed seated by women of the Kamad community in devotion to Baba Ramdev, with thirteen manjeeras (cymbals) tied to the body. In Chari (Kishangarh, Ajmer; Gujjar community), women balance a brass pot with a lit lamp inside.

  • Bhavai — balancing 8–9 pots on the head, sometimes on a sword's edge or a plate rim
  • Terah Taali — Kamad community; thirteen manjeeras; devotion to Baba Ramdev; Pokhran & Deedwana
  • Chari — Gujjar community, Kishangarh (Ajmer); pot with a lit lamp
  • Terah Taali is performed sitting, not standing
  • Bhavai is also the name of a folk-theatre form

Men's and community dances

Gair is a men's Holi dance of the Bhils of Mewar and Marwar, danced in a circle with sticks. Kachhi Ghodi, from the Shekhawati belt, has men riding a dummy horse frame and enacting mock battles — a favourite at weddings. The fire dance of the Jasnathi sect (Bikaner, Churu) has performers stepping barefoot on burning embers to drum beats. Chakri belongs to the Kanjar community of the Hadoti region, and Walar to the Garasia tribe.

  • Gair — Bhil men's dance at Holi; Mewar & Marwar
  • Kachhi Ghodi — Shekhawati; dummy-horse dance performed at weddings
  • Fire dance — Jasnathi sect; Bikaner and Churu; dancing on embers
  • Chakri — Kanjar community, Hadoti region
  • Walar — Garasia tribe; Gawari/Rai — Bhil

How folk dances are asked in the RAS exam

  • Almost every question is a match-the-pairs of dance with community or district. Fix the unmistakable ones: Terah Taali–Kamad, Kalbeliya–Kalbeliya (sapera), Chari–Gujjar, Fire dance–Jasnathi.
  • Kalbeliya's 2010 UNESCO listing is the single most repeated fact in this topic. Note that no other Rajasthani dance carries this tag.
  • Watch the numbers: Terah Taali uses thirteen manjeeras; Bhavai balances eight to nine pots. Examiners swap these figures.
  • Distinguish men's from women's dances — Gair and Kachhi Ghodi are men's; Ghoomar, Chari, Bhavai and Terah Taali are women's.

Frequently asked questions

Which folk dance of Rajasthan is on the UNESCO list?

Kalbeliya, inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010. It is performed by the Kalbeliya (snake-charmer) community.

What is the most famous folk dance of Rajasthan?

Ghoomar. It originated among the Bhils and was later adopted by Rajput women, and is danced in a circle at festivals like Gangaur and Teej.

Which community performs the Terah Taali dance?

Women of the Kamad community, in devotion to Baba Ramdev. It is performed sitting, with thirteen manjeeras (cymbals) tied to the body.

What is the Bhavai dance?

A balancing dance of the Udaipur region in which a woman dances with eight or nine earthen pots stacked on her head, sometimes on the rim of a plate or the edge of a sword.

Which sect performs the fire dance in Rajasthan?

The Jasnathi sect, in the Bikaner and Churu region. Performers walk and dance barefoot on burning embers to the beat of drums.

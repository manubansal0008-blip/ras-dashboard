Frequently asked questions

What was the RAS Prelims 2024 cut-off for General category?

71.59 marks out of 200 for General (male) and 69.80 for General (female) in the Non-TSP area.

What was the RAS Prelims 2023 cut-off?

100.69 out of 200 for General, OBC and EWS (male), and 97.01 for female candidates in the Non-TSP area.

What was the RAS Mains 2024 cut-off?

208.50 out of 800 for General (male) and 205.00 for General women in the Non-TSP area.

Why does the RAS cut-off change every year?

It depends on the difficulty of the paper, the number of vacancies and candidates, and reservation rules. The 2024 Prelims paper was tougher, so the cut-off fell to 71.59 from 100.69 in 2023.

Where can I download the official RAS cut-off?

On rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in, under Results — the 'Result Preamble and Cut Off Marks' PDF released with each Prelims and Mains result.

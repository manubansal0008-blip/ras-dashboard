RAS Vacancy 2026

The RPSC RAS 2026 notification announced 607 vacancies in the Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination — 192 in State Services and 415 in Subordinate Services. Here is the RAS 2026 vacancy breakdown and the services covered.

RAS 2026 total vacancy

RPSC notified 607 posts for RAS 2026 — a combined recruitment for both State (gazetted) and Subordinate services filled through the same Prelims, Mains and Interview process.

  • Total: 607 posts
  • State Services: 192 posts (gazetted)
  • Subordinate Services: 415 posts

Services covered under RAS 2026

The RAS combined examination recruits to several state services. The most sought-after is the Rajasthan Administrative Service (RAS) itself, alongside police, accounts and other services.

  • Rajasthan Administrative Service (RAS)
  • Rajasthan Police Service (RPS)
  • Rajasthan Accounts Service
  • Rajasthan State Insurance Service
  • Rajasthan Co-operative Service
  • Rajasthan Tehsildar Service, and other subordinate services

What the vacancy count means for you

  • Candidates admitted to the Mains are typically 15 times the number of vacancies.
  • State Services posts (like RAS, RPS) are the most competitive within the 607.
  • Post preference is exercised later — a strong Mains + Interview score improves your service allotment.
  • Vacancy numbers can be revised by RPSC; always confirm on the official RPSC website.

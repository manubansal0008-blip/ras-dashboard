How to download the official RAS question papers

  • Go to the official RPSC website: rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  • Open the Previous Question Papers section.
  • Choose the RAS / RTS Combined Competitive (Preliminary) Examination and the year, then download the PDF and the final answer key.

How to use previous year papers

  • Solve at least the last five papers (2016–2024) in exam conditions: 150 questions, 3 hours, 1/3 negative marking.
  • Mark every question you got wrong by theme — this shows your weak areas more honestly than any test series.
  • Rajasthan history, art & culture and Rajasthan geography are asked every year; learn the recurring facts thoroughly.
  • Remember the 2026 syllabus changes: some old topics are no longer named, and new ones (like the Rajasthan Public Examination Act, 2022) will not appear in old papers.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download RAS Prelims previous year papers?

Official papers and final answer keys are on rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in under Previous Question Papers. On RRRSHALA you can practise the 2016–2024 papers online for free, with answers in English and Hindi.

How many RAS Prelims papers should I solve?

At least the last five papers — 2016, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2024 — in exam conditions, then revise the themes you got wrong.

Are RAS previous year questions repeated?

Exact questions rarely repeat, but themes and facts — especially Rajasthan history, culture and geography — are asked again and again, which is why PYQ practice is so useful.

When was RAS Pre 2024 held?

The RAS Prelims 2024 exam was held in February 2025.
